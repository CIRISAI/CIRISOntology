## The lake's prediction (what makes this testable rather than decorative)

maintenance is PAID (`rent_holds`), building NEEDS CONTACT (the valve: never from nothing,
never downward, upward strictly; the pump is asymmetry). So:
- NEUTRALITY (0) is not a midpoint — zero payment already destroys, for free.
- HATE (−) is not free either — running order down FASTER than free decay is itself work

- The negative pole is the DEFAULT direction of dynamics; the positive pole is the paid one.

## The linguistic fossil prediction (the measurable face)

If language is maintained pattern (the stance's founding wager), its valence structure
should fossilize the ledger's lopsidedness in a SPECIFIC SPLIT, and the literature already
reports both halves separately:
- POSITIVE = FREQUENT BUT COARSE: Pollyanna hypothesis (Boucher & Osgood 1969); Dodds et
  al. 2015 (PNAS), universal positivity frequency bias across 10 languages; positive
  antonyms unmarked (Horn) — the paid, maintained default is spoken constantly and needs
  few words.
- NEGATIVE = RARE BUT DIFFERENTIATED: more distinct negative-emotion lemmas than positive
  across languages; negatives morphologically marked (un-, dis-); each failure mode named.
  The Anna Karenina principle as lexicography: one maintained state, many failure modes.
