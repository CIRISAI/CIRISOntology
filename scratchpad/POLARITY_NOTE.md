## The gap, stated plainly

The eleven kinds are currently unsigned LABELS. But every change in every corpus we have
built carries a direction — must->should vs should->must are the same kind with opposite
polarity; a reversed ordering could have been reversed the other way. Across 800+ authored
and wild items we have recorded before/after and analysed ONLY "which kind", never "which
way". That is an analysis we never ran, not a limitation of the taxonomy.

**The kinds are AXES. Changes along them are SIGNED. We have never used the sign.**

## Why it matters for the projective question

A signed taxonomy has LOOPS: move along an axis, move back, ask what returned. The holonomy
campaign already measured that the answer is not the identity — size returns, direction does
not. That is a holonomy, and a holonomy is a PHASE. A phase is exactly what selects a
projective representation, which is the structure the Altland-Zirnbauer signs need and which
I claimed (wrongly, N18_BRIDGE_NOTE.md, same day) we could not have. It reopens on two
measured objects rather than an assertion.

Still not claimed: that the taxonomy's symmetry group acts on the holonomy's transport
space. That is the open question, and it is now well posed.

## The cheap test, on data already in hand

Re-score the existing corpora with POLARITY (direction of the change along its kind's axis),
which requires no new items and no new panel — the before/after pairs are already there.
Then ask, pre-registered:
1. Is the confusion matrix polarity-symmetric? (Does Rules-tightening confuse the same way
   as Rules-loosening?)
2. Do the twin pairs behave the same under sign, given that they already behave DIFFERENTLY
   without it (Structure->Manner 7 vs Circumstances->Manner 1, TWO_WAY_READING.md)?
3. Is there any kind whose polarity is not well defined — which would be a finding about
   that kind, not about the method.
A polarity-asymmetric confusion matrix would be the first evidence that the sign is
structural rather than bookkeeping.
