## §0 Scope, and the two fences

### §0.1 The re-grounding hypothesis under test

> "Not speech acts: forms of recognition of state and transition potential offered to
> conscious beings in this universe." — the steward, 2026-08-20

The eleven kinds' speech-act derivation (`Core/Generator.lean`) is proposed to be the
**communicated special case**, not the ground. Credited ancestor: **Millikan's pushmi-pullyu
representations (1995)**, which derives direction-of-fit from a primitive that is both

(1991)** update-vs-revision — the world changed vs my information was wrong — which is
exactly transition-recognition vs state-recognition, named and formalized in the
belief-revision literature.

### T1.3 THE RECIPE — frozen, and applied identically to Generator2 and to both placebos

Given a mode set `M` (a set of recognition/force modes, each with stated satisfaction
conditions):

- **R1 — content sites.** One per mode: what that mode is *about*. Count `|M|`.
- **R2 — gap sites.** Per mode, the independent places where the mode's **satisfaction can
  come apart from its performance**. Each must be argued from the mode's own satisfaction
  conditions and written out **before** the total is computed.
- **R3 — carrier sites.** The mode-neutral vehicle layer: encoding, presentation, instance
  token. Count 3, independent of `M`.
- **R4 — the kind map.** Each site maps to the kind whose published discriminator question
  (`WrongKind.discriminator`) it answers.

Total = `|M| + Σ gaps + 3`.

For Generator1: `3 + (3+2+0) + 3 = 11`.
For Generator2, the R2 derivations are **required to be written from recognition's own
satisfaction structure**, not transported from Searle, and written into the Lean file header

## §T2 — THE DIT++ / ISO 24617-2 MAPPING: the convergence leg

**Cost ≤ $0.05 (may be $0 if run by agent mappers). Runs second.**

### T2.1 The exact target list, extracted before any band was written

From Bunt, *Revisiting the ISO standard for dialogue act annotation* (2017),
[aclanthology.org/W17-7404](https://aclanthology.org/W17-7404.pdf), §2, verbatim
dimension names:

**The nine ISO 24617-2 dimensions:**

1. **Task** — dialogue acts that move the task or activity forward which motivates the dialogue
2. **Auto-Feedback** — providing/eliciting information about the *current speaker's* processing of previous utterances
3. **Allo-Feedback** — the same, about the *current addressee's* processing
4. **Turn Management** — obtaining, keeping, releasing, or assigning the right to speak
5. **Time Management** — managing the use of time in the interaction
6. **Discourse Structuring** — topic management, opening/closing (sub-)dialogues, otherwise structuring the dialogue
7. **Own Communication Management** — the speaker editing their own current contribution
8. **Partner Communication Management** — the speaker editing another current speaker's contribution
9. **Social Obligations Management** — greeting, self-introduction, apologizing, thanking

**Plus two extras, mapped and reported separately (not counted in the nine):**

10. **Contact Management** — DIT++'s tenth dimension, establishing and maintaining contact
    and attention; excluded from ISO because it is not ubiquitous across dialogue situations
11. **Task Management** — DAMSL's dimension, *discussing or explaining* a task as opposed to
    moving it forward; proposed for re-inclusion by Bunt 2017 §3.4

**The three qualifiers:** certainty (un/certainty), conditionality, sentiment.

**ISO's other structural devices, mapped as candidate Record-analogues (§T2.5):** functional
dependence relations, feedback dependence relations, rhetorical relations. ISO also defines
56 communicative functions split into general-purpose and dimension-specific; these are
**not** in scope — the mapping is at the dimension/qualifier level only, and that scope
restriction is registered here rather than chosen later.

### T2.2 The type mismatch, confronted before mapping

ISO dimensions classify **categories of semantic content of a dialogue act's function**;
our kinds classify **changes to an artifact**. These are different types, and pretending
otherwise would manufacture agreement. The mapping question is therefore fixed in this
operational form, identical for every item:

> *"A change to an artifact that alters what this dimension covers is a change of which
> kind?"*

That makes both sides changes, which is the only type-matched comparison available.

### T2.3 The instrument

Three **independent** mappers — the standing three model families
(`plane_annotate.py`'s `MODELS`, temperature 0, stateless calls, `~/.deepinfra_key`), or
three unrelated agents — each given: the ISO definition verbatim (as quoted in §T2.1), our
public vocabulary (`WrongKind.plain` + `WrongKind.discriminator` as shipped in
`plane_annotate.py`'s `PLAIN`/`DISC`), the two boundary notes, and **nothing else**.

**Mappers must be blind to §T2.4's predictions.** Prompt contains no prediction, no
hypothesis, no mention of Record's specialness, and no mention of this document.

**DETERMINATE** := all three mappers assign the same one of our twelve kinds (or all three
say NO-FIT). Anything else is **indeterminate** and is reported with the split.

Let `D` = the number of the **nine ISO dimensions** that map determinately onto one of our
**eleven artifact-local** kinds.

## §T3 — THE MODEL SUBSTRUCTURE PROBE: the panel leg

**Cost cap $0.15. Runs with T4.**

### T3.1 The question

Active inference (Friston et al.) factorizes what our single `appliedRule` site holds into
**two**: **A** — the likelihood, how hidden states generate observations; **B** — the
transition dynamics, how states evolve. `REGROUNDING_PRIOR_ART.md` records this as a probe
candidate: if our **Model** kind conflates likelihood-model and transition-model, there is
substructure inside one of the eleven.

### T3.2 Design — three arms, and the third is the point

Authored per the standing corpus recipe (`plane_corpus/_helper.py` + the single-span
mechanical self-check used by `conj_author.py`). Domains balanced across the five standing
domains (prose policy, structured config, code, factual report, process description).

| arm | N | what changes |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | 30 | **only** an A-type applied rule — the mapping from evidence/observation to conclusion (a measurement model, a sensor calibration, a scoring rubric that maps evidence to a verdict) |
| **B** | 30 | **only** a B-type applied rule — the dynamics by which a state evolves (a growth/decay rate, a state-machine transition function, a scheduling recurrence) |
| **D** | 15 + 15 | **the discriminability positive control**: 15 items whose change is at the `appliedRule` site and 15 whose change is at the `factContent` site, same domains, same recipe |

Total **90 items × BASE × 3 model families = 270 judgments**.

In both A and B the change must be a rule **USED**, not a rule **MENTIONED** — the
use/mention line is what makes it the Model site at all. Any item where the rule is
asserted as descriptively true of the world is a Facts item by our own boundary note and is
dropped at self-check.

### T3.3 The ban-set, declared (per the **"a PASS names its rule-set"** gate)

Scanned set = the standing `_VOCAB` + `_VALENCE` of `_helper.py`, **extended for this leg**
with the phrases: `likelihood model`, `transition model`, `generative model`, `observation
model`, `active inference`, `hidden state`, `dynamics model`. Domain vocabulary that
happens to contain "transition" or "likelihood" is **not** banned; the ban is on phrases
that name the hypothesis. Our "clean" is a claim about **this** set and will be reported as
such.

### T3.4 Statistics

Panel: BASE condition, 3 model families, **modal by plurality, ties excluded** (standing
convention). Items with no modal are dropped and the drop count reported.

- **RESOLUTION UNIT** `R` := the separation the positive control achieves, measured as the
  difference in Model-modal rate between D's 15 appliedRule items and D's 15 factContent
  items. (Reported alongside the full total-variation distance between their modal
  distributions.)
- **Primary statistic:** `Δ` := |Model-modal rate in arm A − Model-modal rate in arm B|,
  with a permutation test over the A/B arm labels (10,000 draws), plus the distributional
  companion `TVD(A, B)` over the twelve modal labels with the same permutation null.
- **Directional secondary, staked:** if substructure exists, **B-items leak toward Process
  and Rules; A-items leak toward Facts and Premises.**

**Registered power statement (power-or-silence, done numerically).** At N = 30 per arm the
two-proportion design resolves a Model-rate difference of **≈35 percentage points at 80%
power against p < 0.01**, and ≈30 pp at ~65% power. **Differences below 35 pp that fail
significance read UNDERPOWERED, not "no substructure."**

### T3.5 Bands, frozen

**Evaluated in this order, mechanically — the control stage opens only arm D's judgments
and writes its verdict to disk before arm A or B is opened.** (The `--stage void` pattern
of `analyse_gauge.py`, which worked.)

| band | condition | reading |
|---|---|---|
| **VOID / UNDERPOWERED (gate 1)** | the control fails to separate at `p < 0.01`, or `R < 0.30` | the instrument cannot resolve a *known* kind boundary on this corpus, so **no verdict on A vs B in either direction.** |
| **VOID (gate 2)** | <70% of A+B items carry any modal, or >5% parse-failure rate | authoring or instrument failure. No verdict. |
| **SUBSTRUCTURE** | control passes **AND** `p < 0.01` **AND** `Δ ≥ 0.5 R` | **Model has substructure at panel grain**; the taxonomy may be 12+1 at finer grain. Escalated as a **live challenge under the standing bounty**, reported at full volume. `basePlane_card = 11` is a theorem-given-model and is untouched; what is challenged is the model's adequacy at finer grain. |
| **ONE KIND** | control passes **AND** `p ≥ 0.05` **AND** the upper 95% bootstrap bound on `Δ` is `< 0.5 R` | Model is **one kind** at our grain; active inference's A/B split is an implementation detail below our resolution. |
| **UNDERPOWERED** | control passes, `p ≥ 0.05`, but the upper bound on `Δ` is **not** below `0.5 R` | the honest middle: we could not exclude an effect half the size of a known kind boundary. **No verdict.** This is the band that stops a null from being read as a finding. |
| **AMBIGUOUS** | `0.01 ≤ p < 0.05` | reported with the direction it leant; no verdict |

**Adverse secondary (K4b):** if `Δ` is significant but the leak destinations are
*incoherent* — no semantic pattern, or the reverse of the staked direction (A-items toward
Process, B-items toward Facts) — the difference is reported as **adverse but
uninterpretable**, and substructure is **not** claimed from it. Same structure as the gauge
test's K-G2.

### T3.6 T3 kills

- **K4** — SUBSTRUCTURE → the taxonomy's grain is challenged at the bounty's own terms.
  **Page-relevant, reported at full volume.**
- **K4b** — incoherent-direction significance → reported adverse; no substructure claim.

## §T4 — THE MANNER BOUNDARY TEST: the falsifiable rewrite

**Cost cap $0.20. Runs with T3.**

### T4.1 The pin being tested

`COSMOLOGY_PINNING.md`'s follow-up struck the matcher's fusion caveat and gave the Manner
pin a **falsifiable rewrite**: Manner-content is **HOLISTIC** — empty for the whole,
carried at a **boundary / subsystem cut**. This leg is the taxonomy-side test of that
prediction, and **only** the taxonomy-side one (§0.2).

**Restated so a panel can decide it:** a register change is content-free when the whole
artifact is read, because presentation is uniform and the content is carried elsewhere in
the document. **Cut the artifact** — extract the changed passage so it stands alone — and
the register becomes content-bearing, because at the cut it is the only thing carrying what
the whole document carried elsewhere.

### T4.2 Design — 2×2 plus a manipulation check

| arm | N | items |
|---|---|---|
| **M** | 24 | the changed element is a **register/presentation** change (the Manner site), content held identical |
| **C** | 24 | **matched control** — the changed element is a content change (Facts or Rules site), content **local to the passage** |
| **P** | 12 | **positive control / manipulation check** — the changed element is a Premises- or Model-site change whose **grounding lies OUTSIDE the excerpt**, so the cut genuinely removes information the judgment needs |

Conditions:

- **WHOLE** — the full document, before/after, change located. The standing prompt.
- **CUT-A** (primary) — **only** the passage containing the change, framed as a standalone
  artifact ("this passage is extracted and published on its own").
- **CUT-B** (secondary, arm M only, exploratory) — the document is divided between two
  parties and the change straddles the division; the panel judges it as it appears to one
  side. Closer to the physics-side picture, harder to author, **no verdict and no kill may
  be read from it.**

Judgments: `(24+24+12) × 2 conditions × 3 models = 360`, plus CUT-B `24 × 3 = 72`. **432
total.** Stateless calls, so the in-context-fixation control is satisfied automatically and
the paired across-condition analysis is legitimate.

### T4.3 The two confounds, controlled in the design not in the discussion

1. **Length.** CUT shows less text; less text moves labels for reasons that have nothing to
   do with holism. Arm **C** absorbs this: it receives the identical cut operation.
   **Authoring constraint:** the M-arm and C-arm cut ratios (CUT tokens ÷ WHOLE tokens) must
   match within **20%**, measured and reported per arm. If they do not, the interaction is
   confounded and **must be reported as confounded**, not adjusted.
2. **The context-null trap.** Per the registered gate **"a context-null needs a manipulation
   check"** — a null on a context manipulation cannot be distinguished from "the condition
   was never processed" except by asking. Arm **P** is that check, and it is in the frozen
   design, not added afterwards.

### T4.4 Ban-set, declared

Standing `_VOCAB` + `_VALENCE`, **extended** with: `register`, `tone`, `style`,
`presentation`, `phrasing`, `wording`, `excerpt`, `boundary`, `holistic`, `standalone` —
banned from artifact text **and** from the `variation_site` description, because each is a
direct cue to the manipulation.

### T4.5 Statistics

Per item `i` in arm `X`: `shift_i = 1` iff `modal(CUT-A, i) ≠ modal(WHOLE, i)`. Items with
no modal in either condition dropped; drops reported per arm.

- **Gate 1 — VOID (run first, mechanically, on arms P and C only, arm M's judgments
  unopened):** `shift(P) − shift(C)` must be significantly above zero, one-sided
  `p < 0.05`. If it is not, **the cut was never read**, and **no verdict on M in either
  direction.** The verdict stage refuses to run without the gate's file on disk.
- **Primary:** `shift(M) − shift(C)`, two-proportion one-sided plus a permutation test over
  arm labels (10,000 draws).
- **Direction requirement:** among moved M-items, the destination is a **content** label
  (Facts, Rules, Identity, Confidence, Premises) in **≥ 2/3** of cases.

**Registered power statement.** At N = 24 vs 24 the design resolves a shift difference of
**≈30 percentage points at 80% power against p < 0.01**. Smaller true effects read
UNDERPOWERED.

### T4.6 Bands, frozen

| band | condition | reading |
|---|---|---|
| **VOID** | gate 1 fails | the manipulation was not processed. No verdict. |
| **HOLISM CONFIRMED** | `p < 0.01` **AND** `shift(M) − shift(C) ≥ 0.5 × (shift(P) − shift(C))` **AND** the direction requirement holds | Manner-content **is** holistic on this instrument: invisible whole, present at the cut. The pin's falsifiable rewrite gains its **first empirical leg**. Still wager-class; still says nothing about physics (§0.2). |
| **REFUTED** | gate 1 passes **AND** `p ≥ 0.05` **AND** the upper 95% bound on `shift(M) − shift(C)` is below `0.5 × (shift(P) − shift(C))` | the rewrite is **refuted on this instrument**; the Manner pin reverts to *support-with-mechanism-unmeasured*, and it loses its only staked empirical leg. **Note-level, not a page kill.** |
| **UNDERPOWERED** | gate 1 passes, `p ≥ 0.05`, upper bound not below the half-control threshold | could not exclude a real effect. **No verdict.** |
| **AMBIGUOUS** | magnitude passes but the direction requirement fails, or `0.01 ≤ p < 0.05` | reported with the failing condition named |

**Adverse secondary (K5b):** if moved M-items land predominantly on **carrier** labels
(Structure, Circumstances) rather than content labels, the movement is **vehicle-recoding**,
not content-emergence — the prediction's *mechanism* is wrong even where its *magnitude* is
right. Reported adverse even if the primary passes.

**CUT-B secondary:** report `shift(M)` under CUT-B and its destination distribution. If
CUT-B's shift exceeds CUT-A's by ≥10 pp, that is recorded as *the sharper instrument for a
successor design* — and nothing else.

### T4.7 T4 kills

- **K5** — REFUTED → the Manner boundary pin's falsifiable rewrite dies. **Note-level**, per
  "a shot-down guess is not a kill". The pin was never a page claim.
- **K5b** — carrier destinations → mechanism adverse; reported at the same volume as a pass.

## §T5 — THE TYPOLOGY TEST: the lost-words leg

**Cost cap $0.15. Runs after T1.**

### T5.0 Why this leg belongs, and why it is the dangerous one

The steward's insight: languages at the dawn of writing — and living languages far from
English — grammaticalized distinctions English lost or never had, and **each is a
millennia-tested candidate site.** Obligatory morphology is a distinction a speech community
found load-bearing enough to build into *every sentence*, sustained across generations. That
is the deepest wild corpus available to the ecological challenge, and it is deeper than
anything the programme has run at: our wild streams are days to decades old; these are
millennia.

**Stated at the top, because it is the reason to run it: T5 is the leg most likely to break
the eleven.** It has one prong aimed straight at a conflation written into our own
`Core/Generator.lean` header, and two more aimed at axes we have never had to carry. If the
eleven survive this, they have survived the hardest available attack. If they do not, the
data that breaks them is better than the data that built them.

### T5.1 The attack on our own header, named first

`Core/Generator.lean`'s grounding line for `strengthMarker` → Confidence reads, verbatim:

> *graded strength markers on assertions (hedging/evidentiality — a standard linguistic
> category, CoNLL-2010 family) → Confidence*

**Our enumeration folds evidentiality into strength-marking, by name, in the file header.**
Typology does not: Aikhenvald's survey treats evidentiality (the *source* — how you know)
and epistemic modality (the *strength* — how sure you are) as distinct systems that
co-occur, and Searle–Vanderveken hold that degree-of-strength is *one* component of a force,
not the whole apparatus around it. If source-of-knowing is a distinct modulator site, the
eleven are twelve, and the missing site has been marked obligatorily in Tucanoan, Quechuan
and Turkic for as long as those families have existed.

That is an internally-generated challenge to a published `proved` claim, exactly the shape
that produced the conjugation test — and this time the challenge comes with a wild corpus
attached.

### T5.2 Sample frame, frozen

**Primary frame: WALS Online** (Dryer & Haspelmath 2013, `wals.info`), which supplies
genealogically-stratified language samples with coded values. Chapter numbers below were
**verified against wals.info directly, not quoted from a secondary source**, per the
"received numbers are not measured numbers" gate.

**Secondary frame:** for categories WALS does not chapter, the named typological survey
literature. These carry a **weaker independence guarantee** — the language set is the
survey author's, not a stratified sample — and that difference is disclosed per category
rather than averaged away.

**Family independence:** the three languages sampled per category must belong to three
distinct **top-level families** (Glottolog/WALS family level, not genus). Pre-flagged: one
category is expected to fail this gate (§T5.3, #10).

### T5.4 Item form — minimal pairs, and the gloss problem solved by a control

Each (category × language) instance becomes **one minimal pair**: two versions of a short
artifact differing **only** in the obligatory marking, rendered into English. `13 × 3 = 39`
minimal pairs.

**The central confound, and it is severe.** English cannot mark these distinctions, so any
English rendering is a paraphrase — and the paraphrase imports English words that cue our
kinds directly (render a hearsay evidential as *"it is said that…"* and you have handed the
mapper a Record cue in the surface string). **Unaddressed, this leg would measure English
paraphrases, not grammar.**

**The control: two gloss conventions, every item run under both.**

- **GLOSS-N (natural):** the distinction rendered as idiomatic English paraphrase.
- **GLOSS-T (tagged):** the artifact text held **byte-identical** between before and after
  except for a bracketed category tag drawn from a fixed, kind-neutral tag vocabulary
  (e.g. `[EVID:direct]` → `[EVID:reported]`), with the category's meaning supplied in a
  fixed preamble.

Neither convention is trusted alone. **A mapping that differs between them is GLOSS-BORNE,
not grammar-borne** — an anomaly-triage coordinate sweep in the sense of the registered
gate, with the gloss convention as the coordinate. Only mappings **stable across both**
count as determinate.

**Attestation requirement (numeric, VOID-bearing):** every minimal pair must be traceable to
a **named published example or a WALS datapoint**. Invented examples void the item. Items
are logged with their source citation and the citation is verified against the primary
source before the item enters the corpus.

### T5.5 Instrument and statistics

The T2 mapping instrument, unchanged: three independent mappers (the standing three model
families, temperature 0, stateless), given the item, the twelve plain names and
discriminators, the two boundary notes, and a fixed one-paragraph description of what the
category obligatorily marks in that language — **and nothing else**. Mappers blind to every
prediction in this document. Each mapper returns kind, optional second, and a **one-sentence
reason**; the reason field is load-bearing for §T5.8 and is frozen as part of the output
schema now.

`13 categories × 3 languages × 2 gloss conventions × 3 model families = 234 judgments`, plus
up to `54` for stage-2 recurrence. **≈288 judgments, ≈$0.035 against a $0.15 cap.**

- **DETERMINATE** := unanimous across the three mappers **and** stable across both gloss
  conventions, at the category level (≥2 of the 3 language instances agreeing).
- `F` := determinate categories mapping **into** the 11+1.
- `R` := categories reading **determinate NO-FIT**.
- **Informative stratum** (secondary): the count landing on a **non-content** site — anything
  other than Facts, Rules, Identity. Staked: **≥ 6 of 13**. A result concentrated on the
  three content kinds tells us grammar marks content, which we already knew.

**Stage 2 — recurrence.** Any category with `R ≥ 1` or an indeterminate reading is re-sampled
in **three further unrelated top-level families** and re-mapped. Recurrence is the
clustered-residual criterion applied to the deepest corpus: a distinction that resists our
sites *once* is noise; one that resists in six unrelated families is a site.

**Registered power statement (power-or-silence, on a proportion).** With **0 determinate
NO-FITs out of 13 categories**, the 95% upper bound on the true rate of unmappable
grammaticalized categories is **≈20%** — so a clean sweep bounds the missing-site rate
loosely and **may not be reported as "no site is missing."** The 39 language-instances are
**not** 39 independent trials for the site question (instances within a category share the
category), per the "enumerated count is not effective count" lesson; the effective
denominator is 13. **This leg is powered to DETECT a recurring unmappable distinction and is
NOT powered to establish that none exists.** Any write-up that inverts that is void.

Confidence/Model/Premises, that is **indeterminate**, not distributed, and it is reported as
indeterminate — the difference is decided by the per-item unanimity rule, not by
interpretation afterwards.

### T5.7 The aspect prong — an internal disagreement, registered rather than resolved

The brief stakes aspect as **the KM state/transition split appearing in morphology** (credit
Katsuno–Mendelzon's logical twin; Comrie 1976 for the category).

**My dissent, recorded before sampling:** I expect the panel to read a perfective↔imperfective
flip as a **Facts** change, because both versions assert something about the world and the
flip changes what is asserted about the event's boundedness. The KM connection may hold at
the level of *what the category is about* while failing at the level of *what a change to it
is* — and this registration tests the second, not the first.

**Both readings are registered as named alternatives. Whichever the blind mappers give,
stands.** Neither of us gets to adjudicate afterwards.

### T5.8 The *who* / *how* line — a defence registered in advance so it cannot be produced afterwards

`Core/WrongKind.lean`'s `warrant_invisible_to_kind` **proves** that attribution — who made
the change, on whose authority — is invisible to kind, and the PLANE study **measured** the
human shadow of that theorem (labels did not move under the W2/W3 attribution swap).

So: a determinate NO-FIT whose mappers' **reason fields are about WHO** (speaker identity,
authority, whose knowledge it is) is **expected and NOT adverse** — it is the warrant
coordinate, which we already carry and which is *provably not a kind*. Adjudication is on
the frozen reason field, criterion stated here before any mapper runs.

**This defence is deliberately narrow, and the narrowness is the point.** It covers the
*who* only. A NO-FIT whose reason is about the **HOW — the route by which something came to
be known** (seen vs inferred vs heard) — is **not** the warrant axis and **is** a candidate
site. **Evidentiality is about the route, so §T5.8 does not cover the evidentiality prong at
all**, and may not be reached for if E2 fires. Egophoricity (#8) is the category this
defence is registered for.

## §7 Open doubts — stated here, not discovered later

1. **T1 may be a rename.** If Generator2's gap counts are transported from Searle rather
   than re-derived, the theorem is empty and the leg is void by its own rule (T1-VOID-2).
   The non-triviality lives entirely in whether R2 *independently* yields 3/2/0 from
   recognition's own satisfaction structure. **F1 (transition-strength → 12) is the reason
   to think it might not**, and I would put real probability on it.
2. **The placebo is authored by an interested party.** Mitigated three ways (§T1.6), not
   eliminated. An independent third placebo would strengthen this leg more than anything
   else in the registration.
3. **MIXED is my predicted placebo outcome**, which means my predicted overall T1 verdict is
   *the re-grounding is consistent, not established*. Written down now so it cannot be
   produced afterwards as if it had been expected.
4. **T2's type mismatch is real** and §T2.2's operational form is a repair, not a
   dissolution. A determinate mapping under that form is weaker evidence than a determinate
   mapping between same-type taxonomies would be, and must be reported as such.
5. **T3 and T4 are both model-panel-only.** The human ceiling is still owed across the whole
   programme; no verdict here is a claim about human classifiers.
6. **T3's ONE KIND band is tight at N = 30.** A small observed `Δ` will often land in
   UNDERPOWERED rather than ONE KIND. That is the correct behaviour and it means the leg is
   better at finding substructure than at excluding it — asymmetric power, disclosed.
7. **T4's CUT operation is not a subsystem cut in any strong sense.** CUT-A shortens the
   text and reframes it; CUT-B is nearer the intended structure and is the arm we can say
   least about. If HOLISM CONFIRMED fires on CUT-A only, the honest reading is *extraction
   moves Manner labels toward content*, which is weaker than the pin's claim.
8. **Nothing here tests the re-grounding's second licensed test** — recovering the kinds
   from non-linguistic recognizers (RL agents' abstraction structure, perception-action
   taxonomies). That design is still owed and is deliberately out of scope: it needs an
   instrument that does not exist.
9. **The T2 mapping and the T3/T4/T5 authoring all risk the warrant failure class** — the
   number right, the stated reason wrong. Per the proposed **warrant reach** gate, the only
   defence is procedural: a second reader goes to the primary artifact (the ISO text, the
   WALS datapoint, the cited example, the authored item) before any number enters a table.
10. **T5's gloss confound is the leg's central weakness, not a footnote.** English cannot
    mark these distinctions, so every item is a paraphrase or a tag, and both are our
    artefacts. The two-convention control detects gloss-borne mappings; it does **not**
    prove that a stable mapping is grammar-borne rather than an artefact common to both
    renderings. A third convention authored by someone who did not write the first two would
    strengthen this more than more items would.
11. **T5's 13 categories are a small denominator for a completeness question.** The power
    statement (§T5.5) is honest about this — 0/13 bounds the missing-site rate at ≈20%, not
    at zero — but the asymmetry is worth naming twice: this leg can find a site, and cannot
    certify their absence.
12. **T5's non-WALS categories carry a weaker independence guarantee** than the WALS ones,
    because the language set is a survey author's rather than a stratified sample. Four of
    the five most dangerous prongs (egophoricity, mirativity, middle voice, switch-reference)
    are in that weaker class. Disclosed per category, never averaged.
13. **Obviation is pre-flagged to fail the family gate.** It is left in the frozen list
    rather than quietly dropped, so that the drop is visible as a *prediction that came
    true* rather than as a sample chosen after the fact.
14. **Two legs now aim at the same published claim from different sides** — T3 (Model
    substructure) and T5 (a source/route modulator). If both fire, they are **not two
    independent confirmations of "the eleven are wrong"**: they are two different candidate
    twelfth sites, and the shared-lemma lesson applies — grade as scope-corroboration, not
    as independent replication, and say which site each names.
