# PRE-REGISTRATION — the re-grounding: are the eleven kinds forms of RECOGNITION?

**Frozen 2026-08-20, before any leg is built, any panel is run, any source is consulted.**
Nothing in this document may be revised in the light of a result; changes go in numbered
amendments written *before* the next leg runs (§17). Nothing here touches the published page.

Capstone registration of the re-grounding season. It stakes five legs, their methods, the
meaning of **every** outcome each can return, and a separable kill for each — per
`epistemology.md` L1 and §2.

## 0. The hypothesis under registration

The steward's re-grounding (`scratchpad/REGROUNDING_NOTE.md`, 2026-08-20):

> "Not speech acts: forms of recognition of state and transition potential offered to
> conscious beings in this universe."

Stated as a testable proposition:

**H-REGROUND.** The eleven artifact-local kinds are not at root a speech-act taxonomy. They
are the distinct forms in which a being can recognize **state** and **transition potential**.
`Core/Generator.lean`'s speech-act site model is then one **projection** of the recognition
structure into language — which is why its image is the eleven, and why the taxonomy holds on
maps, traces, code and registries, which are not speech (`ECOLOGICAL_RESULTS.md`: 279 wild
changes, one modal no-fit, and that a one-glyph typography swap).

H-REGROUND is a **wager** today and stays one unless the legs below move it. This document
does not argue for it. It arranges for it to die.

**The standing of the evidence that motivated it.** The day's blind matchers, given our
measured profiles and all of physics to choose from, never once landed on a force or a
particle; they landed on the recognition interface every time (`COSMOLOGY_PINNING.md`). That
is a **residual**, and per L6 a residual is never support. It is why the question is being
asked, not a reason to believe the answer.

## 2. Prior art carried in, and the debts that are owed at the top

Per the programme's convergent-art discipline (hits, not strikes: corroboration plus free
learning; credit generously; credits go in the Lean header the day the object exists).

**THE ANCESTOR — Ruth Millikan, "Pushmi-Pullyu Representations" (1995).** Derives the
descriptive/directive direction-of-fit split from a **primitive representation that is both at
once**, with the lineage biological signals → affordances → animal signals → speech acts.
H-REGROUND's direction of derivation — speech acts as the *communicated special case* of
recognition — is Millikan's, thirty years earlier, without a taxonomy and without a count.
**Goes in `Core/Generator2.lean`'s header on the day that file exists.** Not a threat to the
re-grounding; the ancestor of it.

**GIFT AT THE JOINT — Katsuno & Mendelzon (1991), update vs revision.** "The world changed"
vs "my information was wrong" is exactly T1's primitive split (transition-recognition vs
state-recognition), named and formalized in belief revision. **Also goes in the header.**

**THE PRIORITY FACT — Bunt's DIT++ / ISO 24617-2.** Dialogue acts defined semantically as
**information-state-changing potential**, ~10 orthogonal dimensions plus qualifier axes
including **certainty**. A speech-act-derived taxonomy of kinds-of-state-change at count ~10+3
exists, is standardized, and **precedes us**. Consequence, absolute and permanent: **no
first-count claim may be made anywhere, ever** (§16). T2 is the confrontation this fact
obliges.

**Named neighbours, not tested here.** Gibson's affordances and Rietveld–Kiverstein's
landscape (and the field's *principled* refusal of a closed affordance taxonomy — our answer
must be that we classify **recorded recognitions**, i.e. changes to artifacts, not affordances
in the wild, and that answer is an argument, not a theorem); active inference's factorization
(the source of T3); STIT logic; labelled transition systems / Kripke frames; Searle's status
functions; Klein–Noy ontology versioning as the nearest external analogue to Record.

## 3. FROZEN ADJUDICATION RULE — what a candidate twelfth must satisfy

Frozen **now**, before T2 and T5 can produce one. Without this, "we found a twelfth" and "we
absorbed it" are both unfalsifiable, and the leg that finds one is worthless in either
direction.

A candidate kind `K` may be **named as a candidate twelfth** only if all six hold. Each is
recorded PASS/FAIL/UNTESTED separately; a candidate failing any one is **absorbed, named, and
the absorbing kind recorded**.

| # | criterion | test |
|---|---|---|
| **C1 — distinct answer** | `K` is a different answer to *"what different kind of wrong happens if I vary this?"* — not a different word for an existing answer | write `K`'s discriminator question in the style of `WrongKind.discriminator`; a blind panel must distinguish `K`-targeted items from the nearest existing kind's items above the chance-corrected floor |
| **C2 — rename survival** | strip `K`'s home-domain vocabulary and restate; what survives the rename is structure, what dies was the word | the steward's rename-one-side gate (GATES.md). Two independent restatements, both must survive |
| **C3 — not a modulator** | `K` classifies rather than modulates | the idempotency test, §4 |
| **C4 — not a composition** | `K` is not the conjunction of two existing kinds, nor a value on an existing axis | exhibit a `K`-change that no pair of existing kinds jointly labels without residue |
| **C5 — a site, or provably none** | `K` either has a generator site, or is provably not site-generated in the way Record is (`record_not_site_generated`) | mechanized in Lean, or the leg reports UNTESTED and `K` stays a candidate |
| **C6 — traffic, or a principled absence** | `K` appears in wild change-traffic, or there is a stated structural reason it cannot (as `Model`/`Premises` have) | re-score the existing 279+60 wild corpus for `K` as an available label |

**A candidate twelfth that passes all six is named in the record and the count is reported as
under challenge. It does not enter `Stance.lean` on the strength of one leg.** The Lean count
(`basePlane_card = 11`) moves only after a second, independent leg agrees — the same
two-witness bar the programme applies to everything else.

**And the symmetric rule, so the frame is not one-sided:** a candidate that FAILS and is
absorbed must have its absorbing kind named in the record, so that a later reader can see
which absorptions the taxonomy has been made to perform. Ten absorptions into `Manner` would
be a finding about `Manner`, not a run of clean wins.

## 4. FROZEN DISCRIMINATOR — modulator vs kind (the idempotency test)

`Core/Stack.lean` proves the assertive stack terminates because its top operation is
**idempotent**: a hedge of a hedge is a hedge (`modulate_idempotent`). That gives a sharp,
pre-registered, domain-independent test, and it is frozen here before either leg that needs it:

> **A candidate axis is a MODULATOR if stacking it collapses (idempotent), and a KIND if
> stacking it produces a distinct reading (non-idempotent).**

Worked, so the test cannot be bent at scoring time:

- *"probably probably p"* = *"probably p"* → **idempotent** → certainty is a modulator. This is
  the prediction for DIT++'s certainty qualifier (T2) and for grammatical mirativity (T5).
- *"if A, if B, then p"* ≠ *"if A, then p"* → **non-idempotent** → conditionality is not a
  modulator. Predicted to land on `Premises` (T2).
- *"reported that it was inferred that p"* ≠ *"it was inferred that p"* ≠ *"reported that p"* →
  **non-idempotent** → source-of-knowing is not a modulator by our own test (T5). Where it then
  lands is T5's whole question and is **not** settled by this test.

The test is applied to the **surveyed evidence**, not to intuition: for T5 the stacking facts
come from the typological source, not from the analyst's ear (GATES.md: *every mechanism needs
a new search*).

## 5. Shared instrument, shared controls, shared floors

**The panel.** `scratchpad/plane_annotate.py`, unchanged: three model families
(Llama-4-Scout / gpt-oss-120b / gemma-3-27b), temperature 0, stateless calls, modal label per
item, valence-neutral prompt ("what kind of change is this?", never "wrong"), NO-FIT
first-class, multi-label permitted, boundary notes verbatim. This is the same instrument that
produced the 5,994-judgment study and the 279-item wild adequacy measurement; **no leg below
may substitute a different instrument**, because a different instrument makes the comparison
to those baselines invalid.

**The blind-matcher panel** (T2, T5). Five independent agents, no repository access, given
**profiles only** — neutral-language feature bundles with all home-domain vocabulary stripped
on **both** sides. Matching is run in **both directions** and scored separately. Leave-one-out
ablation on any match that wins, per the pattern in `BLIND_CONVERGENCE.md`. A matcher's
per-characteristic **failure** list counts as much as its ranking (`COSMOLOGY_PINNING.md`).

**The foil control (the dye test for every blind match).** Four **chimera profiles**,
constructed by splicing the first half of one real profile onto the second half of another
(pinned seed, construction fixed in advance). A chimera has no true home; a live matcher
should return NO-HOME or SPLIT. This is a generatively-matched surrogate (L2) rather than an
unrelated-taxonomy decoy, which is why it is the default.

- Per matcher: homing ≥2 chimeras at modal ≥50% → that matcher is **excluded**.
- Per leg: ≥3 of 5 matchers excluded → the leg is **VOID / ungauged**, which is a first-class
  outcome reported as loudly as a detection (GATES.md axiology (1)).

**The placebo arm (the dye test for every framing intervention).** Where a leg works by adding
a framing paragraph (T4) or a sub-question (T3), an equal-length, equal-register framing
paragraph that states something *precise but irrelevant* is run on the same items. **The
deliverable is the construction-minus-placebo gap, never the construction's raw number.**
(Registry memory: every change-blind control cleared the label floor too; select on the gap.)

**Floors, frozen.**

| floor | value | source |
|---|---|---|
| VOID on panel reliability | mean pairwise κ < 0.6 on clear items at BASE | `PLANE_PREREG.md` §5, met at 0.687 |
| VOID on legibility | modal NO-FIT / off-taxonomy > 10% in any arm | `GROSS4_FORWARD_PREREG.md` |
| VOID on sample | any arm below its stated usable-n floor | per leg |
| significance | paired permutation over **items**, p < 0.01, respecting item pairing — no χ² on correlated readings | `PLANE_PREREG.md` §4 |
| a matched baseline is measured **in the same study** | historical numbers are context, never the comparison | GATES.md: received numbers are not measured numbers; matched-geometry control |

**Spend.** Global hard cap **$6.00**, per-leg caps in §11. Exceeding a cap aborts the leg and
files it UNGAUGED rather than shrinking the design.

**Seeds.** `20260820 + leg index` (T1 → 20260821 for its blind arm, T2 → 20260822, T3 →
20260823, T4 → 20260824, T5 → 20260825). Sampling and chimera-construction scripts are
committed with their seeds before any sampling runs.

## 6. T1 — GENERATOR2: the same-image theorem

**Free, decidable, runs first.** No API spend, no panel, no data. It is the leg that can be
settled by the machine, and settling it first means every later leg knows whether it is
testing a re-grounding that already failed at the theorem level.

### 6.1 What is built

`CIRISOntology/Core/Generator2.lean` — a second generator, grounded in
**recognition of state and transition potential**, not in speech-act theory, with its image
computed by `decide`/`rfl` and compared to `Generator.lean`'s.

Header credits, mandatory, per §2: **Millikan 1995** (pushmi-pullyu, descriptive/directive from
a primitive that is both) and **Katsuno–Mendelzon 1991** (update vs revision). Also named:
Gibson (and the field's refusal of a closed affordance taxonomy, with our recorded-recognitions
answer stated as the argument it is), active inference, labelled transition systems.

### 6.2 THE FROZEN AXIS SET — written before the Lean exists

Five primitives. **Each carries independent motivation from named prior art. No primitive is
introduced to hit a count**, and this list may not be extended, contracted or re-weighted after
the image is computed.

| primitive | what it recognizes | independent motivation (named, pre-fixed) |
|---|---|---|
| **R1 STATE** | what is the case | Katsuno–Mendelzon *revision* side; active inference hidden state `s`; the einselected pointer sector |
| **R2 TRANSITION POTENTIAL** | what can or must follow | Katsuno–Mendelzon *update* side; labelled transition systems / Kripke frames; Gibson's affordance; active inference `B` and policies |
| **R3 CONSTITUTION** | recognition that makes-so | Millikan's pushmi-pullyu double aspect; Searle's status function; the stipulative definition (CGPM defining constants) |
| **C CARRIER** | the vehicle a recognition is borne in — **not itself a recognition kind** | any recognition offered to another being must be borne by something; sociolinguistic register; serialization |
| **F FRAME** | what survives, so a past recognition can still be established — **not a site, a relation** | Katsuno–Mendelzon's corpus-indexed belief state; Klein–Noy ontology versioning |

### 6.3 THE FROZEN DEPTH RULE

> **A recognition has depths exactly where there is a GAP between the recognition and what
> would satisfy it.**

This rule is **not newly chosen**. It is `Core/Fit.lean`'s gap principle restated in
recognition vocabulary — the same rule that already yields 3/2/0/2 in `Surface.lean` and
`DIMENSION_TABLE.md`. Re-using it is the point: if the recognition grounding needed a *new*
depth rule, that would itself be hand-tuning and is logged as such (§6.5).

### 6.4 THE STAKED PREDICTION, and its honest discount

**Predicted image (staked forward, L6):** the eleven, same roster, with Record from F provably
not site-generated.

- R1's gap (the recognition may be wrong about the state) → 3 depths: how strongly the state is
  recognized (**Confidence**; active inference precision `γ`), under what mapping it is
  recognized from what is available (**Model**; likelihood `A`), from what standing given it
  proceeds (**Premises**; prior `D`). Surface: **Facts**.
- R2's gap (the potential may not be realized) → 2 orderings: over outcomes (**Priorities**;
  preferences `C`, von Wright's axiological/deontic split) and over steps (**Process**; policies
  `π`). Surface: **Rules**.
- R3: no gap — constitution is self-satisfying → **0 depths**. Surface: **Identity**.
- C: 2 vehicle depths — which format bears it (**Structure**) and which instance bears it
  (**Circumstances**). Surface: **Manner**.
- F: **Record**, a relation, no site.

**THE DISCOUNT, STATED BEFORE THE RUN.** The author of this registration has already read
`Generator.lean`. T1 therefore **cannot** be a blind test of "does a recognition grounding give
eleven" — the target was known when the axes were written. A hit is **weak** support and will be
reported as weak. The informative content of T1 is concentrated in three places, and only
these three may be quoted as evidence:

1. **the misses** — any site the frozen axes generate that the eleven do not contain, or any of
   the eleven the frozen axes fail to generate;
2. **the hand-tuning ledger** (§6.5);
3. **the blind-derivation arm** (§6.6), which is the only part of T1 with the discount removed.

### 6.5 THE HAND-TUNING LEDGER — T1's falsifiability, mechanized as bookkeeping

"Natural" is defined operationally, in advance:

> A generator is **natural** iff (a) every primitive carries motivation from the §6.2 table,
> fixed before construction; (b) the depth rule is §6.3's, unmodified; (c) no primitive, site or
> depth is introduced, split, merged or re-motivated after the image is first computed.

Every deviation from (a)–(c) during construction is a **tuning event**, logged with what was
changed and why, in the Lean file's header.

- **Tuning budget: ZERO.** Any tuning event downgrades T1's verdict to **NULL**, whatever the
  image then is. The image after tuning is still reported — it is just not evidence.

### 6.6 THE BLIND-DERIVATION ARM — the only undiscounted part

Three independent agents, **no repository access**, are given exactly: the five primitives of
§6.2 with their motivations, the depth rule of §6.3, and the instruction to enumerate the sites
the rule generates and name each in plain English. They are **not** told the count, the roster,
or that a target exists.

- 3/3 reproduce the eleven-site structure → the discount is substantially removed; report as
  T1's strongest evidence.
- 2/3 → partial; the disagreeing derivation's difference is the finding.
- 0–1/3 → the axes do not determine the image without knowing the answer; **T1 is NULL
  regardless of what the Lean computes.**

Agreement is scored on **structure** (which primitive carries how many depths, and what each
depth is *for*), not on wording. Scoring rubric fixed in advance: a depth matches if a neutral
reader would route the same example change to it.

### 6.7 THE DECOY CONTROL — T1's dye test

**Three decoy generators are built in the same pass and reported alongside**, each with a
deliberately altered axis set: (D1) four recognition primitives (state / transition /
constitution / **evaluation**); (D2) two primitives (state / transition, constitution folded
into state); (D3) the §6.2 primitives with the depth rule replaced by "one depth per
primitive".

**If every decoy also yields eleven, the construction cannot fail and T1 is VOID.** A
construction that returns the same number whatever you put in is an instrument reading its own
floor (L5). At least one decoy must produce a different image for T1's verdict to mean anything.

### 6.8 The declared rival construction (built, not argued about)

Millikan's derivation order is **differentiation from a double primitive**, not a product of
independent axes. Taken seriously, the double-fit corner (`Identity`) would be the **root**, not
one cell of `Fit.lean`'s square. Both are built:

- **G2a (primary):** product/axis form, comparable to `Generator.lean` site-for-site.
- **G2b (declared rival):** differentiation form — the pushmi-pullyu root splitting into
  descriptive and directive, with the undifferentiated root retained as constitution.

If G2a and G2b disagree on the image, **that disagreement is the finding** and both are
reported. Neither is allowed to be quietly dropped. (Same discipline as `Surface.lean`'s
`surfaceAlt`: build the rival, compute what it moves.)

|---|---|---|
| **SAME** | G2a's image = the eleven, Record not site-generated, tuning ledger empty, ≥1 decoy differs | the eleven are robust to re-grounding; the projection reading **survives** (weakly — see the discount). H-REGROUND stays a wager with one theorem-level leg under it |

## 7. T2 — the DIT++ confrontation

### 7.1 Why this leg exists and what it can never buy

DIT++ / ISO 24617-2 is the closest existing taxonomy and it **precedes us**. This leg cannot
establish priority, novelty or a first count, and no outcome of it may be reported as doing so
(§16). It exists to answer: **are these two taxonomies the same object seen twice, and if not,
exactly where do they differ?** Convergence is corroboration of the shape and free learning;
divergence is informative in both directions.

### 7.2 The source, and how it is obtained

**The dimension list must be transcribed from the primary source at run time.** The working
list below is **PROVISIONAL, from memory, and is not data** (GATES.md: received numbers are not
measured numbers; a mechanism asserted is a claim incurred). If the primary source disagrees
with it, the primary source wins and the discrepancy is recorded.

Provisional (to be verified): ISO 24617-2's nine dimensions — Task; Auto-Feedback;
Allo-Feedback; Turn Management; Time Management; Own Communication Management; Partner
Communication Management; Discourse Structuring; Social Obligations Management — with DIT++
adding Contact Management (ten), plus three qualifier axes: **certainty**, **conditionality**,
**sentiment**.

Default source of record: the ISO 24617-2 standard text where obtainable, else Bunt's DIT++
release documentation and the published DIT++ papers, whichever is obtained is **named in the
result** with its edition.

### 7.3 Method — blind mapping panel, by profile, both directions

1. **Profiles.** Write a neutral-language profile of each DIT++ dimension and qualifier
   (function, what it is a dimension *of*, what varying it changes, what it cannot do), with all
   DIT++ vocabulary stripped. Write the same for the eleven+1, with all taxonomy vocabulary
   stripped. **Both sides are stripped** — matching by name is not matching, and the
   rename-one-side gate is what this implements.
2. **Forward map φ**: DIT++ profile → {eleven+1} ∪ {NO-HOME}, five blind matchers.
3. **Backward map ψ**: our profile → {DIT++ dimensions} ∪ {NO-HOME}, five blind matchers, run
   as a separate task so ψ cannot be inferred from φ.
4. **Foils**: four chimeras per direction (§5).
5. **Leave-one-out ablation** on every match reaching modal ≥50%: remove one profile
   characteristic at a time, re-match, and record which characteristics the match depends on.
6. **Scoring, frozen:** modal ≥50% of matcher votes = a **home**; modal <50% with votes spread
   = **SPLIT** (not the same outcome as NO-HOME, and never collapsed into it); modal NO-HOME =
   **residue**.

Reported: the full bipartite vote matrix; THEIR-RESIDUE (dimensions with modal NO-HOME);
OUR-RESIDUE (kinds with modal NO-HOME under ψ); COLLISIONS (kinds receiving ≥2 modal maps);
SPLITS both directions.

## 8. T3 — MODEL A/B substructure

### 8.1 The probe

Active inference splits what we call `Model` into two factors: **A**, the mapping from states
to observations, and **B**, the mapping from states to next states. If our `Model` conflates
them, one of the eleven has substructure.

### 8.2 The n problem, named before it bites

`Model` and `Premises` **never once surfaced as a wild modal** across 279 wild changes
(`Surface.lean`'s closing note; `GROSS4_FORWARD_PREREG.md` P2). Sub-labelling "all Model-kind
items in the wild corpora" would therefore yield ~0 items. The design accounts for this
explicitly rather than discovering it at run time.

**Item sources, in priority order:**

- (a) PLANE authored corpus: items whose BASE modal label is `Model` (~20 by construction, 20
  per kind × 12);
- (b) the Model↔Facts boundary items from PLANE's ambiguous set and Part D;
- (c) **any-vote** (not merely modal) `Model` items from the 279 + 60 wild items and the Stack
  Exchange stream;
- (d) newly authored items only if (a)–(c) fall short, and if used they are flagged and scored
  separately, because they are ours and post-hypothesis.

**Usable-n floor: 40.** Below 40 the adequacy arm is **UNGAUGED**, not null (L5).

### 8.3 The three arms

**ADEQUACY ARM (the question).** Blind sub-labelling of the (a)–(c) items with three options,
worded with **no active-inference vocabulary** (no "likelihood", no "transition model", no
"generative model"):

1. *the change alters what the artifact treats as a **sign of** what;*
2. *the change alters what the artifact treats as **leading to** what;*
3. *neither separately — it moves both at once, or the distinction does not apply.*

Option order is randomized per item (response-order control). Run under three conditions —
BASE, FRAME-3, DESIGN-3 — to test coordinate-flatness without paying for all seven.

**DISCRIMINATION ARM (the dye test).** 24 authored items, 12 designed A-type and 12 designed
B-type, blind to the panel, targets withheld from scorers until after labelling. **The panel
must recover the designed split at ≥75% modal accuracy.** Below 75%, the adequacy arm's
"unsplittable" reading is **UNGAUGED, not a finding** — a control that cannot see planted dye
cannot certify an absence (GATES.md reach 13).

**PLACEBO ARM.** 12 items authored as `Model` changes that are **neither A nor B by
construction** — e.g. a change to the *notation* of a rule that leaves both what-signs-what and
what-leads-to-what identical. Expected: "unsplittable". If the placebo splits at the adequacy
arm's rate, the split is a response-set artifact and the deliverable is zero.

**Deliverable = adequacy split rate − placebo split rate**, never the raw rate.

### 8.4 The composition test — frozen, because it decides whether the count moves

If the split is real, one more question decides whether `Model` is one kind with substructure
or two kinds:

> Do an A-change and a B-change produce **the same kind of wrong** or **different kinds of
> wrong**? (`WrongKind`'s own generating question.)

Operationalized: 12 paired items, one A-change and one B-change to the same artifact, each with
its downstream consequence made visible. A blind panel judges *"do these two changes go wrong in
the same way?"*

- **Same kind of wrong** → `Model` has substructure and remains **one kind**. The count does not
  move. Recorded plainly: the eleven are not atoms, and that is not a defect.
- **Different kinds of wrong** → A and B are two answers to the generating question; the
  candidate is routed through **all six criteria of §3** before anything is named.

### 8.5 T3 outcomes, pinned

| outcome | condition | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **SPLIT** | adequacy−placebo gap p<0.01, unsplittable <50%, coordinate-flat, discrimination ≥75% | `Model` has **real substructure**. Recorded plainly. **Does not break the count** unless §8.4 returns "different kinds of wrong" **and** §3 passes |
| **SPLIT-AND-DIVIDES** | SPLIT plus §8.4 "different kinds" plus §3 all-pass | `Model` is two kinds; the count is **under challenge at 12** and needs a second independent witness before Lean moves |
| **UNSPLITTABLE** | unsplittable ≥50% modal, discrimination arm ≥75% | active inference's A/B split is a **modelling convenience of that framework, not a joint** in the kinds. A real, reportable negative |
| **MIXED** | split rate between, or coordinate-mobile | substructure is present but not clean; report the confusion map, promote nothing |
| **VOID** | n<40, or discrimination <75%, or placebo ≈ adequacy, or κ<0.6 | **ungauged**. "Unsplittable" may **not** be reported |

### 8.6 T3 SEPARABLE KILL

> **KILL-T3.** If the panel cannot recover a *designed* A/B split at ≥75% (discrimination arm),
> every T3 verdict is ungauged and no claim about `Model`'s atomicity — in either direction —
> may be made from this instrument.

Takes down: T3's verdicts only. Not `Model` as a kind, not the count, not any other leg.

## 9. T4 — the Manner boundary

### 9.1 The pin under test

Structure/Manner is one of the **three predicted-and-observed confusion concentrations**
(`PLANE_RESULTS.md` §4: Premises→Facts, Structure→Manner, Model↔Facts, all condition-independent
and replicated at every scale). The pin's falsifiable rewrite, inherited from the edge-modes
strike (`COSMOLOGY_PINNING.md`, PIN_FOLLOW-UP: the condition is a boundary/subsystem cut):

> **Manner = null-fit carrier variation UNDER A FIXED SUBSYSTEM CUT.**

The rewrite predicts a *mechanism*: the Structure/Manner confusion exists because the cut is
left implicit, so annotators cannot tell a re-cut from a re-dressing. Make the cut explicit and
the confusion should collapse.

### 9.2 Method — minimal pairs, crossed with framing

**20 minimal pairs (40 items).** Each pair shares a base artifact and content:

- **item (i) CUT-MOVE**: the subsystem boundary moves, carrier held — e.g. one config block
  split across two files, a section re-parented, a field promoted to a record, a clause moved
  between a schedule and the body;
- **item (ii) CARRIER-VARY**: the carrier varies, cut held — register, phrasing, formatting,
  presentation within the same unit.

**Three framings, crossed, between-annotator (no annotator sees an item twice):**

| arm | framing paragraph |
|---|---|
| **CUT-SILENT** | current prompt, no framing paragraph — **the matched baseline, measured in this study** |
| **CUT-EXPLICIT** | one paragraph, fixed wording, stating where the subsystem boundary is drawn and that it is held fixed |
| **PLACEBO** | one paragraph, same length and register, stating something precise but irrelevant — the retention frame, which PLANE measured does **not** move labels (0/40) |

Historical PLANE Structure→Manner rates are reported as **context only**; the comparison is
always CUT-SILENT measured on these same items (matched-geometry control).

### 9.3 The predictions, staked

- **P4.1** under CUT-EXPLICIT, CUT-MOVE items read **Structure** and CARRIER-VARY items read
  **Manner**, each at modal accuracy ≥75%;
- **P4.2** Structure↔Manner confusion under CUT-EXPLICIT falls **below** CUT-SILENT on the same
  items, paired permutation p<0.01;
- **P4.3** the fall is **not** reproduced by PLACEBO: (CUT-SILENT − CUT-EXPLICIT) −
  (CUT-SILENT − PLACEBO) > 0 at p<0.01. **This gap is the deliverable.**

### 9.4 T4 outcomes, pinned

| outcome | condition | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **PIN-CONFIRMED** | P4.1–P4.3 all hold | the pin's **mechanism** is right: the confusion is cut-implicitness, not a defect in the kinds. The Manner pin's falsifiable rewrite gains its first measured support, and the boundary is a *prompt* problem with a fix |
| **PIN-WRONG** | P4.3 fails (gap ≤0, or p≥0.05) | **KILL-T4 fires.** The pin's mechanism is wrong; the Structure/Manner confusion has some other cause. Recorded as loudly as a confirmation (L8) |
| **PLACEBO-EXPLAINED** | CUT-EXPLICIT falls but PLACEBO falls as much | *any* explicit framing paragraph helps; the effect is attention, not the cut. A distinct and separately reportable negative |
| **REVERSED** | confusion rises under CUT-EXPLICIT | making the cut explicit *creates* the ambiguity — which would be evidence the two kinds are not separated by the cut at all, and the sharpest possible refutation of the rewrite |
| **VOID** | CUT-SILENT confusion <15% on these items (floor effect, no room to fall), or κ<0.6, or parse failure >10% | ungauged; the minimal pairs failed to load the boundary and the instrument says nothing |

### 9.5 T4 SEPARABLE KILL

> **KILL-T4.** If explicit-cut minimal pairs do not reduce Structure/Manner confusion beyond the
> placebo, the Manner pin's **mechanism** is wrong.

Takes down: the mechanism, and only the mechanism. **Does not take down** `Manner` as a kind,
`Fit.lean`'s null corner, `carrier_inert_under_mention`, `Surface.lean`'s carrier-surface choice,
or the physics-side edge-modes reading (which is a note, not a claim, and rides on nothing here).

## 10. T5 — the typology leg (the lost-words leg)

### 10.1 The frame, and the honest warning

The steward's frame: languages of the deep past lexicalized and grammaticalized distinctions we
have lost, and **obligatory grammatical categories are millennia-tested candidate recognition
sites** — a category a language *forces* every speaker to mark on every clause has been under
selection longer than any taxonomy we could author.

**This is the leg most likely to break the eleven, and it is registered for exactly that
reason.** T1–T4 test the taxonomy against our own constructions, our own corpus, and one
neighbouring standard built by people who share our intellectual ancestry. T5 tests it against
distinctions that no one in this lineage chose, that were fixed by use rather than by design,
and whose positive outcome breaks a published count.

### 10.2 Primary probe — EVIDENTIALITY

**Source:** Aikhenvald, *Evidentiality* (OUP 2004), as the survey of record, **consulted, not
recalled**. The categories below are provisional and are **not data** until transcribed from the
source: direct/visual; non-visual sensory; inference; assumption; reportative/hearsay; quotative
— in languages where source-of-knowing marking is **obligatory**, which is the selection
criterion (an optional marker is a lexical choice, not a millennia-tested site).

**Method:** the §5 blind-matcher machinery, identical to T2 — neutral profiles both sides, all
linguistic and all taxonomy vocabulary stripped, five matchers, both directions, chimera foils,
leave-one-out ablation. Plus:

- **the idempotency test (§4)**, applied to **stacking facts drawn from the source**, not from
  intuition;
- **the rename gate**: restate "evidentiality" as *"obligatory marking of the source of a
  recognition"* with no linguistic vocabulary, and re-run the match. What survives the rename is
  structure; what dies was the word.

### 10.3 The staked question, and the third answer the brief did not name

The brief stakes two answers: evidentiality maps **INSIDE** the eleven (onto the
Confidence/Model boundary), or **SOURCE** is a distinct modulator site and therefore a
principled candidate twelfth.

**A third answer is staked here, and it is the one this registration considers most likely.**
`Core/WrongKind.lean` already carries **WARRANT** as a *second axis*: "on whose say-so", proved
**invisible to the kind function** (`warrant_invisible_to_kind`), orthogonal on the content
classes, and the repository's own registered warrant reach (GATES.md) appearing as a structural
feature of classification. Source-of-knowing is, on its face, that axis.

- **P5.1 (primary, staked):** grammatical evidentiality maps onto the **WARRANT axis** — already
  present, already proved not to be a kind. Predicted modal home under φ: **NO-HOME among the
  eleven+1, with the matchers' free-text reasons naming source/authority/attribution.**
- **P5.2:** source-of-knowing is **non-idempotent** under stacking (reported-inference ≠
  inference ≠ reported), hence **not a modulator** by §4 — which is what makes P5.1 non-trivial:
  warrant would then be a *non-modulator, non-kind orthogonal coordinate*, which is exactly what
  `warrant_invisible_to_kind` says it is.
- **P5.3:** **mirativity** (surprise / newly-integrated knowledge) is a **transition in
  Confidence**, not a kind — a marked value on an existing axis, failing §3's C4.
- **P5.4:** the **certainty/strength** dimension of evidential systems is **idempotent** →
  modulator → Confidence, agreeing with T2's P2.3 from an unrelated source.

### 10.4 T5 outcomes, pinned

| outcome | condition | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **INSIDE** | evidential categories home at modal ≥50% onto the eleven+1, concentrated on the Confidence/Model boundary | a millennia-tested obligatory category fits inside the taxonomy; the strongest available adequacy evidence, from outside the programme entirely |
| **WARRANT** | modal NO-HOME with matcher reasons naming source/authority, **and** P5.2 holds | evidentiality is the **warrant coordinate we already have and already proved is not a kind**. **No twelfth.** A striking convergence: an obligatory grammatical category lands exactly on the axis we proved orthogonal — and one that would have looked like a twelfth to anyone without the theorem |
| **TWELFTH** | modal NO-HOME, **and** all six of §3 pass, **and** it is not the warrant axis | **SOURCE is a principled candidate twelfth. It is named as such and NOT absorbed.** The count is reported under challenge and the second-witness rule (§3) applies before Lean moves |
| **MODULATOR** | homes on Confidence **and** is idempotent under stacking | the modulator layer is real and bigger than one axis; `Stack.lean`'s modulation rung gains company; the count is untouched |
| **SHATTER** | evidential categories distribute across ≥5 kinds with no modal above 50% (SPLIT dominant) | the eleven do not carve source-of-knowing at all; the taxonomy is **language-specific in a way we did not know**, and H-REGROUND — which claims the kinds are pre-linguistic recognition forms — is in serious trouble |
| **VOID** | foils homed per §5, or the source cannot be obtained, or fewer than 3 usable matchers | ungauged. **No conclusion about the count in either direction** |

### 10.5 The lexical companion, and a boundary drawn deliberately

`scratchpad/WORD_TESTS.md` is the **lexical** companion to this **grammatical** leg: T5 tests
what languages force every speaker to mark; the word-test registry tests individual ancient
words against reality.

- **LOGOS** — tested, **survived**. Formalized as the whole-only share, proved to exist
  (`third_sees_parity`), measured across five streams, unified as one theorem with three
  witnesses (`NonFactoring`). The standing demonstration that the method works on a
  twenty-five-century word.
- **AGAPE** — staked at **wager-class** (design-knowing, value-creating, unconditioned
  maintenance), with three gates unrun. **It is AXIOLOGICAL and is OUT OF SCOPE for this
  registration.** The boundary is drawn deliberately and stated here so the referee can see it
  was drawn rather than overlooked: this registration is about the *structure of recognition*,
  and importing a value-term would mix a taxonomy question with an axiology question in a single
  frozen design — which is precisely the tangling that §2 of `epistemology.md` forbids. Agape
  gets its own registration or none.

### 10.6 The named queue, scope declared now

Obligatory categories **named but not run** in this registration, so a later reader can see the
scope was chosen rather than exhausted: egophoricity / conjunct-disjunct; engagement and
intersubjectivity (Evans, Bergqvist — arguably the sharpest untested one, and the first
candidate for the successor leg); grammatical aspect; deixis and person; honorifics and
register (a `Manner` candidate); noun class and classifiers; associated motion.

### 10.7 T5 SEPARABLE KILL

> **KILL-T5.** If evidential categories distribute across five or more of the eleven with no
> modal home (SHATTER), the claim that the eleven are **pre-linguistic recognition forms** is
> falsified for the source-of-knowing dimension: a distinction that obligatory grammar treats as
> one thing, our taxonomy shreds.

Takes down: H-REGROUND's pre-linguistic reading. **Does not take down** the eleven as a
taxonomy of artifact change (the wild adequacy measurement is a separate object with a separate
basis), the count, T1's theorem, or any `Stance.lean` claim.

## 16. What this registration does NOT claim

Stated plainly, because these are exactly the claims a reader might otherwise infer.

1. **No first-count claim. Ever, anywhere, on any outcome.** DIT++ / ISO 24617-2 is a
   speech-act-derived taxonomy of kinds-of-state-change at count ~10 plus qualifier axes, and it
   **precedes us**. Convergence with it is corroboration of a shape, never priority. This
   sentence survives every possible result of T2.
2. **No physics claim rides on any leg.** The cosmology pinning programme
   (`COSMOLOGY_PINNING.md`) is out of scope: no outcome here may move a pin, and no pin may be
   cited as evidence for any outcome here. The pins are wager-class and stay there.
3. **H-REGROUND is not asserted.** This registration arranges for it to die. A SAME image in T1
   is weak support with a stated discount (§6.4), not a demonstration.
4. **No claim that the eleven are natural kinds**, that they are complete, or that recognition
   is a natural kind. T1 is theorem-given-model in exactly `Generator.lean`'s sense and inherits
   its frame entirely.
5. **No claim about consciousness.** "Offered to conscious beings" is the steward's phrasing of
   the hypothesis, and nothing here tests, assumes, or requires any theory of consciousness. If
   H-REGROUND survives every leg, it will have survived as a claim about *recognition structure*,
   with the consciousness vocabulary carrying no load.
6. **No human ceiling.** Every panel leg is a **model panel**. The human ceiling is owed and
   stays owed; the standing bounty stays unclaimed. No leg's outcome may be reported as a fact
   about human classifiers.
7. **No claim that a taxonomy of affordances is closed.** The field is principled about the
   opposite, and our answer — that we classify **recorded recognitions**, i.e. changes to
   artifacts, not affordances in the wild — **is an argument, not a theorem**, and is inherited
   as such.
8. **Nothing here touches the published page.** No `Stance.lean` edit, no claim status change, no
   count change follows automatically from any outcome. A count change requires two independent
   witnesses (§3) and a steward decision.
9. **The provisional lists in §7.2 and §10.2 are not data.** They are from memory and are
   flagged as such; the primary sources decide, and the discrepancy is recorded when they differ.
10. **Agape and the axiological word-tests are deliberately excluded** (§10.5). The boundary was
    drawn, not overlooked.

## 17. Amendments, the record, and the human commitments

**Amendments** are numbered (A1, A2, …), dated, and must state what they change and **that they
were written before the next leg ran**. The precedent is the polarity prereg's AMENDMENT A1
(written before reading any result). An amendment written after a result is not an amendment; it
is an analysis deviation and is labelled as one in the results document (`PLANE_RESULTS.md` §3
is the model for how that is reported).

**What CI can check here: nothing.** Every commitment in this document is human-upheld. Per
`epistemology.md` §4, a timestamp proves a file existed, not that nobody had peeked; the machine
cannot check that a null is generatively appropriate, that a stated domain is honest, or that a
kill is a real kill rather than one written to be unreachable. The only mechanical products of
this registration are `Generator2.lean`'s theorems and their axiom audit.

**Results go in `scratchpad/RECOGNITION_RESULTS.md`**, one section per leg, with:

- the staked-vs-measured table first, before any narrative;
- every VOID and every UNGAUGED arm reported as loudly as any detection;
- every fired kill reported first and loudest, with the dead reading kept and marked dead (L8);
- the hand-tuning ledger and the decoy images for T1, in full;
- the full bipartite vote matrices for T2 and T5, including foil performance per matcher;
- construction-minus-placebo gaps for T3 and T4, never the raw rates;
- every deviation from this document named as a deviation, in its own subsection.
