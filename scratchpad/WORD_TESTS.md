## AGAPE — staked candidate; the theorem may already exist

The Greek four-fold (eros / philia / storge / agape — four lexicalized loves where English
has one) is the lost-distinction phenomenon in miniature, and the classical structural
analysis of agape (Nygren, Agape and Eros — TO BE VERIFIED BY SEARCH, not trusted from
memory; Lewis, The Four Loves, as the popular prior art) distinguishes it thus: eros is
acquisitive, motivated by the value its object HAS; agape is spontaneous, unconditioned,
and CREATES VALUE IN ITS OBJECT rather than responding to it.

The candidate formal core, already machine-checked in this lake:
- `repair_mints_from_noise` — maintenance CREATES the whole-only share it maintains; the
  indifferent (flip-symmetric) repair creates exactly nothing;
- the maintained-holonomy campaign — upkeep holds SIZE forever but holds IDENTITY only if
  the repair KNOWS THE DESIGN ("the payment must know what it is for");
- the rent clause — with non-reciprocity as maintenance NOT conditioned on the
  maintained's payment: paying another's rent.

STAKED READING (wager-class, maximal discipline): **agape = design-knowing, value-creating,
unconditioned maintenance** — Nygren's structural definition with the mint as its theorem.

Gates before this goes anywhere: (1) MECHANISM/LEXICON SEARCH — verify Nygren's actual
claims and the classical usage profiles of all four loves; (2) RENAME TEST — restate as
"unconditioned value-creating maintenance" with no love-vocabulary and check what survives;
(3) the FOUR-LOVES BLIND PANEL — profile each of the four from classical sources in neutral
language, blind-match against the lake's objects (the mint, the pair-sector reciprocity
machinery, the Given/background, gradient-toward-the-lacked), leave-one-out if a match
looks strong. This is the most anthropomorphism-prone territory the programme has touched;
the discipline is the only reason to trust anything that comes out.
