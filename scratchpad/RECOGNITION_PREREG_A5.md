# AMENDMENT A5 — T2's third instrument: change-vignettes (2026-08-20, steward-authorized, written BEFORE any vignette existed)

Steward authorization on the record ("authorized") after T2's two-run VOID. This is a
DESIGN change, not a wording tweak: the mapping moves from definition-level to
vignette-level — concrete artifact-changes whose whole effect instantiates each ISO item.

## The instrument

1. VIGNETTE AUTHORING, out of the orchestrator's hands: for each of the 17 frozen items, a
   clean-channel model (DeepSeek-V3.1, temp 0.7, no project context) writes TWO concrete
   scenarios from the item's verbatim definition alone, under the frozen template below.
   The orchestrator may REJECT a vignette only for format violation (it names one of our
   kinds, references a standard or taxonomy, or fails to describe a change to an artifact)
   — never reword one. Rejections are logged and regenerated. Vignettes are committed
   VERBATIM before any mapping call.
2. TEMPLATE (frozen): "Write a concrete scenario of 2-4 sentences: a specific document,
   transcript, or record is changed, and the whole effect of the change is an instance of
   the following: <ITEM DEFINITION>. Describe only the artifact, the change, and its
   effect, in plain everyday language. Do not name any classification scheme, standard,
   dimension, or category. End with the sentence: 'The change described above is the whole
   difference between the two versions.'"
3. MAPPING: the same three shipped families, temp 0, same vocabulary/boundary notes, the
   frozen schema (kind · second · reason · SURVIVES-OUTSIDE verbatim), question: "The
   scenario below describes a change to an artifact. That change is a change of which
   kind?" 34 vignettes × 3 families = 102 calls.
4. DETERMINACY (frozen): an item is DETERMINATE iff BOTH its vignettes are individually
   3/3 on the SAME kind. Both-3/3-but-different = VIGNETTE-SENSITIVE, counts to nothing,
   reported by name. All A2 MAJOR-3 count rules otherwise unchanged.
5. κ floor 0.4 over the 34 vignette-classifications; D, bands, predictions P2.1-P2.8, the
   RECORD-ANALOGUE trigger (3/3 SURVIVES-OUTSIDE=yes on BOTH vignettes), and kills stand
   exactly as frozen. Disclosure: the orchestrator knows the predictions; the vignette
   channel does not, and the format-only rejection rule is the guard.
6. If this instrument also VOIDs, T2 closes VOID for the registration, period.
