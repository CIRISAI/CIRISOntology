exists.** The pre-registration is not edited.

**Occasion.** Planck-pilot supplied the scannable form of the warrant reach — **"a true statement

## H1. THE TEST, AND WHY THIS RUN IS DIFFERENT FROM THE THIRTEEN

Amendment 6 F1 ran a **theorem-citation** grep: every theorem name, checked against its signature
at source. It found one error and passed everything else.

This run is a **different test on the same documents**: one grep for unconditional quantifiers —
`always`, `never`, `every`, `any`, `all`, `cannot`, `identically`, `whatsoever`, `in general` —
then each hit checked against its own boundary case.

> **It fired three times on documents the theorem grep had already cleared.** That is the first
> evidence that the scannable form catches a **class the citation audit does not**, and it is
> prospective rather than retrospective — the form was written first and applied second.

None of the three changes a verdict. Two change a stated reason, and one of those is the warrant
for a design decision.

## H2. W4 — the sharpest differentiation argument in the campaign, false at its own reference point

`WATER_PRIOR_ART.md` §1.1, point 2 — the argument that our object differs from Chau–Hardwick /
Errington–Debenedetti `q`:

> *"`q` is normalised so that it reads **0 for the ideal gas** and 1 for a perfect tetrahedron. So
> a **pair-potential liquid with no three-body physics whatsoever has `q ≠ 0`** — hard spheres
> have angular structure."*

**The ideal gas is a pair-potential liquid with no three-body physics, and it has `q = 0` — by
the normalisation named in the immediately preceding sentence.** The universal is false at
exactly the boundary the same paragraph sets up.

**Bounded form, superseding it:** *an **interacting** pair-potential liquid at liquid density has
`q ≠ 0`.* The differentiation argument **survives intact** — the case that matters is the
interacting one, and there our share subtracts the pair contribution by construction where `q`
does not — but the universal as written was refutable in one line by a reader who took it at face
value, in the section where this campaign is at its most assertive about priority.

## H3. W5 — a design rejection resting on an over-broad universal

`WATER_PREREG.md` §2.2, rejecting hydrogen-bond count as the slot label:

> *"every H-bond definition is a threshold on a **pair** of continua (distance and angle), so the
> design would import two arbitrary cuts instead of one."*

**Not every one.** Purely **energetic** H-bond criteria threshold a single continuum, and
electron-density / topological criteria are not distance-and-angle at all. So the stated reason —
*two cuts instead of one* — does not hold against the whole class.

**And this one costs something, which the other two do not.** If an energetic criterion is one
threshold on one continuum, it sits on **equal footing** with the coordination-number cutoff this
campaign chose, and the margin claimed for the choice disappears.

**The rejection stands; its warrant is replaced.** The defensible reason is not *fewer cuts*, it
is **where the cut sits**: coordination number's `r_cut = 3.50 Å` is placed at the **measured
first minimum of `g_OO`** — a feature of the data, swept over a pre-registered ladder
`{3.3 … 3.7}` — whereas no H-bond energy or geometry threshold sits at a measured feature of this
system's own pair structure. **That is a real difference and it is the one that should have been
given.** Recorded as a warrant replacement, not a design change: the primary label is unchanged.

## H4. W6 — the justification for expecting the physical null to read nonzero

`WATER_PREREG.md` §5.1:

> *"a pair-matched point process has genuine triplet structure (Kirkwood superposition is violated
> in **every real liquid**)."*

**Kirkwood superposition becomes exact in the dilute limit** — the triplet correlation factorises
into pairs as density → 0 — so the violation vanishes continuously and "every real liquid" has no
lower bound attached. **Bounded form:** *violated at liquid density, by an amount that vanishes in
the dilute limit.* The conclusion — N3 will read nonzero at water's density — is unaffected, and
the campaign already treats N3's reading as **measured rather than assumed**.

**A lower-severity fourth, recorded for completeness:** `WATER_PRIOR_ART.md` §1.1 point 3 says
`share ≥ 0` *"always, identically, **with no condition**."* There is a condition — `IsProb p` —
and in a campaign that has spent four amendments on exactly this species of over-claim, "no
condition" should read "no condition beyond `IsProb`".

## H5. WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE PROPOSED REACH — and what it does NOT

**For the form.** Applied prospectively to two documents that had passed a theorem-citation audit
hours earlier, the scannable form returned **three hits plus one minor**, of which **one carries
the warrant for a design decision**. The two audits are not redundant: the citation grep looks at
*named objects*, the scannable form looks at *quantifiers*, and W4/W5/W6 are invisible to the
first because none of them cites a theorem at all.

**Against over-claiming it.** Glass's constraint is right and this run does not meet it. Three
hits found by an author auditing their own prose is **not** a measured detection rate: I do not
know how many unconditional universals remain, the grep's term list was written by the same
person who wrote the prose, and there is no known-answer case anywhere in it. **This is evidence
the form is live, not evidence of its power**, and it is exactly the distinction `GATES.md`
already draws between *"the gate fired on one real incident"* and a dye test.

**The planted plumb line, proposed concretely rather than agreed to in principle.** What is
missing is an artifact where the answer is known. A minimal one that would work:

> Take a **committed, correct** results document. Produce a variant in which `n` claims — the
> number and locations recorded in a sealed key, never in the artifact — have their **warrants
> inverted while every number is left exactly right**: a theorem stripped of a hypothesis it
> needs, a law quoted outside its band, a universal with its bounding condition removed. Give a
> reader the procedure and not the key, and score **recall** (of `n`) and **false-positive rate**
> (flags on untouched claims).

Two properties make it a real dye test rather than a demonstration: **every number stays correct**,
so nothing numerical can betray a planted instance, and **the false-positive rate is measured**,
which is the quantity `GATES.md`'s design rule 3 says every gate owes and which **no gate in the
registry currently has**. This campaign has not built it and does not claim it; it is offered as
a specification to whoever takes the reach forward.

**On authorship, since it has been passed around.** Glass named it, pump supplied seven instances
and the structural-gap observation, planck-pilot supplied the scannable form, and this campaign
supplied three instances plus the first two procedural catches. It is a shared finding and this
document claims no more of it than that.
