## 1. WHY — a scope mismatch in the original design, corrected

The original prereg built a **spatial** substrate: `k` slots at one time, uniform on a
maximum-share support. But the finding that motivates the whole experiment
(`HABIT_DYNAMICS_RESULTS.md`, `6b97e15`) measured the **temporal** share: one channel
sampled at three times, `(y_t, y_{t+Δ}, y_{t+2Δ})`, decaying with lag. Those are not the
same measurement, and comparing them would not have been apples-to-apples. This addendum
adds the temporal arms.

**Scope is unchanged and still primary: this is a DESIGNED substrate, a control, not a
discovery about nature.** An LFSR is built to satisfy a three-time parity exactly. Finding
that it does is not a result about the world. What is transferable is the *price* of
holding the structure under noise, and — see §4, T0 — what the measurement can and cannot
see.

## 2. THE SUBSTRATE, AND WHAT WAS ESTABLISHED BEFORE THIS FILE WAS WRITTEN

A binary LFSR with a **trinomial** recurrence `y_t = y_{t−a} ⊕ y_{t−b}` locks a three-time
parity by construction. A length-`T` window of its output is a codeword of a linear code
`C ⊆ F_2^T` whose dual is generated by the weight-3 check words — so the temporal substrate
is exactly the *spatial* machinery of the main prereg with `S = C`, and the same estimator,
floors and cost definitions apply unchanged.

**Population convention (load-bearing).** Replicas' initial states are drawn uniformly from
**all `2^L` states, including the all-zero state**. Over a single m-sequence period the pair
counts carry the classic `1/(2^L−1)` imbalance; including the zero state makes the window
distribution *uniform on a linear code*, hence **exactly** pair-uniform. This is a design
choice made to remove an `O(2^{−L})` bias, and it is stated because it matters.

Computed by `lfsr_design_check.py` **before this file was written** — construction facts,
no dynamics, no noise:

- 14 maximal-length trinomial recurrences exist for `b ≤ 13`. **Chosen: `a = 4, b = 9`**,
  verified period `511 = 2^9 − 1`.
- The parity triple sits at **offsets `(b−a, b) = (5, 9)`**, not at `(a, b)`: substituting
  the recurrence gives `y_t ⊕ y_{t+5} ⊕ y_{t+9} = 0`. (Recorded because the brief said "tap
  lags", and the tap lags are not where the spike is.)
- **The comb is extremely sparse.** Scanning all 276 lag pairs `(i,j)` with
  `1 ≤ i < j ≤ 24`: exactly **2** carry any share — `(5,9)` and `(10,18)` — and both carry
  **exactly `ln 2`**, the machine-checked `k = 3` maximum, with pair deviation `0.0e+00`.
  That is 0.7 % of the grid.
- **Pairwise mutual information is exactly `0.000e+00` at every lag 1…40.**
- **The equally-spaced readout `(Δ, 2Δ)` — the one the sibling used — reads exactly
  `0.000000000` at every `Δ = 1…20`,** because neither `(5,9)` nor `(10,18)` has `j = 2i`.

The last two are the reason this addendum is worth running, and they generate T0 below.

**One dynamics step**, on a population of `M` replicas each holding a length-`T` record:

1. **Bit rot** — every bit of the record flipped independently with probability `ε`.
2. **Upkeep** — with probability `q` per replica, the record is decoded to the nearest
   codeword of `C` (exhaustive over all `2^L` codewords; ties broken uniformly at random).

Readout: the sibling's estimator on the three-time marginals, pooled over replicas and over
all positions in the record, with the **matched pairwise-maxent surrogate null and shuffle
floor**, excess = share − null, `z` reported. Tied fraction is exactly 0 (already binary).

## 3. THE ANALYTIC BACKBONE, derived before running

Bit rot is a bit-flip channel, diagonal in Fourier: after `t` steps the surviving weight-`w`
coefficient is `λ^{wt}`, `λ = 1 − 2ε`. The three-time marginal at a parity lag pair has only
`p̂(∅) = 1` and `p̂(111) = λ^{3t}` nonzero, so it stays **exactly pair-uniform at all times**
and

```
share_t  =  ln 2 − H_b( (1 + λ^{3t}) / 2 ),     H_b the binary entropy in nats.
```

For small share this is `≈ ½ λ^{6t}` — **geometric with ratio `λ^6`**, which is the `d = 3`
rate of the main prereg's §3.2 and is literally `unpaid` of `Core/Maintenance.lean` with
`γ = 1 − (1−2ε)^6`. With upkeep at rate `q` the stationary coefficient is
`g_3 = q / (1 − (1−q)λ^3)` and `share_∞ = ln2 − H_b((1 + g_3)/2)`.

Cost, as in the main prereg §3.5: `cost_erase = q·[H(p_pre) − H(dec#p_pre)]` nats per
replica per step, reported also in **bits per recorded bit per step**.

## 6. WHAT WILL NOT BE CLAIMED

1. **No claim about nature.** An LFSR was built to satisfy the parity; its doing so is not
   evidence about any natural system. `wild-share` is untouched.
2. **T1 and T2 are constructions, not discoveries**, and will be labelled so in the results
   wherever they appear.
3. **No world-claim from the rent clause and no refutation of it.**
4. **T0 is a statement about our instrument, not about the world** — it says a particular
   probe geometry can miss maximal structure. It does **not** say the sibling's chaotic
   lattice had hidden order-3; that substrate was measured on its own terms and its null
   stands on its own terms.
5. **No novelty claim.** m-sequences, their ideal two-level autocorrelation, and the
   pairwise independence of distinct-lag samples are textbook (Golomb, *Shift Register
   Sequences*). Nothing here is offered as new mathematics.
