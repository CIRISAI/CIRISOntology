**(e) a small fixed count of "kinds of change" derived from first principles and compared to physics' small counts: NOBODY, technically.** What exists is (i) pop-management "four forces of change" analogies riffing off the four fundamental forces, and (ii) one genuine cross-disciplinary formalism — Gabora & Aerts, *A Cross-disciplinary Framework for the Description of Contextually Mediated Change* (arXiv:0706.0156), the CAP framework — which is a continuous state-space/context-actualization formalism, **not** a taxonomy with a cardinality. ## Ranked: who is nearby

**1. Quantum Memory Matrix (Neukart, Marx, Vinokur — Terra Quantum / Leiden). CLOSEST, and it is a direct prior claim on two of your three pins.**
Claims: spacetime is a discrete lattice of Planck-scale *memory cells*; matter interacting with a cell imprints information there reversibly, so information is strictly conserved. Cells have finite capacity; **when cells saturate they can no longer record independent information and contribute a residual energy "of the same mathematical form as the cosmological constant"** — dark energy as accumulated, saturated record. Separately, the surviving entropy field acts as **an effective dust component deepening curvature** — their dark-matter and PBH mechanism (arXiv:2506.13816, "information wells").
Proximity to your object: it has the **ledger** and it has **records→dark energy** and **medium→dark matter** in one framework, with cosmological cycles. It has **no** taxonomy of change, no language layer, no speech acts, no frame-relation. So: same cosmological pins, entirely different upstream.
Mechanism or shape-matching: **real mechanism** — capacity bound, saturation, entropy field sourcing curvature via a stress tensor. Papers: arXiv:2504.00039, arXiv:2504.03817, arXiv:2506.13816; *Entropy* 26(12):1039; a cyclic-universe paper reported accepted at JCAP (2026-06). Caveat I could not discharge: single-group program, MDPI-heavy venue, affiliated with a company; I did not assess its quality.

**2. Michael Paul Gough — "dark information energy". The EARLIEST specific prior claim on records↔dark energy.**
Already credited in your stance, and this sweep confirms he is the right person to credit: Gough's *Information Equation of State* and *Holographic Dark Information Energy* (Entropy, 2008/2011) derive a dark-energy density from the Landauer energy of the information held by the universe's stars/structure. Mechanism: Landauer, explicitly. Note for your side: this is exactly the family whose Landauer normalisation your K4 kill already fired on at 3–5 dex — so QMM above, which routes around Landauer via capacity saturation rather than erasure cost, is the *live* version of this pin and the one worth engaging.

**3. Verlinde and successors — and note the SIGN IS INVERTED relative to your pins.**
Verlinde's emergent gravity assigns **dark energy = the medium** (the entropy-carrying de Sitter volume law) and **dark matter = the memory/elastic response** of that medium to displaced entropy ("the universe remembers"). Your pins read records→dark energy, medium→dark matter. Verlinde's read the other way round. That inversion is worth taking seriously as a live alternative assignment rather than a difference in wording. Successor state as of 2024–25: the sweep surfaced mainly critical work — entropic corrections found incompatible with solar-system and particle-physics constraints (arXiv:1909.01734 and follow-ups), plus non-local reformulations. Mechanism: yes, holographic/thermodynamic.

**4. Vopson — information as dark matter. A prior claim on medium↔dark matter, by a different mechanism.**
Mass-energy-information equivalence (AIP Advances 2019): a stored bit has finite mass; ~5.2×10⁹³ bits would account for the missing dark matter. Plus the "second law of infodynamics" (entropy of information systems constant-or-decreasing). Also relevant to your third pin: *Estimation of the information contained in the visible matter of the universe* (arXiv:2112.04473) — the nearest thing I found to a **visible-content↔luminous-matter** claim, but it is a bit-*count* of visible matter, not a role assignment. Mechanism: yes, but a contested one (bit-mass), and the program has drifted into simulation-hypothesis claims that have drawn heavy criticism. Treat as a claimed pin you must cite and distance from, not as support.

**5. Chris Fields / Glazebrook / Levin — "physics as information processing"; semantics FROM physics, with mechanism.**
The most serious extant program deriving *meaning* from physics: holographic screens as information boundaries between subsystems; quantum reference frames represented as hierarchies of binary classifiers **provide a semantics for measurement outcomes**, hence "a theory of meaning for interacting agents"; generic quantum systems as observers/agents. Mechanism: strong — separability, HP as a consequence of quantum information theory, principled self-representation limits (*Entropy* 26(3):194; arXiv:2210.16021). Proximity: this is the closest anyone gets to *semantics as a physical sector with a derivation*. But there is no taxonomy of change, no fixed count, no cosmological-component pins. If you want a neighbour whose *method* rhymes with yours — derive the semantic structure, don't assert it — this is it.

**6. Coecke et al. — categorical quantum mechanics / DisCoCat. The strongest existing LANGUAGE↔PHYSICS structural correspondence in the literature.**
Grammar (Lambek pregroups) and quantum processes (finite-dim vector spaces with the Abramsky–Coecke protocol semantics) are **the same object**: compact closed monoidal categories. Grammatical derivations become linear maps on tensor products of word meanings.
Proximity: it is a genuine, worked-out, non-metaphorical structural correspondence between language and physics — but it is a **shared-formalism** claim, not a claim that meaning occupies a physical sector, and it is silent on cosmology and on kinds of change. Mechanism: shared categorical structure, which is a real reason the correspondence holds, but it explains the *compositional* structure only.

**7. Constructor theory (Deutsch, Marletto) — the nearest thing to a Rules-sector, with a mechanism but no taxonomy.**
Physics restated as which transformations (tasks) are possible vs impossible and why; counterfactual properties admitted into fundamental physics; information itself defined counterfactually. Proximity: it resembles your Rules sector in *type* — laws as constraints on permissible change — and it is the only program treating the possible/impossible distinction as fundamental. But its "taxonomy of change" is a binary (possible/impossible) plus a task calculus, not a finite enumeration of kinds, and it makes no cosmological-component assignments and no contact with commitment or speech acts.

**8. Pattee — the physics of symbols, semantic closure, the epistemic cut.**
Symbol-matter complementarity as an irreducible primitive; rate-independent symbolic constraints on rate-dependent physical dynamics; semantic closure as the self-referential condition. Mechanism: yes, and a physical one (constraint, measurement, the cut). Proximity: it is *the* canonical prior art for "meaning is a physical category, not an add-on" — and its descendants (biosemiotics, Peircean; Rosen's modeling relation with encoding/decoding between natural and formal systems; Deacon's constraint-based account) are your intellectual neighbourhood. No fixed count, no cosmology.

**9. Wheeler it-from-bit / law-without-law → Smolin precedence.** Already in your stance and correctly credited. The sweep found the it-from-bit lineage described as having spawned three divergent programmes sharing vocabulary; nothing new that pins components.

## The single closest prior claim on your specific pins

**QMM is it.** In one framework it asserts both records/ledger↔dark energy (saturated Planck memory cells produce a residual energy of cosmological-constant form) and medium↔dark matter (the entropy field as effective dust). It is recent (2024–2026), technical, and has a mechanism rather than a shape-match. Gough (2008/2011) has temporal priority on records↔dark energy specifically, via Landauer. Verlinde holds the same two components with the **assignments swapped**. Nobody I found has a specific claim on visible-content↔luminous-matter as a *role*; Vopson's arXiv:2112.04473 is the nearest artifact and it is only a bit-count.

Practical consequence: the ledger-cosmology composition needs to cite QMM and Gough, and it needs a stated reason it is not Verlinde-with-the-signs-flipped. The taxonomy-of-change leg and the speech-act-to-physics bridge, by contrast, have no competitors at all — which cuts both ways: no one has taken the territory, and no one has independently found it worth taking.
