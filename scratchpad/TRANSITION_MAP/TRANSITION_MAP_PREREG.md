# TRANSITION MAP — the selection rules of the eleven, pre-registered
# FROZEN 2026-08-21, after legs A and B (derived blind of each other), BEFORE any alignment
# between them was computed and BEFORE any wild transition was counted.

## The object

The exhaustive map over ordered kind-transitions (i -> j), 121 cells plus the Record axis:
each cell to carry {ALLOWED / FORBIDDEN / CONDITIONAL(stated) / EMPTY}, a valence
{+ extend / 0 independent / − revert}, and the verdict's source (theorem / grammar /
measured), kept separate per source forever — a theorem cell and a measured cell may
disagree, and the disagreement is the finding, never averaged away.

## The three legs and their standing

- LEG A (LEG_A.md, sealed): theorem-backed cells — the operation channels (grounding,
  modulation, mention, absorption), the one-way Record axis (sealed both directions), the
  frame-supply obligation, force-budget co-availability. THEOREM-GIVEN-MODEL throughout;
  three cells + ten more are declared choice-dependent and carry that flag into the map.
- LEG B (LEG_B.md, sealed): the grammar's own transition structure — CEG 1.0-RC29's 1+4
  (scores; delegates_to; supersedes; withdraws; recants), the 1+1+3 typing backbone
  (prospective entity / prospective pair / retrospective-requiring-prior), spec-marked
  invalidities. In the spec's own vocabulary; no kind names imported.
- LEG C (this prereg): the wild transition matrix, measured.

## STAGE 1 — THE ALIGNMENT (A x B), design frozen before it runs

Three blind mappers (clean API channel, the standing families) receive: the five CEG
operations with their sourced pre/postconditions (LEG B verbatim), the twelve plain kind
names + discriminators + boundary notes, and the question per operation: "an act of this
operation, applied to content, is a change of which kind — and which kinds of change can
it lawfully FOLLOW, per its preconditions?" Output schema: kind for the act; list of kinds
it can follow; one-sentence reason. Counts rule as everywhere (3/3 determinate; 2/3 named,
counts to nothing). THE TOURNAMENT CHECK, staked: the steward's regular-tournament
conjecture holds iff the determinate alignment gives each kind EXACTLY five lawful outward
operation-channels with every ordered pair covered at most once. Kills (steward's own,
MAXIMAL_OBJECT.md): two operations on one hallway; an act landing NO-FIT off the plane;
an irregular orientation. Any one kills the tournament reading; none touches the map.

## STAGE 2 — LEG C: the wild matrix

INSTRUMENT: revision chains. Source: Stack Exchange post edit-histories (the gross-four
stream's source, panel-valid regime), N = 100 chains with >= 3 revisions, sampled by the
frozen recipe: the gross-four forward test's site and sampling rules, chains ordered by
the site's own chronology, no selection on content. Each successive revision-pair is one
change-item; the standing panel labels each (BASE, 3 families); a chain of n revisions
yields n-2 ordered transition events (modal_k -> modal_{k+1}).
SUPPLEMENTARY: the agent-repo governed-file history (64 commits; block-class attribution)
— reported beside, never pooled (different generative process).

STATISTICS: transition counts T[i][j] vs the order-permutation null (shuffle each chain's
change order, 10,000 draws, per-cell and matrix-level chi-square with the tied/sparse
cells handled by exact tests; the effective-count gate: transitions within one chain are
not independent — the effective N is the chain count, and every per-cell claim carries
it). VALENCE, mechanical: change k+1 REVERTS k if it intersects k's span and restores >=
half of k's removed text; EXTENDS if it intersects without restoring; INDEPENDENT
otherwise. Span logic identical to conj_author.py's diff machinery.

## STAKED PREDICTIONS (before any alignment or count)

- TM1: the alignment is CONSISTENT with every LEG A theorem cell (no determinate
  alignment verdict contradicts a theorem-backed FORBIDDEN).
- TM2: the wild matrix respects the Record axis's one-way theorem: transitions the frame
  seals are observed at 0 (any hot sealed cell -> a derivation dies or a mint is found;
  reported at full volume either way).
- TM3: the wild diagonal dominates: T[i][i] exceeds the null in aggregate (changes of a
  kind follow changes of the same kind — maintenance traffic).
- TM4: the off-diagonal mass concentrates in the three boundary channels + the
  surface-absorption direction (deep -> its surface), consistent with stages 0-1.
- TM5 (the steward's polarity face): REVERT (−) transitions are rarer than EXTEND (+)
  at matrix level — building outweighs unbuilding in maintained artifacts — but the
  REVERT share is HIGHER toward the surface four than among the deep seven.

## BANDS

VOID — agreement (panel kappa < 0.4 on the change-labeling); VOID — coverage (< 70%
modal); UNDERPOWERED per cell by the effective-N rule; each TM scored HELD/FAILED on its
own numbers; the map is published as three overlays (theorem/grammar/measured) regardless
of verdicts. Spend cap $0.20, human-upheld, preflight file.

## What this is not

No physics. No stance change. The map is a model-and-measurement object; adequacy of the
site model is untouched by any cell (LEG A's own caveat, carried forward).
