## System construction (the design decision this document freezes)
- CONTEXTS c = target kinds (the item's authored kind). RANDOM VARIABLES: for each
  response kind q, the dichotomous indicator R^c_q = 1 iff a judge answers q on an
  item of target c.
- The measured joints: three judges per item give within-context joint
  distributions of (R^c_q, R^c_q'); the same q appears across contexts —
  connections. The system is INCONSISTENTLY CONNECTED (marginals of R^c_q vary
  with c) — CbD's home case: contextuality is s_odd exceeding (n−2) + Δ, where Δ
  is the direct-influence allowance (Kujala–Dzhafarov 2015 criterion for cyclic
  systems; PRA 101:042119 graded measure = L1 distance to the noncontextual
  polytope).
- CYCLIC SUBSYSTEMS: rank-2 cycles assembled over kind-pairs along the measured
  top channels (e.g. c₁: q,q′ ; c₂: q′,q″ ; … closing the loop). The candidate
  cycle set is generated mechanically: all 3-cycles and 4-cycles among the top-6
  mass channels of the instrument under test. No hand-picking after seeing scores.
