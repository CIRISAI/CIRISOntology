You are repairing STAGE 2 of a change-classification pipeline. Be concrete and terse.

## Task
Given BEFORE/AFTER versions of a document and where the change is, pick exactly ONE of twelve kinds.
- Facts: What claimed fact becomes wrong?
- Rules: What becomes allowed or required?
- Manner: How is the same thing presented or used?
- Identity: What is this said to be?
- Priorities: What becomes more important?
- Confidence: How sure are we, and on what standard?
- Circumstances: What just happens to differ here?
- Process: What steps or ordering change?
- Model: What rule or model are we reasoning under?
- Structure: How are the pieces put together?
- Premises: What are we taking as given?
- Record: Can the event still be established from what survives?

## Where Stage 2 sits
Stage 1 has already produced a SURFACE reading (one of Facts/Rules/Manner/Identity) plus a rationale.
Stage 2 receives that verbatim and must either CONFIRM it or name the true kind. The governing idea
is CARRIES-INVERSION: a deep kind often ARRIVES DRESSED AS a surface kind, and Stage 2's job is to
un-dress it.
