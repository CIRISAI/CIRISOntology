Critique a proposed classifier-prompt revision. Be adversarial and specific. Do not be agreeable.

## Context
A 12-way change classifier. Current measured per-kind accuracy of the prompt being revised
(124 items, one model family): Circumstances 10/10, Model 10/10, Priorities 10/10, Process 10/10,
Manner 10/10, Identity 11/12, Record 9/10, Rules 9/10, Facts 7/10, Confidence 7/10,
Structure 5/10, **Premises 4/12**. Overall 0.823.

## The two weak kinds and what their items actually look like

PREMISES (4/12). The misses go to Model (3), Circumstances (4), Rules (1). The twelve items are all
the same shape: a DECLARED BASIS at the top of an artifact that everything below is read against —
the units system declared at the top of a config; the coordinate frame; an EPOCH constant; the
definition of the reference year; DAYS_IN_YEAR; "durations are counted in working days" -> "calendar
days"; "times are UTC" -> "local site time"; the batch a recipe is founded on; the stipulated basis of
counting ("what a single entry is taken to be"). In every case the downstream text is UNCHANGED and
its MEANING shifts.

STRUCTURE (5/10). The misses go to Circumstances (2), Manner (1), Facts (1), Premises (1). The items:
a clause moves from a parenthetical into the Scope line; a condition moves out of step 4 into a
precondition above the list; the order of two parameters in a function signature; inline role tags
replaced by a separate role-note line; check dates change from calendar dates to day-offsets.

## The proposed new tests (to be added to the prompt)
1. PREMISES — THE RIPPLE TEST: "If the changed element is a declared basis, convention, frame or
   starting point, and the tell is that every other statement in the artifact keeps its exact wording
   while what it MEANS changes, it is Premises. Contrast: Model is a framework applied to DERIVE
   values, and changing it changes the derived answers; Circumstances is an interchangeable instance
   whose change alters nothing else's meaning; Facts is a claim becoming wrong."
2. STRUCTURE — THE RELOCATION TEST: "If the same content is moved, re-nested, re-ordered, re-scoped
   or re-shaped within the artifact — so that what it attaches to, or how it must be addressed,
   changes — it is Structure, even when no wording changes. Contrast: Manner is a change in wording,
   tone or presentation with the same arrangement."

## Your job
1. Name every way test 1 will MISFIRE — items of other kinds it will wrongly capture. Be concrete:
   which of the eleven other kinds is now at risk, and by what mechanism? Rank by expected damage.
2. Same for test 2.
3. The two tests can collide: a declared basis that also relocates. State the tie-break, in one sentence.
4. Test 1's "every other statement keeps its wording while its meaning changes" — is that criterion
   actually CHECKABLE from a BEFORE/AFTER pair plus a one-line description of where the change is?
   If not, give a checkable substitute.
5. Propose the single highest-value ADDITIONAL sentence to add, and say which kind it rescues and
   which kind it endangers.
Answer in five numbered blocks. No preamble.
