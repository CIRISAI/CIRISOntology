You are helping repair a broken stage of a text-classification pipeline. Be concrete and terse.

## The task the pipeline performs
Given BEFORE and AFTER versions of a document plus a one-line description of where the change is, label the change with exactly ONE of twelve kinds:

SURFACE FOUR
- Facts: What claimed fact becomes wrong?
- Rules: What becomes allowed or required?
- Manner: How is the same thing presented or used?
- Identity: What is this said to be?

DEEP SEVEN (+ Record)
- Priorities: What becomes more important?
- Confidence: How sure are we, and on what standard?
- Circumstances: What just happens to differ here?
- Process: What steps or ordering change?
- Model: What rule or model are we reasoning under? (the framework APPLIED to derive an answer; a model ASSERTED to be true of the world is a Fact)
- Structure: How are the pieces put together?
- Premises: What are we taking as given?
- Record: Can the event still be established from what survives?

## The architecture
STAGE 1 (cheap, one call): decide whether the change is one of the SURFACE FOUR, or "DEEPER".
  If SURFACE and confidence >= 0.7 -> that is the final answer, pipeline exits.
  Otherwise -> STAGE 2, an expensive call that picks among all twelve using
  "carries-inversion" (deep kinds arrive DRESSED AS surface kinds).

## The measured failure (372 runs, 124 labelled items x 3 model families, temperature 0)
- "DEEPER" was returned ZERO times out of 361 parseable calls. Never once.
- Confidence distribution: 0.90 -> 145 calls, 0.95 -> 126, 1.00 -> 86. Mean 0.913. 97% are >= 0.7, so the gate is inert.
- Confidence as a signal for "the true label is one of the surface four": AUC 0.608 (near-useless).
- Consequence: 233 of 233 items whose TRUE label is a deep kind exited on the fast path and were therefore WRONG BY CONSTRUCTION (the fast path cannot emit a deep label). Deep-kind accuracy 0.025.
- Surface-target items on the fast path: 116/125 correct (0.93). Stage 1 is EXCELLENT at surface items.
- The 12 items that did reach Stage 2 (all via parse errors, not the gate) were 8/12 correct including Model 4/4, Record 1/1. Stage 2 WORKS.
- A plain single-pass 12-way classifier (no pipeline) scores 0.737 overall and 0.626 on deep kinds on the same items. The pipeline currently scores 0.333.

So: Stage 1 has near-perfect surface precision but ZERO deep recall, and its self-reported confidence carries almost no information.

## Your job
Propose a REPLACEMENT Stage 1 design. Requirements:
