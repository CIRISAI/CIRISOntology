# The Babel Object — one artifact, twenty-two lawful states

**v2, corrected 2026-08-18 (steward's catch, same day as `SM_LADDER_NOTE.md` §6).**
**The construction: one object, 11 × 2 = 22 states, no more, no less — generated not by
touching the object but by choosing which of the eleven artifact-local kinds is edited, and
then declaring whether anything else survives to check the edit against.** A reverse Tower
of Babel: at Babel one tongue became many because the coordinates of understanding were
lost; here twenty-two readings resolve into one understanding because every reading declares
its coordinate. The confusion of tongues was an undeclared frame.

The v1 reading of this object claimed forty states from a second multiplying coordinate.
That coordinate was not real — see **"Superseded: the v1 40-state reading"** at the foot of
this file for the full retracted geometry and why it failed. Everything above that heading
is the corrected v2 account.

## The geometry it demonstrates

The state of a reading is a **pair**: **(kind, record-bit)** — not a triple. There is
exactly one coordinate beyond the choice of kind.

* **Kind** — which of the ELEVEN base-plane changes was made to the object (one edit each,
  same document): Premises, Identity, Priorities, Rules, Process, Facts, Confidence, Model,
  Manner, Structure, **Circumstances**. Claimed invariant under the frame.
* **Record bit** — set by the FRAME alone: does any other record survive? (2 values)

That is the whole product. There is no second, design-level coordinate multiplying against
the eleven kinds. What v1 called the "circumstances-bit" was not an independent classifying
axis at all — see **the disposition note**, below, before reading the count as 10×2×2.

Complete orthogonality is the claim, now over one coordinate instead of two: flip the frame
and only the record-bit moves; the kind never moves under it. 11 × 2 = **22 distinct
states**, the full product, with no interaction — and every one is a PREDICTION the plane
study's annotators can test (22 cells × 3 annotators = 66 judgments, well under the forty-
cell run's $0.10).

## The disposition note — POLICY, not a second classification

v1 introduced a "circumstances-bit," set by whether the comparison DESIGN held the edited
clause fixed or let it vary, and multiplied it against all ten of its edits to get a second
factor of 2. That was the fusion this registry exists to catch: **the held-fixed-or-free
question is not a classification that can attach to any kind. It is the DISPOSITION that
already, and only, belongs to the Circumstances kind** — `WrongKind.disposition .contingent
= .outOfScope`, i.e. "this variation is not classified, because the comparison never held
it fixed." That is a POLICY statement about edit 11 specifically, not a bit that multiplies
against Premises or Facts or any other kind. Folding it in as a universal second coordinate
was reading a policy as if it were a classification, and it produced ten extra rows for
every one of the ten kinds that never had a design question to answer in the first place.

Record is the only kind whose classification genuinely depends on an outside coordinate
(`WrongKind.frameDependent = testimonial => true`), and even Record does not GENERATE a
site of its own (`record_not_site_generated`) — it enters strictly as the ×2 frame relation
on every kind's row, never as a twelfth edit. Circumstances' held-fixed question, by
contrast, is not a second coordinate on every kind; it is the one kind's own policy,
already fully expressed the moment edit 11 exists. Nothing left to multiply.

## Why "reverse Babel," precisely

Babel: one object of understanding, shattered into mutually unintelligible readings, because
the readers no longer shared coordinates. This object: twenty-two readings, zero confusion,
because every reading carries its coordinate on its face. The states do not multiply meaning
away — they resolve back to ONE artifact plus one declared bit. Undeclared coordinates are
the confusion of tongues; declared ones are its cure. Which is the entire argument of the
11+1 geometry, held in the palm of one hand.

## The spectrum of lawful counts — and why there is no forbidden neighbor now

Downward compressions are still diagnostic, but the arithmetic changed shape along with the
coordinate count. With only ONE multiplying coordinate (frame), each kind contributes
`a_k ∈ {2, 1, 0}`: both frame-readings realized (2), the frame-bit inert for that kind so
its F⁺/F⁻ rows adjudicate identically (1), or the kind fully absorbed into a neighbor, both
rows gone (0). So

> **N = 22 − (b + 2c)** — `b` kinds with an inert frame-bit (deficit 1 each), `c` kinds
> fully absorbed (deficit 2 each).

Because the deficits on offer are **1 and 2**, every integer from 11 (every kind reduced to
one merged row) up to 22 (the full product) is reachable by some mix of `b` and `c` — there
is **no forbidden neighbor**. That is the direct consequence of dropping the second
coordinate, and it is the corrected geometry's least intuitive and most important feature.

**v1's headline result — "39 is unreachable, the unique gap in the semigroup generated by
deficits {2,3}" — does not port, and this is its formal death, not just a note.** That
theorem was about the RETRACTED two-coordinate product (10×2×2=40, deficits drawn from
{2,3}, Frobenius number 1). With one coordinate the deficit-generating set is {1,2}, whose
Frobenius number is −1: there is no unreachable count above the trivial floor at all. The
tooth is gone because the coordinate that grew it was never real. The full statement, kept
for the record and marked dead, is in the superseded appendix.

A measured count outside [11, 22] is still diagnostic exactly as before (24 → missing kind,
44 → a resurrected coordinate). A measured count strictly inside [11, 22] localizes to WHICH
kinds and frames went inert via the adjudication table — but no interior count is
architecturally forbidden, and claiming one is now itself the error to watch for.

## The valence reading — two imports that still earn their keep, one retired

**1. Coordinates are degeneracy-lifting fields (the Zeeman import) — one field, not two.**
Without a declared frame, `(K, Record:FIRES)` and `(K, Record:CLEAN)` are DEGENERATE — one
unresolved line per kind. Declaring the frame is switching on the one field this geometry
has. Field off: eleven degenerate lines. Field on: the full twenty-two. This still reframes
REFUSED exactly: an instrument refusing without its coordinate is not declining to answer —
it is telling you *you asked for the split spectrum with the field off.* And the Babel story
still lands as physics: the confusion of tongues is unresolved degeneracy. (v1 had a second
field here — design — and split ten lines into forty in two steps. That field was never
real; there is only one split to make.)

**2. Warrant is a gauge direction.** Forty-four readings collapsing onto twenty-two states
under the W-split is a symmetry NO observable in the system lifts
(`warrant_invisible_to_kind`) — which is precisely what physics calls gauge redundancy:
transformations that change the description and no measurable. Kinds and the record-bit are
the gauge-invariant observables. The plane study's W-arm then has a crisp name: **it
measures whether human annotators are gauge-invariant.** The theorem says the formal system
is; authority bias is measured gauge symmetry breaking in the human classifier.

**3. The nebulium lesson, RETIRED as a specific instantiation, kept as a general caution.**
v1 staked the sixty-year Huggins→Bowen misreading of forbidden [O III] lines as "nebulium"
against a specific number: a measured 39 was never to be read as a new kind, only as a
selection-rule failure. That specific stake is now moot — there is no forbidden number left
in the 22-state geometry to protect, because the axis that produced 39 as special was never
real. The GENERAL form of the lesson survives and is worth keeping: a surprising measured
count should be checked against adjudication noise and coordinate mis-specification before
being read as evidence of a new kind. It simply has no numerical target to defend here
anymore, and reporting that plainly is more honest than porting a dead tooth forward.

**The boundary, so this stays a recognition and not a claim:** there is no Hamiltonian
here, no dynamics, no transitions — the analogy is about COUNTING, not energies. Our
per-kind contribution is now just 2 (present) or 1 (inert) or 0 (absorbed) — a single binary
flip, not a Cartesian product of two flips. The exact mathematics of the reachable-count set
is now the trivial numerical semigroup generated by {1,2} (everything above the floor is
representable), not the Frobenius-number machinery v1 needed. The atom is still the cousin
who learned the lessons first, not the theorem — and this pass, the corrected geometry
needed fewer of its lessons than the retracted one did.
