# The Babel Object — one artifact, forty lawful states

**The construction Eric asked for: one object, 10 × 2 × 2 = 40 states, no more, no less —
generated not by touching the object but by choosing what to relate it to.** A reverse Tower
of Babel: at Babel one tongue became many because the coordinates of understanding were lost;
here forty readings resolve into one understanding because every reading declares its
coordinates. The confusion of tongues was undeclared frames.

## The geometry it demonstrates

The state of a reading is a triple: **(kind, record-bit, circumstances-bit)**.

* **Kind** — which of the TEN base-plane changes was made to the object (one edit each,
  same document). Claimed invariant under everything below.
* **Record bit** — set by the FRAME alone: does any other record survive? (2 values)
* **Circumstances bit** — set by the DESIGN alone: was the edited clause held fixed by the
  comparison, or free to vary? (2 values)

Complete orthogonality is the claim: each factor reads exactly one coordinate. Flip the
frame and only the record-bit moves; flip the design and only the circumstances-bit moves;
the kind never moves under either. 10 × 2 × 2 = **40 distinct states**, the full product,
with no interactions — and every one is a PREDICTION the plane study's annotators can test
(40 cells × 3 annotators = 120 judgments, ~$0.10).

## THE TWO COORDINATES (two values each, one paragraph each)

**FRAME — what else survives?**
* **F⁺ (registry survives):** the Registry at Highfield keeps certified duplicates of every
  edition of every waystation order, and its copies are available. → *Every before-value is
  re-derivable; the record-bit is CLEAN in all ten edits.*
* **F⁻ (sole copy):** no registry exists; this parchment is the only record of the order in
  the world. → *The before-value of the edited clause can no longer be established from
  what survives; the record-bit FIRES — in all ten, identically.*

**DESIGN — what did the comparison hold?**
* **D⁺ (clause held):** editions are compared under the valley's uniformity rule — every
  clause was intended identical across copies. → *The edit is in scope; circumstances-bit
  CLEAN.*
* **D⁻ (clause free):** waystations locally adapt their orders; clause-level differences
  between copies are expected and uncontrolled. → *The edit is outside the held-fixed set;
  circumstances-bit FIRES — the variation is incidental to this comparison, whatever its
  kind.*

## The forty states

Each edit `k ∈ {1..10}` × frame `∈ {F⁺,F⁻}` × design `∈ {D⁺,D⁻}`:

> **state(k, F, D) = ( kind_k , Record: F⁻?FIRES:CLEAN , Circumstances: D⁻?FIRES:CLEAN )**

Predictions, staked for the annotator test: the **kind column is constant down every edit's

## Why "reverse Babel," precisely

Babel: one object of understanding, shattered into mutually unintelligible readings, because
the readers no longer shared coordinates. This object: forty readings, zero confusion,
because every reading carries its coordinates on its face. The states do not multiply
meaning away — they resolve back to ONE artifact plus two declared bits. Undeclared
coordinates are the confusion of tongues; declared ones are its cure. Which is the entire
argument of the 10+1+1 geometry, held in the palm of one hand.

## The spectrum of lawful counts — and the one forbidden neighbor

Downward compressions are as diagnostic as additions, and not every count is reachable.
Under per-kind orthogonality each kind contributes `a_k ∈ {4, 2, 1}` states (both bits
active / one inert / both inert), so the observable count is

> **N = 40 − (2b + 3c)** — `b` kinds with one inert coordinate, `c` kinds with both.

* **36, two ways, distinguishable by collision pattern:** (i) a full kind absorbed —
  `9 × 2 × 2`, the 9+1+1 verdict, with all four of its states collapsing into a
  neighbour's column (the plane study's named at-risk candidate: Manner); or (ii) two
  kinds each losing one coordinate — the collisions sit inside two columns instead of
  deleting one. Same count, different fingerprint, and the adjudication table tells them
  apart.
* **38** = one kind with one inert bit (Structure is the live suspect: does "out of
  scope" mean anything to a parser that is broken either way?). **30** = the coordinates
  are not independent (F × D collapsing to three joint values). **20** = a coordinate
  wholly inert. All lawful, all localizable.
* **39 is UNREACHABLE.** Deficits come only in steps of 2 and 3, and every deficit ≥ 2 is
  expressible — so deficit 1 is the unique gap. **A measured 39 cannot mean a smaller
  taxonomy under any orthogonal degradation whatsoever; it can only mean a genuine
  interaction** — a state that exists for one specific (kind, frame, design) combination
  and no factorization — which falsifies the product geometry itself, not a component.

So the count is a spectrometer: 44 and 80 say the taxonomy is short, 36-38-30-20 say it is
long or partially inert and WHERE, and 39 — the nearest number to full — is the one reading
the geometry stakes its life on never seeing.

## The valence reading — three imports that earn their keep, one boundary

The resemblance is real and it has a common lemma: *state spaces that factor as products
have observable multiplicities that are sums of allowed factor-multiplicities, and the gaps
in the representable set are structural fingerprints.* Atomic shells instantiate it with
representation theory; this object instantiates it with two binary observables. One
principle, two costumes — so per the hits-not-strikes rule, take the older costume's
lessons rather than its authority:

**1. Coordinates are degeneracy-lifting fields (the Zeeman import).** Without a declared
frame, `(K, Record:FIRES)` and `(K, Record:CLEAN)` are DEGENERATE — one unresolved line.
Declaring the frame is switching on the field that splits it; declaring the design splits
it again. Field off: ten degenerate lines. One field: twenty. Both: the full forty. This
reframes REFUSED exactly: an instrument refusing without its coordinate is not declining to
answer — it is telling you *you asked for the split spectrum with the field off.* And the
Babel story lands as physics: the confusion of tongues is unresolved degeneracy.

**2. The nebulium lesson (the forbidden-line import, and it is a gate).** Astronomy spent
sixty years (Huggins 1864 → Bowen 1927) reading the nebulae's forbidden [O III] lines as a
NEW ELEMENT, "nebulium." It was never a new element — it was the assumed selection rules
failing in an unfamiliar regime (low-density gas letting metastable transitions through).
**Staked now for the plane study: a measured forbidden count — 39 above all — is never to
be read as a new kind. It is to be read as the selection rules (orthogonality) failing.**
New-element is the seductive reading and the wrong one; it is also exactly the g−2 shape
(a tension read as new physics that lived in the inputs). Nebulium is the known-bad anchor
this outcome-rule ships with.

**3. Warrant is a gauge direction.** Eighty readings collapsing onto forty states under the
W-split is a symmetry NO observable in the system lifts (`warrant_invisible_to_kind`) —
which is precisely what physics calls gauge redundancy: transformations that change the
description and no measurable. Kinds and bits are the gauge-invariant observables. The

gauge-invariant.** The theorem says the formal system is; authority bias is measured gauge
symmetry breaking in the human classifier.

**The boundary, so this stays a recognition and not a claim:** there is no Hamiltonian

The atom is the cousin who learned the lessons first, not the theorem.
