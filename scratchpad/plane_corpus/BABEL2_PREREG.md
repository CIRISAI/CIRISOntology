# BABEL-2 — stage 1 of the interferometer: double edits on the Thornfield object
# FROZEN 2026-08-21, before any composite item existed or any judgment ran.

## The design honesty point, stated first

All eleven Babel edits touch DISJOINT clauses, so the composite text of edits {i,j} is
ORDER-INVARIANT: i-then-j and j-then-i produce byte-identical AFTER states. Edit-route
holonomy is therefore not testable at text level on this object — and that is exactly the
interferometer's own distinction: the endpoint is one state; the ROUTE is narration. What
stage 1 CAN test: (a) the off-diagonal structure — what kind a two-kind composite reads
as; (b) NARRATED-route dependence — the same endpoint presented with the two changes
described in opposite orders. "Loops remember which route you took" becomes: does the
telling order move the reading, endpoint fixed?

## Items (mechanical, from the sealed v2 corpus)

All 55 unordered pairs {i,j} of the eleven edits. Per pair: before = the v2 object; after
= both edits applied (order-invariant); TWO presentations differing ONLY in the
variation_site sentence order ("Two things changed: [site_i] And separately: [site_j]" vs
the reverse). 55 × 2 × 3 models = 330 judgments, BASE condition, standing instrument,
est. ≤ $0.05 (cap $0.10; human-upheld, preflight file).

## Frozen scoring

Per presentation: modal by plurality (2/3), ties = no modal. Per pair: the pair's READING
= the two presentation modals.

## Staked predictions (before any run)

- **P1 DOMINANCE:** in ≥ 80% of pairs with modals, the modal is one of the two component
  kinds — composites do not routinely read as third kinds.
- **P2 CHANNELED LEAKAGE:** where a third kind IS modal, it lies in the stage-0 boundary
  channels (Facts for {Premises,Model,Circumstances}-involving pairs; Manner for
  Structure-involving pairs) in ≥ 2/3 of such cases.
- **P3 NARRATION-INVARIANCE:** the two presentations agree (same modal or both none) in
  ≥ 90% of pairs. The interferometer expects route-dependence to live BELOW panel grain;
  a large narration effect would be an instrument finding about reading order, reported
  as such and NOT as holonomy.
- **P4 SURFACE ABSORPTION:** in pairs of one surface kind (Facts, Rules, Manner, Identity)
  with one deep kind, the modal is the SURFACE kind in ≥ 2/3 of decided pairs — the 91%
  surface-traffic result's compositional face.

## Bands

VOID — agreement: Fleiss κ < 0.4 over the 110 presentations. VOID — coverage: < 70% of
presentations carry a modal. Otherwise each prediction scores HELD / FAILED on its stated
threshold, with counts; no prediction may borrow support from another; a FAILED P1 with
channeled destinations is P2's question, not a defence of P1.

## What this is not

Model-system measurement of panel behavior on one artifact. No physics, no corpus claim,
no page change. Stage-2 (real loop tests on labeled change-streams) is a separate future
prereg.
