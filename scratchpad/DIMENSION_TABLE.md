# The dimension table — what each of the eleven IS, and its symmetry

Every entry read off the Lean (`Core/Generator.lean` Site.kind, `Core/Surface.lean` blocks
and depths, `Core/Symmetry.lean` automorphism groups). Reference document; the physics
readings live in N18_BRIDGE_NOTE.md and are never claimed here.

## The four surfaces — one per family, readable off the changed words

| # | dimension | site | family | what it IS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Facts | factContent | assertive | the content of a claim — what the world is said to be |
| 2 | Rules | directiveContent | directive | the content of a requirement — what is to be done |
| 3 | Identity | declarationContent | declaration | the content of a declaration — what something COUNTS AS |
| 4 | Manner | register | carrier | the vehicle's manner — how it is said |

SYMMETRY: **rigid**. All 24 automorphisms fix all four (surfaces map to same-force
surfaces; one per family; hence fixed).

## The three — the assertive apparatus (the gap around a claim)

| # | dimension | site | what it IS |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Confidence | strengthMarker | how strongly the claim is held |
| 6 | Model | appliedRule | the rule used to derive it |
| 7 | Premises | foundingAssumption | the assumption it rests on |

SYMMETRY: **S3, freely interchangeable — BROKEN to the identity by the grounding order**
(Premises founds Model derives Facts modulated by Confidence). This breaking is the entire
factor of six between |Aut| = 24 (without Stack.lean) and 4 (with it).

## The two + two — the twinned pairs, both unbroken

| # | dimension | site | family | what it IS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Priorities | preferenceOrder | directive | the order over outcomes — what beats what |
| 9 | Process | stepOrder | directive | the order over steps — what comes first |
| 10 | Structure | encoding | carrier | which format carries it |
| 11 | Circumstances | instanceToken | carrier | which instance carries it |

SYMMETRY: **Z2 x Z2, unbroken** — the residual group. Priorities/Process are both ORDERINGS
(the structure cannot tell an outcome-order from a step-order); Structure/Circumstances are
both TOKENIZATIONS (both choose a vehicle, leaving content fixed).

## The zero — forced, not missing

Every depth is a place where the GAP between saying and satisfying can be structured.

- assertive: a claim can be true or false -> gap -> strength, derivation, grounding = **3**
- directive: a requirement can be complied with or not -> gap -> what-beats-what,
  what-comes-first = **2** (NOT strength: measured 12/12 that deontic strength changes
  compliance-conditions, hence is content — CONJUGATION_TEST_RESULTS.md)
- declaration: **no gap** — double direction of fit, saying makes it so; cannot half-declare,
  cannot derive (it IS the ground), cannot sequence a single act = **0**
- carrier: no force, so no satisfaction conditions of its own; its depths are vehicle-
  structure not gap-structure = **2**

The zero is the signature of SELF-SATISFACTION: the one dimension where word and world
close on each other with nothing in between.

## The +1

**Record** (testimonial) — a relation between artifact and frame, provably not generated by
any site (`record_not_site_generated`), outside the automorphism group entirely because it
is not a site to permute. In the `4,3,0,3,2` reading the "2" is {Identity, Record}.

## THE FORK — the four have two different odd-ones-out, and they disagree

- **By depth**: **Identity** is singular (0 depths vs 3, 2, 2). This nominates Identity as the
  in-plane time in the two-times reading.
- **By conversion**: **Manner** is singular. The mention/enact projection relates
  Facts-Rules-Identity (a described rule reads as a fact; an enacted fact reads as a rule),
  and `carrier_inert_under_mention` proves Manner participates in NONE of it. Three mix,
  one is inert.

Both are theorems, and they single out different dimensions. Any "3+1" story must say WHICH
property it means. Unresolved, and recorded as unresolved.
