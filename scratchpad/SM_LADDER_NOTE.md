**What it left behind — the transformed finding (theorem candidate, next Lean brick):**

> **The CP phase is unmaintainable by pair-level upkeep.** A repair that knows only the pair

structure's SIZE (angles) and loses its IDENTITY (phase). Consequence for the ladder's
