# AMENDMENT 4 — outcome (b) is withdrawn from this campaign

Committed **before the unblind**, on the reviewer's ruling in response to the provenance
objection raised in `SKY_REALDATA_PREUNBLIND.md` §5.1.

## A4.1 The restriction

> **This measurement is restricted to outcomes (a) and (c).**
> **Outcome (b) — "excess beyond the gravitational prediction" — is WITHDRAWN.**
> No excess-beyond-prediction claim may be made from this run, whatever the data reads.

## A4.2 Why

The frozen prediction comes from MultiDark-Patchy, which is **not** an N-body suite: it is
augmented Lagrangian perturbation theory plus a stochastic bias model calibrated to reproduce a
BOSS-like two-point function. `SKY_REALDATA_PREREG.md` §7.1 required the gravitational
prediction to come from N-body mocks, and that requirement is **not met**.

An excess above a bias-calibrated approximate-gravity mock is not evidence of an anomaly,
because that mock was never a first-principles prediction of the three-point sector. Reporting
one as an anomaly would be a category error, and the pre-registration's own §6(b) already
forbade the automatic-primordial reading for the same reason.

## A4.3 What survives

* **Outcome (a)** — detection above all floors, consistent with the prediction — survives, but
  with "consistent with the gravitational prediction" reading as **"consistent with the Patchy
  suite's higher-order structure"**, which is a product of its bias calibration as much as of
  gravity. Any (a) verdict must carry that phrasing.
* **Outcome (c)** — a null above the floors, reported as an honest bound — is unaffected.
* **VOID** conditions are unaffected.

## A4.4 What is deferred, not abandoned

Outcome (b) adjudication is deferred to a future campaign carrying a **first-principles N-body
suite** for the prediction, independent of the suite used for the window and floor model. That
campaign would need its own pre-registration. **Nothing in this run may be cited as evidence
for or against an anomaly.**
