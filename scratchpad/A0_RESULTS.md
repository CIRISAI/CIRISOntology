## 10. Deviations, amendments, and defects found in the frozen design

Every entry below was written to `scratchpad/a0run/AMENDMENTS.md` **before** the computation it
governs, and none was written after an outcome-crossed number was seen.

**A0-NOTE-1 — execution-order reading (not a design change).** §12 lists CP-FACT second; §18
requires the outcome column to open "only by the analysis stage, after every gate that can be
evaluated without it". Reconciled by running all six outcome-blind stages to their done-markers
first, then opening the outcome with **CP-FACT first among the outcome-crossing computations**
and the tautology diagnostic last, exactly as §12 and freeze item 22 require. The seal was
enforced mechanically: `src/a0lib.py::load_rows()` deletes the entire §6.1 banned set from every
record unless the caller passes an explicit opt-in that only analysis-stage scripts use. The
probe's judge calls ran outcome-blind in OB5; only its AUC crossed the outcome.

**A0-NOTE-2 — which "distinct tables" the N1c mixing gate counts.** §7.4 does not define the
term. Pinned before the chain ran: the literal reading (3-way contingency tables) governs and its
pinned consequence is taken; the chain-state count and the exact fibre size are reported beside
it. Measured: 1 table, 10,000 states, fibre size 1 (CP-FACT) and 64 (CP-KIND). **The gate's
1,000-table bar is unreachable by any chain on a 2×2×2 in this corpus**, because the fibre itself
is smaller than the bar — a defect in the gate, disclosed.

**A0-NOTE-3 — N1c is degenerate by construction (analytic proof, recorded before the chain ran).**
Under §7.4's move set, both clusters in any proposed swap carry the same language, so the induced
change in the A–O margin cell (a, 1) is *exactly* the induced change in the 3-way cell (a, L, 1);
requiring the A–O margin to be unchanged is therefore identical to requiring every cell to be
unchanged. Every accepted move leaves the table fixed. **Confirmed on both frames and on
synthetic data**: acceptance 0.647 (CP-FACT) and 0.815 (CP-KIND), thousands of distinct chain
states, **one distinct table**. The pinned NON-MIXING fallback to N2 with printed margin drift is
what the verdict uses; no new null was invented after freeze. The failure is in the conservative
direction — N2's ensemble is wider than the conditional one — and no detection occurred against
the wider null either. **This is a defect in the frozen design, and any successor must not reuse
N1c as written**: a cluster-respecting, margin-preserving null needs a move set that can move
probability between different (action, context) cells, which a whole-vector transpose within one
language cannot do.

**A0-NOTE-4 — the same degeneracy disables N1c as a floor for every mutual information.** §10.1
subtracts an "N1c permutation mean" from `I_K` and `I_L` and V6/V12 read N1c percentiles; an MI is
a function of the very margin N1c holds fixed, so an N1c ensemble reproduces it exactly with zero
spread. Pinned before those numbers were computed: the same NON-MIXING fallback applies, and the
null is **N2** (cluster permutation, 10,000 draws) — wider, hence a higher bar for V6 and V12.
V7's ratio was pinned to read the **floor-subtracted** values, because the leak arm realises up to
six levels against the kind arm's five; the raw ratio (2.53) is reported beside the subtracted one
(2.65) and both fire the gate.

**One code defect found and fixed during the run**, disclosed for completeness: the diff-arm stage
initially read the instruments' result key as `fires` rather than `fired`, which reported zero
firings. Fixed and re-run before any diff-arm number entered this document; the corrected numbers
are in §7. The diff arm is outcome-blind and never kill-bearing, so no gate or band was affected.

**Realisation defects in the frozen design, surfaced by the data** (all reported in place above):
the "matched-alphabet" contest realised 4 levels against 5 because Record came up empty (§4.4);
`KIND-M5` coverage is 76.5% rather than the assumed ~100% (§4.4); V5's placebo bar uses the
no-3-way fit while §7.5's advance projection used an independence estimate, moving the numbers but
not the gauged/ungauged calls (§3); and §16's C5 row quotes pre-m3 frame sizes (§1).

## 12. What A0 established, and what it did not

**Established, and none of it was the thing A0 set out to test:**

1. **The production conscience's override is, in this corpus, a near-deterministic function of
   the proposed action** — PONDER is overridden, SPEAK and TASK_COMPLETE never are, with 54
   exceptions in 2,662 rows. `I(action; override)` = 0.531 nats, against 0.089 for the
   kind-reading and 0.095 for the legacy faculty margin.
2. **Any faculty failure implies an override with zero exceptions** (273/273), with a 4.9%
   residual override rate when all four pass.
3. **The §6.3 text scrub does not work.** An adversarial panel recovers the override from the
   scrubbed transcript at AUC 0.882 on column 1's own frame. The dominant missed cue is
   **`ponder`** — the token rev 2 explicitly dropped from consideration — followed by the scrub's
   own redaction marker and by infrastructure failure text.
4. **The single-text kind-reading adaptation is not decisive enough to use.** 22.6% NO MAJORITY
   on distinct inputs, three-way agreement 26.0%, pairwise κ 0.245–0.429 against PLANE's 0.687,
   and **not one Record label on any of the 120 gold items**, ten of which were gold Record. On
   production text the panel does reach Record, but rarely — 9 of 716 distinct inputs, 25 of
   1,885 FRAME-TL rows, and zero on FRAME-H, which is why column 1's `KIND-M5` arm realised four
   levels against the legacy arm's five.
5. **The frozen verdict null cannot work**, and the proof is structural rather than empirical
   (A0-NOTE-3).
6. **The cheap diff heuristics are silent on 94% of resolvable chain pairs**, so A1's "decisive at
   0 API cost on the bulk" premise needs re-examination.

**Not established:**

- Nothing about whether the whole-only share of action-context-outcome triples concentrates on
  recorded overrides. The authoritative table had no room for the question.
- Nothing about whether a kind-reading carries as much decision-relevant information as the
  legacy faculties. The contest's CI was 15.7× its point estimate and every gauge failed.
- Nothing about the 4→1 conscience collapse, which §3.10 established was untestable on this
  corpus before any data was crossed.
- Nothing about CIRIS agents in general. Every analysed frame is overwhelmingly one task class
  (qa_eval, 96.9% of FRAME-H and 98.2% of FRAME-CP), one cognitive state, two or three action
  classes, and three days of deployment wearing a five-week costume.

**What a successor design would need**, stated as measured requirements rather than aspirations:
an outcome column with variance that is not already exhausted by a single recorded categorical
field; a null construction whose move set can actually move the statistic; a kind-reading
instrument that produces a majority on more than three-quarters of its inputs; and a scrub
validated by an adversarial probe **before** the campaign rather than during it.
