v0c BAKE-OFF — the USE/MENTION feature (task N5)
==============================================================================

WHAT WAS BUILT, AND WHAT EACH OUTCOME WOULD HAVE MEANT (stated first)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Part D measured use/mention blindness: a norm an artifact DESCRIBES reads
as a norm it ENACTS, an is-a it OBSERVES reads as one it ASSIGNS. Four of
its twelve items were absorbed that way (empirical-report-07, -08, -09,
-11). v0c adds a mention detector and wires it three ways:
  empirical_v2   MENTION ROUTE  — a mentioned span whose content moved is
                                  a change to what the artifact CLAIMS.
  deontic_v2     MENTION GUARD  — plus the mirror ENACTING ROUTE (an
                                  indicative standing order in an artifact
                                  that declares itself applied as written).
  ontological_v2 MENTION GUARD  — plus the PERFORMATIVE-REGISTRY route,
                                  because registers and catalogues DO
                                  assign. Part D's registry items are that
                                  control and must keep firing Identity.

Meanings fixed in advance of the corpus run:
  * four absorbed items now read Facts, eight others unchanged  -> feature works
  * any corpus item losing a true fire or gaining a false one    -> REGRESSION,
    reported item by item whether or not it is repaired
  * the detector never firing on the corpus                      -> the corpus
    CANNOT validate the feature; it bounds regression, nothing more
The third outcome is the one that occurred. It is not support (rule 6).

4. REGRESSIONS — every corpus reading that changed
==============================================================================
NONE. Zero of the 744 (248 items x 3 changed instruments) readings moved.
Zero improvement on this corpus, too — see the activation table below.

Two regressions WERE found during the build, by this same control, and are
recorded because the corpus caught them and nothing else would have:
  (a) the quoted-span route accepted the ASCII apostrophe as a quote mark,
      so possessives ('Tuesday's second round') read as quotation. It cost
      4 items: deontic-report-03 and ontological-report-03 lost true fires,
      axiotic-report-02 and nomological-report-03 gained false empirical
      fires. Fixed by restricting the route to double quotes.
  (b) the registry route accepted a bare 'as'/'under' as an assignment cue,
      so 'contractors ... count as staff' (axiomatic-policy-04, a Premises
      stipulation) read as a register entry. Fixed by requiring the slot to
      be NAMED (name/genus/class/heading/... or an assignment verb).

5. DETECTOR ACTIVATION — why the corpus tables did not move
==============================================================================
on 248 corpus items: genre word in title 45, MENTION 3, performative registry 1, performative enacting 0
routes fired: {'M1b reporting-frame': 3}
MENTION by authored kind: {'epistemic': 1, 'structural': 1, 'axiomatic': 1}

   epistemic-report-01        epistemic    routes=['M1b reporting-frame'] registry=False enacting=False
   structural-process-04      structural   routes=['M1b reporting-frame'] registry=False enacting=False
   axiomatic-policy-04        axiomatic    routes=[] registry=True enacting=False
   axiomatic-process-04       axiomatic    routes=['M1b reporting-frame'] registry=False enacting=False

Read plainly: the artifact-level routes (attributed-norm-in-genre,
declared-descriptive-stance) fire on ZERO corpus items, and the three
reporting-frame hits are all caught by the route's own deferral guards
(Premises, Confidence, Model). The corpus therefore bounds this feature's
regression at zero and validates nothing about it. The 248 items were
authored before Part D named the boundary and do not carry the stance
sentences the detector keys on; Part D's twelve were authored to carry
them. The repair is in-sample. A wild test is owed.

The sharpest form of that limitation, named so it can be attacked:
  empirical-report-09  'The large tree at the lychgate is an English oak'
                        -> 'is a sycamore'      (authored FACTS)
  ontological-report-01 'The raised feature ... is a seamount'
                        -> 'is a mud volcano'   (authored IDENTITY)
Both sit in survey-genre artifacts; both are copular; both replace a
common-noun predicate. The ONLY surface feature separating them is that
the Part-D item carries an explicit stance sentence ('These notes describe
what was found on the day; nothing here directs any work') and the older
item does not. If wild descriptive artifacts do not carry such a sentence,
route M2 will not fire on them and the absorption returns. That is the
kill for this feature: a descriptive artifact with no self-describing
sentence whose observed is-a changes, still read as Identity.
