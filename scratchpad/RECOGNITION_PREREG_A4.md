dimension covers is a change of which kind?" — admits the construal "a change that alters

## The repair (this amendment's only change)

The operational question is replaced, for the re-run, by:

> "Consider an artifact — a document, transcript, or record. A change is made to it, and
> the WHOLE EFFECT of that change is of the sort this item describes: the change does to
> the artifact what the item's definition says such acts do. That change is a change of
> which kind?"

Still type-matched (both sides are changes to an artifact); construal (b) is closed by
anchoring the item's content as the change's EFFECT, not as the text being edited. NOTHING
