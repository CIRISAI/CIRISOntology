# Scope note — the contextuality/cost link is REFUSED on this instrument

The proposed stake (CF of a circuit's magic states predicts simulation cost)
is not posable with the current pieces, for two named reasons:

1. Our magic tier's naive branch sum costs EXACTLY 2^t regardless of the
   injected state's magic — cost tracks T-count, not magic magnitude. The
   cost-magic link only becomes measurable on a rank-REDUCED simulator
   (Bravyi–Gosset extent), which is the named next improvement.
2. The contextuality-magic equivalence (Howard–Wallman–Veitch–Emerson 2014)
   lives in ODD PRIME dimensions, where Wigner negativity and contextuality
   coincide. Qubits are the awkward case: a single qubit has no state-
   dependent contextuality to measure (KS needs dimension ≥ 3), and our
   QF-1 CF instrument is a qutrit instrument.

An arm that cannot fail — or cannot be posed — is refused, not staked. The
posable successor, when wanted: implement the BG extent, then stake
cost = extent^{...} against measured extents; or move the CF instrument to
qutrit circuits where Howard et al.'s equivalence gives the derived link.
