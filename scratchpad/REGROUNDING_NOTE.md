# The re-grounding hypothesis (the steward's, 2026-08-20)

> "Not speech acts: forms of recognition of state and transition potential offered to
> conscious beings in this universe."

The eleven kinds' speech-act derivation (Generator.lean) is proposed to be the SPECIAL
CASE, not the ground: the kinds are the distinct ways a conscious agent can recognize
STATE and TRANSITION POTENTIAL, with speech acts as recognition-communicated.

## Why this reframe has evidence before it has a test

The day's blind matchers, given our measured profiles and all of physics to choose from,
never once landed on a force or a particle. They landed on the universe's RECOGNITION
INTERFACE every time: the einselected pointer sector (what can be recognized as stable
state), constraints (recognizable transition potential), the stipulative definition
(constituted identity), the Past-Hypothesis tower (the recognition stack), the null
direction (the recognition-invariant presentation). A speech-act taxonomy has no reason to
keep matching epistemic physics; a recognition taxonomy does.

It also explains the wild adequacy: maps, traces, code and registries are not speech, and
the taxonomy held anyway (339 wild changes, one glyph no-fit).

## The re-reading

Facts = recognized state · Rules = recognized transition constraint · Priorities/Process =
orderings over potential transitions · Identity = CONSTITUTED state (recognition that
makes-so) · Confidence/Model/Premises = the recognition's strength, applied rule, and
given · Manner/Structure/Circumstances = recognition's embodiment · Record = whether past
recognitions survive their frame.

## The two tests it licenses

1. **Generator2 (mechanizable):** rebuild the generator on recognition primitives —
   {state-recognition, transition-potential-recognition, constitution} replacing
   {assertive, directive, declarative} — and prove the image is the SAME eleven. Same
   image => re-grounding at theorem level, speech-acts were scaffolding. Different image
   => the reframe is wrong or the taxonomy is language-specific. Either outcome is a
   result.
2. **Non-linguistic recognition systems:** if the kinds are recognition-forms, they should
   be recoverable from systems that recognize state+transition without language (RL agents'
   abstraction structure; perception-action taxonomies). Unstaked; design owed.
