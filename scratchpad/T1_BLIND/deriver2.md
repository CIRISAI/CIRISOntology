FLIP TEST. P is a gap site for mode m only if: m's stated satisfaction condition depends on P; P is not fixed by performing m; and holding the performance AND the mode's content site fixed, varying P alone can flip satisfaction. Consequence: the PLAIN mismatch (asserting what is false, expressing an attitude one lacks) is NOT a gap site — it is a change of value at the R1 content site, since R1 already holds that mode's world-facing matter. The gaps are the other ways a completed performance fails.

SLOT RULE. Different participant slots (performer, principal, addressee) are different sites; different properties of one slot (an agent's capacity vs. authority) are one site. Without this, identity sites multiply without limit.
