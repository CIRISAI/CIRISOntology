# deriver-3, verbatim (order: A=recognition, B=Habermas, C=Jakobson) — SEMI-BLIND, self-disclosed

BLIND DERIVATION PASS 3 — deliverable. Totals: A = 16, B = 15, C = 20 (17 under a collision reading I flag).

Headline finding: the totals differ but the GAP-SITE SETS nest. C = A's gaps + 1 ("how the parts are arranged"). B = A's gaps − 1 ("whether the other takes it up"). Derived independently, before comparing.

=== TWO CONVENTIONS, FIXED BEFORE COUNTING ===
C1 — A FALSE CONTENT IS NOT A GAP. R1 already assigns one site per mode for what the mode is about; if a state-recognition fails because the state doesn't obtain, the thing that must change is the state it names — a change AT the content site. So R2 gaps are only the NON-CONTENT ways satisfaction detaches from performance: places where content could stay fixed and satisfaction still fail. Without this rule every mode gets a free gap duplicating its own content site.
C2 — A SITE IS A PLACE, SO SITES DEDUP ACROSS MODES. Two modes pointing at the same place = one place. Every dedup is recorded; un-deduped sums given at the end.

================ MODE SET A — recognition triple ================

(a) R2 GAP ARGUMENTS

STATE-RECOGNITION ("satisfied iff the recognized state actually obtains; when it fails the recognizer's information was wrong")
G1 the grip. What is recognized is a state held with some force; "certainly thus" and "probably thus" are recognitions of the same state with different satisfaction conditions, so force is a parameter the content doesn't fix. Varies both ways: right about the state, unentitled to the certainty performed; wrong about the state, properly hedged.
G2 what is taken as given rather than recognized. The condition quantifies over "the recognized state," and a state is individuated only relative to conditions the recognition assumes without recognizing. Those can fail with content untouched — the recognition isn't unsatisfied-though-performed, it isn't evaluable. Assumption-failure and content-falsity vary independently.
G3 the scheme that individuates states. "The world is thus" presupposes a carving under which there are states to be thus. Same recognition under a different individuation goes true → false → not well-formed with no change in what was meant. Matching needs a mapping rule; the mapping rule is its own site.

TRANSITION-RECOGNITION ("satisfied iff the transition is genuinely available or required as recognized — the world moved, or can be moved")
G4 the situation indexed to. The condition NAMES the world's moving. Availability is availability FROM a situation, and the situation is not part of the transition recognized. Satisfaction flips with no error and no content change. Cleanest gap in the set: the mode's own wording separates "the world moved" from "the recognizer's information was wrong."
G5 the route. Genuine availability is availability BY SOME MEANS. Means can fail while the endpoint stands; a well-formed means can be recognized for an unavailable endpoint. Independent both ways.
G6 the standard that makes a move required. The condition disjoins "available OR required." Availability is what the world affords; requirement is a verdict of a governing standard. Available-not-required and required-not-available both real, so the source of requirement is its own parameter.
G7 the ranking among competing requirements. "Required" is determinate only when requirements don't conflict, and conflict is the normal case. With two moves each genuinely required, "this one, now" can be unsatisfied while every availability/requirement claim under it is true. The selector is not itself a transition. WEAKEST gap in A — flagged as a judgment call.

CONSTITUTION ("satisfied by its own successful performance; the status comes to obtain because the recognition is performed AND ACCEPTED")
Note: this mode has NO content gap — the world conforms by fiat, so satisfaction cannot fail by mismatch. It can only fail at conditions on the performance.
G8 who performs it. Same words, same status, same audience: succeeds from one performer, fails from another. Standing is not the content and not the performance; it gates whether performing suffices.
G9 whether it is taken up. The condition names acceptance as its own conjunct. The act occurs completely and the status does not come to obtain. Independent of standing: authorized-but-unaccepted and unauthorized-but-accepted are both real.
G10 the form of the performance. For a status-conferring act the form is constitutive, not decorative — wrong formula, wrong setting botches it with standing intact and audience willing. Standing failures and form failures are separately repairable, so separate places.
NON-GAP recorded: insincerity opens NO site here, because on this condition the status still comes to obtain. Noted so the boundary with B/C is explicit rather than accidental.

(b) SITES
Content (3): 1 what is claimed to be the case · 2 which move is claimed open or owed · 3 which status is being conferred.
Gap (10): 4 how firmly it is held · 5 what is taken for granted rather than claimed · 6 the scheme that says what counts as a state · 7 the situation the claim is indexed to · 8 the route by which the move is made · 9 the standard that makes the move owed · 10 which of the owed moves comes first · 11 who has the standing to do it · 12 whether the others take it up · 13 the form the performance must take.
Carrier (3): 14 the notation it is put in · 15 the way the material is laid out · 16 which particular copy or occurrence this is.

(c) TOTAL A = 3 + 10 + 3 = 16. No dedups needed; every mode opened places no other mode opened.

================ MODE SET B — Habermas's three validity claims ================

(a) R2 GAP ARGUMENTS

TRUTH ("about the objective world; satisfied iff the asserted state of affairs obtains")
G1 the force of the claim. Strength is part of what is asserted — "p" and "presumably p" have different satisfaction conditions over the same state. Strength wrong with state right, and right with state wrong.
G2 the unthematized background. "The asserted state of affairs" is picked out against conditions held rather than asserted; their failure detaches satisfaction from performance with no error in what was said.
G3 the carving of the objective world. For there to be a state of affairs to assert, the objective world must be individuated; the apparatus is not the assertion, and under a different one the same assertion may pick out nothing. No correction of content repairs this.
G4 when it obtains (WEAKER — flagged). "Obtains" carries a time/situation index the act of asserting doesn't fix; satisfied at utterance, unsatisfied later, nothing changed. Unlike A, no clause names the world's moving — I read the index off the tense. Reject it and B = 14.

RIGHTNESS ("about the social world of shared norms; satisfied iff the act is legitimate under those norms")
G5 the standard itself. The condition names the norms as a separate relatum from the act; the same act unchanged is legitimate under one norm-set and not another.
G6 the position the actor occupies. Norms assign entitlements to positions, so legitimacy is role-indexed: same act from a different position, norms fixed, different verdict.
G7 the ordering among norms. "Legitimate under those norms" is indeterminate when norms conflict, and shared norm-sets routinely conflict. What resolves it is neither any norm nor the act, and satisfaction depends on it.
G8 the form of the act. Legitimacy attaches to the act AS PERFORMED; performance has register and bearing beyond content. The right act in the wrong manner fails under the very norms that make it right.
G9 the way it was arrived at. The condition makes legitimacy relative to SHARED norms, and sharedness is about how standing was established, not only what it says. Content and actor unimpeachable, route not followed → fails. SECOND JUDGMENT CALL: leans on "shared" doing real work rather than on the surface.
DELIBERATE COLLAPSE: a separate uptake site (whether others in fact hold the norms) collapsed into G5, because this condition presents the norms as ALREADY SHARED. Contrast A, where constitution names acceptance as its own conjunct and earns its own site. This difference is the substantive reason A and B come apart.

TRUTHFULNESS/SINCERITY ("about the speaker's subjective world; satisfied iff the expression matches the inner state")
FINDING: opens NO place the other two hadn't already opened. Reported as a result, not a shortfall.
· manner → G8: matching is a relation over the whole expression, not just its proposition; right content, misfitting form.
· degree → G1: the inner state has an intensity and the expression claims one; same "how much is claimed" place wearing an expressive face instead of an epistemic one.
· self-classification → G3: an expression can fail to match through misclassifying one's own state, not through lying; matching needs a state→expression mapping, the same individuating-scheme place.
· whose state → G6: the condition indexes the match to THE SPEAKER'S state, so speaking for another shifts what must match.

(b) SITES
Content (3): 1 what is asserted to be the case · 2 which norm-governed relation the act invokes · 3 which inner state is expressed.
Gap (9): 4 how strongly it is put · 5 what is held rather than asserted · 6 the carving that says what counts as a state of affairs · 7 when and where it is claimed to hold · 8 the standard the act is judged by · 9 the position the actor speaks from · 10 which standard wins when standards conflict · 11 the form and register of the act · 12 the way the act was arrived at.
Carrier (3): 13 the notation it is put in · 14 the way the material is laid out · 15 which particular copy or occurrence this is.

(c) TOTAL B = 3 + 9 + 3 = 15. Four sincerity gaps deduped into open places; one uptake gap collapsed into the standard.

================ MODE SET C — Jakobson's six functions ================

STRUCTURAL WARNING FIRST, because it changes how the total reads: THREE of the six take the CARRIER LAYER as their content. Phatic is about contact, metalingual about the code, poetic about the message's own form. R3 then adds encoding, presentation and instance token as if untouched — counting the vehicle twice. I compute as instructed and give the corrected figure.

(a) R2 GAP ARGUMENTS

REFERENTIAL ("oriented to context; satisfied when conveyed information matches the facts")
G1 the strength conveyed — information is conveyed at a strength, strength is part of what must match, can be wrong with the information right.
G2 what is relied on but not conveyed — matching is evaluable only against assumed conditions; their failure unseats satisfaction without touching conveyed content.
G3 the carving of the facts — "matches the facts" needs facts individuated, and the individuation is not the message; a different carving leaves the same message nothing to match.
G4 the context it is indexed to — this function is DEFINED by orientation to context, and context is a separate element of the speech event, not part of the message. Message unchanged, context shifts, match flips. EXPLICIT here, unlike B's tense-read.

EMOTIVE ("oriented to the addresser; satisfied when the expression matches the actual attitude")
G5 the form of the expression — the function is borne by intonation, interjection, delivery, so the match runs THROUGH form; stated attitude correct, delivery fails the match, and content-correction doesn't repair it.
· intensity → G1 (deduped; same "how much is claimed" place).
G6 whose attitude it is — satisfaction is indexed to THE ADDRESSER'S state, so who is speaking is a parameter of the match, separate from what is expressed.

CONATIVE ("oriented to the addressee; satisfied by the addressee's uptake or compliance")
G7 whether the other takes it up — satisfaction IS BY uptake, and uptake is not in the speaker's performance; issued perfectly and completely, still unsatisfied. The mode's defining gap.
G8 the standard that makes compliance owed — "compliance" is not mere causation but doing what one is owed; whatever makes it owed is separate from the demand's content and from whether it is taken up.
G9 what the doing consists of — compliance can fail because the act demanded isn't performable AS SPECIFIED, with the addressee willing and the demand legitimate; the steps are a separate place from the demand.
G10 which demand comes first — under conflicting demands compliance is indeterminate; the resolving ranking is none of the demands. Same judgment call flagged in A, included on the same reasoning.
· standing to command → G6 (deduped, consistently with B). CAVEAT: this is the dedup I am LEAST sure of across all three derivations — "who is speaking" and "who has standing to command" may well be two places. Split them and C = 21/18.

PHATIC ("oriented to contact; satisfied when the channel in fact holds")
NO new gap site. Channel-failure is content-side; the audience side is uptake (open); the occasion is the instance token (carrier). Phatic's contribution to the recipe is not a gap but a demonstration that the vehicle layer is real enough to be a mode in its own right.

METALINGUAL ("oriented to the code; satisfied when agreement on the code is reached")
NO new gap site. Satisfaction is by agreement = uptake (open); the code is content here and encoding at the carrier layer. Adjacency noted: the code and the individuating scheme (G3) are hard to separate; I hold them apart on the line that the code is WHICH NOTATION is in use while the scheme is WHICH THINGS THERE ARE to talk about.

POETIC ("oriented to the message; satisfied by the message's own palpable form doing its work")
G11 how the parts are arranged — the form works through relations among elements (parallelism, repetition, ordering), so the arrangement can change with every element preserved and the work stops. Satisfaction detaches from performance at a place NOTHING ELSE in any of the three mode sets opened. Distinct from G5, which is register of delivery; this is composition.
FLAG: G11 vs the presentation carrier is the hardest pair to keep apart anywhere in this exercise. I keep them apart because presentation is layout that leaves what is said untouched, whereas here the arrangement IS the work. Collapse them and C loses one.

(b) SITES
Content (6): 1 what is conveyed to be the case · 2 what attitude is expressed · 3 what the other is being moved to do · 4 whether the channel is open · 5 which code is in force · 6 the message's own form.
Gap (11): 7 how strongly it is put · 8 what is relied on but not conveyed · 9 the carving that says what counts as a fact · 10 the context it is indexed to · 11 the form and delivery it is given · 12 who it comes from and who has standing · 13 whether the other takes it up · 14 the standard that makes compliance owed · 15 what the doing consists of · 16 which demand comes first · 17 how the parts are arranged.
Carrier (3): 18 the notation it is put in · 19 the way the material is laid out · 20 which particular copy or occurrence this is.

(c) TOTAL C = 6 + 11 + 3 = 20 as the recipe computes it. Collision-corrected (refusing to count contact, code and message-form as both content and carrier): 3 + 11 + 3 = 17.

The gap layer is the robust part of this recipe; the content-site count is the fragile part.
