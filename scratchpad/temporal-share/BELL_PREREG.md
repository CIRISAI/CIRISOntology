# Pre-registration — the Logos Bell test

**Date:** 2026-07-25. **Status:** written AFTER the classical cap was proved and pushed
(`Core/ShareK.lean`, commit `5c18af7`) and BEFORE any experiment circuit was built or
simulated. The threshold below is a machine-checked theorem; it cannot move after data.

## The claim structure (Bell form)

**The classical law (proved, `entropy_map_le` / `shareK_le_of_pair_uniform`):**
coarse-graining never raises classical entropy — for every classical 5-slot state,
H(whole) ≥ H(pair view) for every pair, equivalently the whole-only share is at most
(5−2)·ln 2 given a uniform pair. This is our "CHSH ≤ 2."

**The quantum prediction:** the five-qubit ring graph state (AME(5,2)) is pure
(S_whole = 0) with every one of its 10 pair reductions maximally mixed
(S_pair = 2 ln 2). Its qShareK is 5 ln 2 — 2 ln 2 above the classical cap. The
violation statistic below is the entropy-monotonicity reversal that no classical
5-slot state can produce.

**What hardware is asked:** can a physical device HOLD that pattern through full
tomography? A yes is the first measured super-classical whole-only share in a physical
system. A no is an honest device-scoped null. Engineered state, law-level claim —
exactly the Bell-test scoping; this is NOT a claim about wild processes, and the
temporal (process-tensor) version remains open.

## Design (fixed)

- **State:** C5 ring graph state: H⊗5, then CZ on edges (0,1),(1,2),(2,3),(3,4),(4,0).
- **Negative control state:** the best classical stabilizer competitor — the uniform
  superposition-free classical code state over the [5,3] code with dual distance 3
  (duals generated by 11100, 00111): H on q0,q2,q4; CX q0→q1, q2→q1, q2→q3, q4→q3.
  Diagonal state, all pair marginals uniform, ideal S_whole = 3 ln 2 > S_pair = 2 ln 2.
- **Tomography:** all 3⁵ = 243 product-basis settings per state, 1024 shots each.
- **Readout calibration:** 2 circuits (|00000⟩, |11111⟩) → per-qubit assignment
  matrices, inverted per setting at the distribution level.
- **Total: 488 circuits × 1024 shots, one job.** No adaptive re-runs.
- **Reconstruction (pinned):** Pauli expectations averaged over all compatible
  settings → linear inversion → PSD projection (eigenvalue clipping with
  redistribution, Smolin–Gambetta–Smith) → trace renormalized. S_vN of the whole and
  of all 10 pair partial traces from eigenvalues.

## Claim statistic (fixed)

V := min over the 10 pairs of S_vN(pair) − S_vN(whole). Ideal C5: V = 2 ln 2 ≈ 1.386.
Ideal control: V = −ln 2. Classical impossibility of V > 0 is the machine-checked
monotonicity (`entropy_map_le`; the diagonal-state correspondence between partial
trace and classical marginal is mechanized at 3 slots, `ptr₁₂_diagEmbed` — the k = 5
instance of that correspondence is stated here as the standard identification and is
the named next mechanization).

**Bias pin:** linear inversion + projection biases S_vN(whole) downward, which FAVORS
the claim; therefore the claim requires the conservative variant:
- bootstrap (B = 200, per-setting multinomial resample, full re-analysis) 1st
  percentile of V > 0, AND
- bias-corrected V_bc := 2·V̂ − mean(V_boot) ≥ 0.05 nat, AND
- per-qubit readout assignment fidelity ≥ 0.95 (else VOID — device day too noisy), AND
- negative control V_ctrl ≤ 0 at its bootstrap 99th percentile (else VOID —
  the pipeline manufactures violations; kept as an instrument finding).
- Fidelity F = ⟨ψ|ρ̂|ψ⟩ is reported alongside (no threshold; context only).

## Meaning of every outcome (fixed)

- **VOID cases** as above: no claim in any direction; report loudly, fix, re-register.
- **V ≤ 0 (or < 0.05 nat) with clean controls:** hardware null — this device, through
  this tomography, did not hold super-classical whole-only structure. The cap and the
  theory are untouched (the theorem never promised any given device could). Recorded
  as loudly as a positive.
- **V > 0 by the conservative rule with clean controls:** a physical five-slot state
  was measured whose every pair view carries more entropy than its whole — a pattern
  impossible for any classical five-slot state, by machine-checked theorem. First
  measured super-classical Logos share. Promotes only the "nature can physically hold
  super-classical share" leg; requires the refuter pass before any stance/page change.

## Simulator gate (must pass before the job)

(a) ideal Aer: V_C5 = 2 ln 2 within bootstrap spread, V_ctrl ≈ −ln 2;
(b) noisy Aer (per-qubit readout error 2%, depolarizing 1e-3 on 1q / 1e-2 on 2q):
V_C5 still passes the full conservative claim rule after readout correction, and
V_ctrl stays ≤ 0. Any failure: fix, re-run gate; hardware only after a clean pass.
