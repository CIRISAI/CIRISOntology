## 0. The finding, up front

**The sign of evolution is right, and it was not put in by hand.** Feed the rent clause the
observed cosmic star-formation history and the record's energy density rises while stars
form, **peaks around z ≈ 0.4–0.7, and then turns over** — which is exactly the behaviour
DESI DR2's preferred quadrant (w₀ > −1, wₐ < 0) describes. No potential, no tuned initial
condition, no thawing scalar. One free parameter, the decay rate λ.

Three things are wrong with it, and one of them is fatal to the *mechanism* as opposed to
the *shape*:

1. **The Landauer normalisation fails by 3–5 orders of magnitude when priced
   self-consistently.** Gough's agreement comes from multiplying the *radiation field's*
   bit count (~10⁸⁶) by the *gas's* temperature (~10⁷ K). Those are different degrees of
   freedom. Every pairing that uses one set of degrees of freedom for both N and T falls
   short. **This kill has already fired** (§6, K4).
2. **The shape is too stiff where the data are sharpest.** The model traces a
   one-parameter line in (w₀, wₐ) with slope −1.22, while the data's degeneracy runs at
   about −3. It passes through DESI+CMB's point estimate (0.2σ) but sits 2.7σ from
   DESI+CMB+DESY5 and 3.8σ from DESI+CMB+Pantheon+ — the combinations carrying DESI's
   *highest* significances.
3. **λ ≈ 1.7 H₀ is an unexplained coincidence.** The record's decay rate has to land within
   a factor of two of today's expansion rate. The cosmic coincidence problem is not solved
   here; it is relocated into λ.

Recommendation: **do not promote `precedent-is-bits`.** The growth law is now derived and
the shape result is genuinely interesting, but the promote condition asked for a match, and
what we have is a partial shape match resting on a mechanism whose magnitude is off by
orders of magnitude. The honest move is to record the derivation, record that K4 fired, and
keep the wager at wager-tier with the kill conditions in §6 pre-registered for DR3.

## 1. The model, assumption by assumption

Every assumption is numbered. Free parameters are marked **[FREE]**; inputs that are fixed
from published data are marked **[FIXED]**; assumptions that are *imported rather than
derived from the rent clause* are marked **[IMPORTED]**.

**A1 — Background.** Flat FRW with radiation, matter, and the record component.
Ω_r = 9.24×10⁻⁵ **[FIXED]**; Ω_m **[FREE]** in the fits, 0.315 in the illustrative tables;
Ω_rec,0 = 1 − Ω_m − Ω_r by flatness.

**A2 — Star-formation history [FIXED].** Madau & Dickinson 2014 (arXiv:1403.0007) eq. 15,
verbatim from the paper's own text:

```
psi(z) = 0.015 (1+z)^2.7 / (1 + [(1+z)/2.9]^5.6)   Msun / yr / Mpc^3   (Salpeter IMF)
```

ψ(0) = 0.01496, ψ(2) = 0.13186, peak at z = 1.863 with ψ_peak/ψ_0 = 8.89. Robustness against
the Madau & Fragos 2017 update is checked in §5.

**A2b — Writing starts at z = 20 [FIXED].** MD14 is calibrated to z ≈ 8; beyond that the
falling branch is extrapolated. §5 shows the result is insensitive to this (z_start = 10 and
30 give identical answers to three decimals).

**A3 — Bit yield [FREE, but cancels].** Y bits of record written per solar mass of stars
formed, constant.

**A4 — Landauer price [FREE, but cancels].** Each bit costs k_B T ln2, T constant (variant A).

**A5 — Non-dilution [IMPORTED — this is the load-bearing import].** The record's energy
density tracks the *comoving* stock; it is not diluted by expansion. This is the
holographic-type step Gough takes, and **the rent clause does not supply it.** Variant D in
§4 is the control with A5 dropped.

**A6 — The rent clause [DERIVED FROM THE REPO].** This is the cosmological reading of
`Core/Maintenance.lean`'s `step` / `unpaid` / `rent_holds` / `underpaid_shrinks` /
`unpaid_decays`: entries decay at rate λ unless upkeep is paid.

```
dn/dt = Y psi(t)  -  lambda n(t)
```

with n the record stock. Paying the decay holds an entry steady; underpaying strictly loses;
paying nothing tends to zero — the same three cases, now with a source.

**A7 — Effective equation of state [DEFINITION].**

```
w(a) = -1 - (1/3) dln(rho_rec)/dln(a)
```

i.e. the w a covariantly conserved fluid would need in order to reproduce the same ρ(a). The
ledger has a source, so it is *not* covariantly conserved on its own; this is an effective,
not a fundamental, equation of state. Gough uses the same definition. Stated so it cannot be
laundered later.

**A8 — Normalisation [FIXED BY FIAT].** α set by ρ_rec(a = 1) = Ω_rec,0 ρ_crit,0. The record
is *required* to supply all of today's dark energy. This is not a prediction.

**A9 — λ [FREE].** The record's decay rate, quoted in units of H₀. **The model's only
genuinely free parameter.**

### Why α, Y and T drop out

Because ρ ∝ n, the ratio ε/ρ = ψ/n is independent of the normalisation, so

```
w(a) = -1 + (lambda - psi/n) / (3H)
```

contains **no free normalisation at all**. Further, writing τ = H₀t gives dn/dτ = ψ − (λ/H₀)n
and dτ/dln a = 1/E(a), so H₀ cancels too: the shape ρ̂_rec(a) depends only on (Ω_m, λ/H₀).

That is the useful structural fact of this study. **The bit yield, the Landauer temperature,
and the absolute normalisation cannot be tuned to fix the shape.** Given the SFH, w(z) is a
one-parameter family. The magnitude question (§6, K4) is therefore completely separable from
the shape question — and they get different verdicts.

The equation is solved self-consistently: ρ̂_rec feeds back into H(a), which sets t(a), which
sets ρ̂_rec, iterated to 10⁻¹² in ln ρ̂. The finite-difference w and the closed-form w agree
to 3.9×10⁻⁴ over a > 0.2.

## 6. Pre-registration stub for DESI DR3

Fixed and frozen *now*, before DR3: A1–A9 as stated; MD14 eq. 15 with the published
coefficients; the ledger equation dn/dt = Yψ − λn; the effective-w definition
w = −1 − (1/3) dlnρ/dln a; a single free model parameter λ with uniform prior λ/H₀ ∈ [0, 6],
alongside the standard nuisances (Ω_m, c/H₀r_d, and a SN absolute-magnitude offset when SNe
are included). Current DR2 posterior: λ = 1.67 H₀, 68% interval [1.33, 2.02] H₀.

**Primary test.** Fit the rent-ledger directly to DR3 BAO + CMB + SNe and compare to ΛCDM and
to w₀wₐCDM by Δχ². Do *not* test in CPL space — §3 shows the CPL projection carries a ~0.2
systematic in wₐ from the weighting choice alone, comparable to the measurement error.

**Survives if:** Δχ²(ledger − ΛCDM) ≤ −4 **and** Δχ²(ledger − w₀wₐCDM) ≤ +2, i.e. the
one-parameter ledger stays within 2 of the two-parameter fit while still beating ΛCDM.

**Kills, each separable — each takes down its own claim and nothing beneath it:**

- **K1 — no ΛCDM limit.** If DR3 pulls dark energy back toward w = −1 (the ΛCDM point within
  1σ, and the w₀wₐCDM preference over ΛCDM falling below 2σ), the ledger dies outright.
  There is no λ that yields (w₀, wₐ) = (−1, 0); λ → 0 gives (−0.97, −1.01). This is the
  cleanest kill and it is unusual to be able to state one this sharp.
- **K2 — the shape relation.** The ledger requires wₐ = −1.223 w₀ − 2.201, widened to ±0.25
  to cover the CPL-projection systematic. If DR3's posterior excludes that line at > 3σ, the
  ledger *form* is wrong even if evolving dark energy survives. **This kill is already half
  fired**: DR2+Pantheon+ sits 2.7σ off in wₐ, DR2+DESY5 2.0σ off. If DR3's SNe combinations
  hold their current central values with DR3-level errors, K2 fires.
- **K3 — no early dark energy.** The ledger is identically zero before star formation:
  Ω_rec(z=10) ≈ 1.2×10⁻³, Ω_rec(z>20) = 0 exactly, r_d untouched. If DR3+CMB requires
  ρ_DE(z > 10) > 1% of ρ_DE,0, dead.
- **K4 — the Landauer normalisation. ALREADY FIRED, see below.**
- **K5 — λ stability.** If DR3's best-fit λ is inconsistent with DR2's [1.33, 2.02] H₀ at
  > 3σ, the model is being fitted rather than tested.

   the model is in the most tension. Until Pantheon+/Union3/DESY5 are in the fit, §4's 1.5σ is
   the model's best case, not its score.
2. **A real CMB likelihood**, or at minimum the standard compressed distance priors (R, l_A,
   ω_b) with their correlation matrix, replacing the single-point CMB-lite.
3. **A derivation of λ, or an admission that it is a fitted constant.** λ ≈ 1.7 H₀ is
   currently a coincidence with a factor-of-two of the expansion rate. Either the rent clause
   predicts it, or the model has traded one coincidence problem for another and should say so.
4. **A defensible bit accounting** that survives K4 — i.e. a set of degrees of freedom whose
   count *and* temperature jointly reach ρ_DE. Absent that, the shape result is a curiosity
   about the SFH's turnover, not evidence for the record.
