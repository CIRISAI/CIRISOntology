## Why the 345 cannot move

Every one of them is a statement about `Real`. Mathlib builds `Real` as a quotient of
Cauchy sequences (`Quot.sound`) and gives it a `LinearOrder` whose decidability is
`Classical.dec` (`Classical.choice`). The floor is visible in Mathlib itself:
`#print axioms Real.mul_pos` already reports all three. No proof stated over `Real`,
however elementary, can be axiom-free, so this is a property of the ambient library
and not of anything in this repository. The same holds for the handful whose
statements hide `Real` behind a definition (`IsProb`, `IsKernel`, `samePairs`,
`signSymmetric`) — all of those are `Real`-valued predicates.

## Audit wiring

`Audit/AxiomAudit.lean` gained, this pass:

- **336 assert lines** (168 theorems x `assert_no_sorry` + `assert_standard_axioms`)
  covering the six modules that had never been named there (`WrongKind`, `Generator`,
  `Instrument`, `Confront`, `Scan`, `Stack`) plus the partly-covered analytic modules
  (`Share`, `ShareQuantum`, `EntropyIneq`, `BellCeiling`, `HammingCap`, `Intensive`).
- **The enumeration gate, section (2b)**: a `run_cmd` that asks the ENVIRONMENT for
  every theorem the library declares instead of reading a list, and applies both
  tests to each. It covers private and compiler-generated declarations too. One
  shared traversal, so it costs ~5s where the equivalent 850 `collectAxioms` calls
  cost ~3m20s. This is the part that cannot go stale.
- **27 exact `#guard_msgs` pins** for the newly axiom-free theorems, plus 3 stale
  pins corrected — `parityRepair_not_percell`, `majorityRepair_not_percell` and
  `bool_const_of_eq` were pinned at `[propext]` and are now free. Without a pin a
  later `simp` would silently restore the axiom and no other gate would notice.

Cost: the audit file elaborates in roughly 10 minutes (9m41s measured with the new
assert lines in place, before the gate and the pins were added). The hand lists
dominate that — `collectAxioms` re-walks the whole transitive closure on every call,
about 0.65s each. The enumeration gate subsumes sections (1) and (2) at 5 seconds
total, so trimming the lists would take the audit under a minute. That is a call for
the steward, not for this pass; CI sets no job timeout, so 10 minutes does not break
anything today.
