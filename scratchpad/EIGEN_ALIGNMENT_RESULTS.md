## 2. The forward prediction, scored

§9.1 staked this on 2026-08-18, before any embedding of any corpus text existed, with the 0.25
PASS floor deliberately **not** lowered:

> *Median Ω(11) on the primary arm will land in [0.02, 0.08] — and P1a will not meet its 0.25
> PASS floor.*

**Measured: 0.0600. The prediction is CONFIRMED.**

Per §9.1's own fixed meaning, and per Ruling 1 which scores it "regardless of verdict", the
credit goes to **the instrument-and-power model, under house rule 6 — and explicitly not to the
taxonomy.** What was predicted in advance, and correctly, is that a change-embedding cloud built
from 247 documents at 1024 dimensions cannot resolve an eleven-direction coordinate system,
whatever coordinate system is true. The prediction was derived from a no-embedding TF-IDF power
proxy (changed span → kind at 0.170 against chance 0.083) cross-referenced with §8.2's synthetic
gauge, and it named the landing zone to within a factor well under two.

**The honest limit on that credit, stated here rather than left to a reader:** the predicted band
[0.02, 0.08] **contains the N1 null median of 0.0598**. The prediction therefore could not have
distinguished a weak real alignment from no alignment at all — it predicted that the instrument
would read approximately nothing, and the instrument read approximately nothing, to within
0.0002 of its own null. That is a confirmed advance prediction about reach, and it is not a
measurement of signal. §9.1's own trailing clause "and P1a reads WEAK" is superseded by Ruling 1:
strength is graded only if detected, and detection failed.

Also confirmed in the same direction, and worth recording because it was the uncomfortable half
of §4's table: the unchanged `before` document carries **more** recoverable kind structure than
the change does. See §3.

## 3. K1c — the night's most informative number

The placebo-P1 control asks whether the Δ construction reads *change* or reads *topic*. It runs
the identical end-to-end pipeline on the before-cloud and pairs the two within each of the 200
splits.

| | Ω(11) | rank(B) |
|---|---|---|
| **Δ cloud** (the change vector — the primary) | **0.0600** | 11 |
| **before cloud** (the unchanged documents) | **0.1337** | 11 |
| paired difference (Δ − before) | **−0.0733** | |
| paired permutation p (500 sign-flips of the Δ/before assignment within split) | **1.0** | |
| fraction of the 200 splits where Δ > before | **0.00** | |

**K1c has FIRED, and it fired in the strongest possible direction: not narrowly, not
ambiguously, but in 0 of 200 splits.** The Δ construction — the whole point of the design,
chosen over the templated route precisely because it is leakage-free with respect to
author-written change descriptions — reads **less than half** the kind structure that the raw,
unchanged documents read.

Its blast radius as staked (§16-K1c): it takes down P1a's *interpretation*; the statistic is
reading topic, not change. Since K1 already fired, there is no surviving alignment for K1c to
reinterpret. Its value is diagnostic, and the diagnosis is clean.

**Why, mechanically.** The two documents in every pair are nearly identical by construction.
Median cos(ê(before), ê(after)) across all 247 items is **0.9963** (`main_primary.json`,
`V1.global_median_cos`) — an angle of about 4.9°. The per-class un-normalized change magnitudes
run from **0.042** (Priorities/axiotic) to **0.227** (Identity/ontological). Δ is therefore a
small residual of two large, nearly parallel vectors, and unit-normalizing it — a step §4
defends, correctly, as removing edit magnitude — rescales that residual to length 1 without
adding any information to it. The before-cloud has the entire document to work with; the Δ cloud
has a few percent of it. The order of the two Ω values follows.

This is the number that most constrains a successor instrument, and it is why the successor
question is a design question and not a re-run.

**K1b (the privilege kill) has FIRED** on its stated condition: Ω_taxonomy ≤ Ω_nontaxonomy. Its

comparison is not rank-matched and the rival had the easier job. The **k-means comparator is
rank-matched at 11**, and k-means still reads higher than the taxonomy.

The scope limit, which the prereg did not require but which the reader needs: that validation
sits at scale 4.0, where the gauge's own Ω(11) is **0.299** — five times the signal actually
measured. At the measured Ω of 0.060, the gauge brackets between its scale-1.5 rung (Ω = 0.043,

separated). **At the signal level this corpus actually delivered, the gauge does not demonstrate
that η can tell the two worlds apart.** That is a statement about scope, not a retraction of the
Ruling-3 verdict, which was staked at the anchor and stands as recorded. It is also the same
conclusion V5 reached from the data side.

### Sensitivity arms — reported, and none of them is support

| arm | Ω(11) | p_N1 | note |

**The span-only arm is the one number in this run that clears p < 0.01, and it is not support.**
It reads Ω(11) = 0.152 at p_N1 = 0.001996 — which is the **minimum reportable p** at 500
permutations, i.e. *zero* of 500 label permutations matched it. Four reasons it is reported and
not banked, all pre-registered:

1. **§17's first bullet is explicit**: "Any Ω computed against a null that is not the label
   permutation (N1) **and** the span-stratified permutation (N1b)" does not count as support.
   **The span-only arm was run against N1 only. N1b was not run on it.**
2. N1b is the null that exists **specifically to price span**. §2.1b measured changed-span length
   varying **87×** across kinds (median 1.5 chars for Circumstances to 130.5 for Process),
   Kruskal–Wallis p = 7.6e-16. The span-only arm is the arm in which that confound is
   *maximal*, because the span **is** the signal, and it is the one arm missing the null built to
   control it.
3. The arm is residualized on the same nuisance matrix as the primary — including
   log₁₀(1 + span chars), domain and batch one-hots — but §2.1b's whole finding was that one
   rank-1 linear term is insufficient against an 87× nonlinear dynamic range. That is why N1b was

## 11. Deviations

Every deviation is labelled with its cause. **The arms are deterministic functions of the on-disk
embedding cache under a pinned seed**, which is what makes the restarts harmless, and the argument
is spelled out below rather than asserted.

**D-1 — the run died and was restarted three times (session deaths).** The measurement stage
(`run_main.py`) was launched detached (`setsid nohup`, PID to `out/main.pid`, log to
`out/main.log`), per the protocol in `scratchpad/eigen/RESUME.md`: long compute runs detached with
disk logs, so a session death kills the narration and not the computation. The final complete
pass ran 3,632.7 s and is recorded end to end in `out/main.log`.

**D-2 — `gesvd` fallback added mid-run.** LAPACK's `gesdd` driver failed to converge on a valid
matrix inside the split loop. A fallback to the slower, more robust `gesvd` driver was added at
`scratchpad/eigen/pipeline.py:79-82` and the run restarted. This changes the numerical *route* to
the same SVD, not the SVD.

**D-3 — `atexit` checkpoint added.** After a session death lost an in-progress result dictionary,
an exit handler was registered at `scratchpad/eigen/run_main.py:15-17` to dump the partial results
to `out/main_checkpoint.json`. Pure instrumentation; it writes a file and touches no statistic.
(The final checkpoint file is empty, `{}`, because the completed run wrote its real output first.)

**D-4 — zero-delta span guard added, after a NaN crash.** In the span-only arm, two items produced
*identical* span embeddings on both sides, so the change vector had norm 0, and normalizing 0/0
produced NaN and killed the arm. The guard at `scratchpad/eigen/run_main.py:53-58` keeps those rows
as explicit zeros and **reports the count** rather than silently normalizing — the count appears in
`out/main.log`: "`[span] 2 zero-delta span rows kept as zeros`". Two rows of 247.

**D-5 — client-side 1,500-character cap on the RATCHET placebo rationales.** §3 budgeted truncation
against a **2,048-character** exposure and predicted 0.65%. That was the wrong unit: the DeepInfra
endpoint rejects over-length input with **HTTP 400 rather than truncating it**, at a 512-**token**
context. The first embedding pass crashed on the rationales (`out/embed.log`: "You passed 513 input
tokens... the model's context length is only 512"). Truncation was moved client-side at a 1,500-char
cap in `run_ratchet_embed.py`, and the truncated fraction was **measured** and checked against V7's
2% threshold: **25 of 2,148 = 1.16%, below threshold, V7 does not fire.** Scope: the RATCHET placebo
only. **Artifact corpora are unaffected** — their longest single text is 1,273 characters, so nothing
in Corpus A, A-held, BAB or B was truncated.

**D-6 — cache path.** §3 named `scratchpad/eigen_cache_<model-slug>.jsonl`; the cache was written to
`scratchpad/eigen/cache/eigen_cache_<model-slug>.jsonl`. Housekeeping only; the keying
(`sha256(model || "\x00" || text)`) is as specified and the sha256s are recorded in §12.

### Why the restarts do not contaminate the result

1. **`run_main.py` makes zero API calls on a warm cache.** It reads embedding vectors from the
   on-disk cache; the embedding stage is separate (`run_embed.py`) and had already completed. Total
   spend across the entire campaign is $0.0048, all of it in the embedding stage.
2. **Splits and permutations are pinned.** `NSPLIT, NPERM, SEED = 200, 500, 20260819` are fixed at
   module scope, and every arm draws from an explicitly derived seed (`SEED`, `SEED+1`, `SEED+5`,
   `SEED+77`, and so on). A restart regenerates the identical 200 stratified splits and the
   identical 500 label permutations.
3. **Therefore each restart recomputes the same deterministic function of the same bytes.** There
   is no accumulation across restarts, no resumption of partial state into a final number, and no
   opportunity for a restart to select a friendlier draw. D-2's `gesvd` fallback is the only patch
   that touches numerics, and it substitutes one exact-arithmetic-equivalent SVD driver for another
   on matrices where the first refused to converge at all.
4. **No patch was made after seeing a verdict-bearing number.** D-2 and D-4 are crash fixes on arms
   that had produced no output; D-3 and D-5 are instrumentation and an API-limit workaround. The
   last edit to `run_main.py` is timestamped 17:19 and the complete run that produced
   `out/main_primary.json` ran 17:20–18:20.

## 13. What this closes, and what it does not

### Closed

**This instrument, and its staked claim — not the geometry question.** The proposition AS
STAKED was: *alignment as read by the Δ = e(after) − e(before) construction.* On 247 authored,
balanced, kind-labelled change pairs, with two embedder families, against two permutation nulls
and two rival partitions, that claim failed every conjunct: 0.0600 against a null of 0.0598;
K1 fired; K1b fired; K1c fired; the Babel topic control returned 0/10; both P2 legs went VOID.
The eigen-bridge paragraph in `LEAN2_CONFRONTATION.md`, as formulated for this instrument, is
withdrawn.

**But K1c cuts both ways, and the steward's correction governs the reading**: an instrument
that reads LESS kind-structure than the unchanged documents it was built from (0 of 200
splits) was not reading changes at all. A meter that fails its own placebo renders no
world-verdict in either direction. **What this run closes is the instrument. It produced no
geometry-null and no geometry-support.**

**The forward prediction is banked, under rule 6, for the power model and not for the taxonomy.**
[0.02, 0.08] staked before any embedding existed; 0.0600 measured. With the honest caveat of §2:
the band contained the null, so the prediction was confirmed but not discriminating.

**Two design questions are answered for any successor.** First, the difference-vector construction
reads *less* kind structure than the raw documents it is built from (K1c, 0 of 200 splits), and the
reason is measured: cos(before, after) = 0.996, so Δ is a small residual of two nearly parallel
vectors. Second, the gauge fixed the instrument's reach in advance — σ_R = 0.918 at n = 124 — and
the reach was the binding constraint, not the analysis.

### Not closed, and explicitly untouched

**The label-level 11+1 results stand entirely untouched.** `basePlane_card = 11`,
`record_not_site_generated` and `repairable_does_not_factor` in `Core/Generator.lean` and
`Core/WrongKind.lean` are theorems and are unaffected by any measurement. The PLANE study's
measured label flatness — coordinate-flat at p < 0.01 across 5,994 panel judgments, κ = 0.687, zero
modal no-fits on 170 wild changes — is a different instrument asking a different question, and §1
declared before the run that this experiment could disagree with all three without retiring any of
them. It disagreed. They stand.

**Record's relation-typing is neither supported nor refuted.** Both P2 legs are VOID — V5 (3 of 11
base kinds detectable, 6 required) and V6 (lever ratio 0.969 against a 1.1 threshold). K3 did not
fire, and on a void instrument that is worth nothing. This is unfalsifiable-at-null and is recorded
as such, not as a survival.

**The geometry question itself is OPEN-UNTESTED.** No valid instrument has read it yet: v1
failed its own placebo, and the successor's calibration numbers on this burned,
batch-confounded corpus are not support either — the first world-reading, null or
supportive, will be the frozen v2 protocol on the interleaved replication corpus.

**The rank question was not asked.** P1b is UNDECIDED by its own rules, because there was no
alignment to count. "Not 7, not 13" was retracted in advance by the gauge, automatically, per
Ruling 4 — the sharpest-sounding part of the prediction was never tested, exactly as §19-D7 warned
it might not be.

**The RATCHET leg remains REPORTED-BUT-UNPINNED**, per §21, and becomes pinnable the day the
original analysis script or signal list surfaces. Nothing here changes that, and no correction is
owed.

**Corpus authorship remains the deepest unaddressed weakness** (§19-D6). Corpus A was written by
us with the taxonomy in hand, in two batches split 6/6 by kind. Ruling 2 already committed to an
interleaved corpus rebuild — every authoring batch containing all 12 kinds — as the replication
corpus, "scheduled after the primary run reports, whatever its verdict short of VOID". That
commitment stands and this verdict does not discharge it.
