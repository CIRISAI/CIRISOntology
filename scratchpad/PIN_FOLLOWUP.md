# Pin follow-up: mechanisms + prior art for the six blind matches (2026-08-20) — verbatim

Six pins searched — mechanism and prior art each. Findings below.

---

## 1. Facts → the einselected pointer sector

**Mechanism.** The privileged sector is not postulated; it is selected by the *predictability sieve*: pointer observables are those that commute (or nearly commute) with the system–environment interaction Hamiltonian, so they survive monitoring undisturbed. Quantum Darwinism supplies the second step — *only* those same pointer observables can leave multiple redundant imprints in the environment, because the complementary information is at best held in a single shared copy and is therefore effectively inaccessible ([Zurek, quant-ph/0105127](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0105127); [Ollivier–Poulin–Zurek, PRA 72:042113](https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevA.72.042113)). Objectivity is then *defined* by many observers reading the same record off disjoint environment fragments without perturbing the system.

**The complication, searched and real.** The pointer basis is **not universal**. It is fixed by the interaction Hamiltonian: in the quantum-measurement limit it is the eigenbasis of the coupling operator, in the quantum limit of decoherence it is energy eigenstates, and when the terms do not commute "it is not obvious what decides the preferred basis" ([arXiv:1911.06282](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1911.06282)). Redundancy is further degraded by non-Markovian memory effects ([Sci. Rep. 6:19607](https://www.nature.com/articles/srep19607)) and by correlated/structured environments. So "everything decoheres INTO pointer states" holds only *relative to a monitoring channel*.

**Nearest prior art — Richard Healey's pragmatist QM.** "Decoherence, not measurement, licenses application of Born's rule," and what it licenses is the assertion of *non-quantum magnitude claims* — sentences of the form "the value of A on s lies in Δ" ([arXiv:1008.3896](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1008.3896); [Observation and Quantum Objectivity](https://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/9691/4/QuantumObjectivityFinal.pdf)). That is exactly "decoherence is what converts a quantum description into a fact-shaped assertible claim," and Healey uses quantum Darwinism to get differently-situated agents to agree on the objective content of magnitude statements. **Closeness: neighbour** (same mechanism doing the same job — decoherence underwrites assertion — but no taxonomy, and no claim that description converts *everything*). Second: Chris Fields treats decoherence as "a purely informational process, a model-theoretic or semantic mapping from quantum states to classical information" ([chrisfieldsresearch.com](https://www.chrisfieldsresearch.com/)) — **same-family**.

**Verdict: SUPPORT, with one word to change.** The correspondence survives and gains an independently-derived prior-art anchor. But the tabled wording "*the* einselected pointer sector" must become "*an* einselected pointer sector, selected by the monitoring channel." Note that this makes the physics side *more* like us, not less: the sector you convert into depends on what is doing the describing, which is the site-relativity the taxonomy already carries.

## 2. Rules → the constraint (Dirac; laws-as-constraints)

**Mechanism.** Weak equality (`F ≈ G`, equality only on the constraint surface) versus strong equality (`F = G`, throughout phase space) is not bookkeeping: you may not substitute a weak equality into a Poisson bracket before the bracket is computed, because the bracket sees off-surface behaviour. The first/second-class split is itself computed from brackets among the constraints, and it *determines what the constraint does* — first-class constraints generate gauge transformations and mark redundancy; second-class constraints generate nothing and are eliminated by passing to the Dirac bracket, in which they become strong identities ([First class constraint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_class_constraint); [arXiv:2201.06558](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.06558)). Treat strength as external modulation and you lose the degree-of-freedom count and the gauge/restriction distinction.

**Prior art.** Adlam, *Laws of Nature as Constraints* (Found. Phys. 52:28, 2022) characterizes lawhood in terms of constraints "understood as a form of modal structure," built to accommodate non-local, atemporal, all-at-once and retrocausal laws ([arXiv:2109.13836](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13836)). Chen & Goldstein's Minimal Primitivism: laws "govern by constraining the physical possibilities," with no fundamental direction of time — accommodating least action, the Past Hypothesis and the Einstein equation as laws ([arXiv:2109.09226](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.09226)). Meacham 2023 is the third. **Closeness: same-family** — they make constraints the ontology of law; we map a taxonomy member onto the constraint object. Neither carries anything like weak/strong equality.

**The break.** The deontic search comes back with a named structural disanalogy: "a main difference between norms and other constraints lies in the fact that norms typically allow for the possibility of violation" ([SEP, Deontic Logic](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/logic-deontic/)). Our Rules kind is violable and its strength is *bindingness* (must vs should). Dirac's strength is *surface-versus-everywhere* / bracket class. These are two unrelated senses of the word. The meta-claim (strength is not detachable from content) is genuinely true on both sides, but by different mechanisms, and the searched literature offers no bridge — the prescriptive/descriptive debate about laws is old and unresolved and does not supply one.

**Verdict: COMPLICATE — demote the sharp detail.** Keep `Rules ↔ constraint` as an ontology pin (well-populated, three independent defenders). **Drop** the claim that Dirac weak/strong equality is "a named physics face" for our strength-is-content-for-rules result. As tabled it is a pun on "strength," and it is the weakest line in the table.

## 3. Identity → the stipulative definition / SI exact-by-fiat constants

**Mechanism.** The standard theory of definitions requires *eliminability* (every formula of the extended language is provably equivalent to one of the base language) and *non-creativity/conservativeness* (nothing new in the base language becomes provable). Shoenfield: adding an explicit definition always yields a non-creative extension in which the new constant is eliminable ([SEP, Definitions](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/definitions/); [Conservative extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservative_extension)). A proper definition therefore adds **nothing** — the zero-depths signature, independently derived. Credit correction worth carrying: the folklore attribution of these criteria to Leśniewski is a documented mistake; they come from the Lvov–Warsaw school more broadly ([Urbaniak & Hämäri, HPL 33:2](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/abs/10.1080/01445340.2011.583771)).

**The metrology side does not read the SI as purely conservative.** Eran Tal's central result is that *unit definitions do not completely fix the referents of unit terms*; idealized models do the semantic work of coordinating a theoretical definition with its multiple concrete realizations — the "problem of coordination" ([Tal, *The Epistemology of Measurement*](https://philpapers.org/rec/TALTEO-3)). Fixing *h* to an exact number does not tell you what a kilogram is; the *mise en pratique* (Kibble balance, XRCD sphere) carries empirical content and uncertainty. Meanwhile the redefinition itself "did not alter the physical constants themselves but altered only the conventions by which units are established."

**Prior art.** Reichenbach and Carnap's **coordinative definitions** — a priori, definition-like statements linking theoretical terms to empirical procedures, the canonical example being "a measuring rod retains its length when transported" ([SEP, Measurement in Science](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/measurement-science/)). This is old, named ground for "unit-fixing is stipulation," **same-family**. Tal's coordination problem is **neighbour** to our Identity/Premises split and is the sharper instrument. Searle's status-function declaration ("X counts as Y in C," [Construction of Social Reality](http://ontology.buffalo.edu/smith/articles/searle.pdf)) is the exact speech-act shape of a unit declaration — and repeated searching found **no** paper applying speech-act theory to metrology. That adjacency is unclaimed.

**Verdict: SUPPORT, and it adjudicates the si2019 collision.** The mechanism (eliminability + non-creativity ⇒ adds nothing) is a clean independent derivation of zero-depths. And the metrology literature independently supplies exactly the split the file guessed: the **declared constant** is conservative and eliminable (Identity), the **coordination to realizations** is neither (the Premises face — later work composes over it and inherits its uncertainty). Confront.lean's `si2019` entry should split, and the warrant is Tal's coordination problem, not our own reasoning.

## 4. Manner → the null/degenerate direction — the fusion caveat

**The caveat as tabled is false.** Physics has several objects argued to be both gauge-like and content-bearing, and it is a large live literature.

**Mechanism.** Three routes. (a) *Direct empirical significance*: Greaves & Wallace argue gauge transformations are empirically significant when applied to a subsystem with the environment held fixed ([Empirical consequences of symmetries](https://users.ox.ac.uk/~mert2255/papers/symmetries.pdf)); Teh supports this via the constrained-Hamiltonian formalism ([Galileo's Gauge](https://philpapers.org/rec/TEHGGU)); Healey resists; Gomes reframes the significance as **holism** — it lives in the subsystem/complement relation, not the subsystem ([Holism as the empirical significance of symmetries](https://philpapers.org/rec/GOMHAT)); Murgueitio Ramírez presses a puzzle against it. (b) *Large gauge / asymptotic symmetries*: BMS supertranslations and large U(1) transformations act nontrivially at the boundary, carry conserved charges, and give soft hair. (c) *Edge modes*: the current reading is that edge modes are **dynamical reference frames that dress subregion variables**, non-trivial corner symmetries arise only for *extrinsic* frames, and "the relationship between asymptotia and finite distance is frame-dependent" ([arXiv:2412.14548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.14548)). The opposing camp is real too: soft variables can be canonically decoupled from hard dynamics, and soft-hairy solutions are indistinguishable in Unruh/Hawking effects ([Soft hair as a soft wig](https://iopscience.iop.org/article/10.1088/1361-6382/aa8be2)).

The condition under which a null direction acquires content is **the presence of a boundary or a subsystem cut**. That is a sharper statement than we had, and it is our own non-factoring shape wearing physics clothes: the direction is empty for the whole and content-bearing for the parts.

**Closeness: same-family** — the DES literature asks our exact question (when does a null direction carry content?) about gauge orbits rather than presentation-markers. Our specific correspondence is unclaimed.

**Verdict: SUPPORT the pin, BREAK the caveat.** The matcher's "physics has no object carrying content AND presentation" is refuted and must be struck. Net effect is a *stronger and more falsifiable* pin: it now predicts Manner-content appears exactly at subsystem boundaries, and that the content is holistic/frame-dependent rather than carried by the marker alone. That is a testable rewrite, not a cosmetic one.

## 5. Twins → the graphene Dirac point

**Mechanism.** The Dirac-point degeneracy is protected by the *combination* of time-reversal T and inversion P: a mass term is forbidden in any two-band model invariant under both, so a gap requires breaking at least one ([graphene/hBN gaps, Nature Physics](https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys3856)). The two masses break *different* symmetries. The **Semenoff mass** is an on-site staggered potential ±M breaking sublattice equivalence (inversion), T intact → trivial band insulator, obtainable passively from a substrate. The **Haldane mass** is a staggered flux with zero net flux through the hexagon, inducing an Aharonov–Bohm phase φ, breaking T with P intact → Chern insulator with quantized anomalous Hall response, requiring magnetic order or a Floquet drive. "Either a non-zero Semenoff mass M or Aharonov–Bohm phase φ lift the degeneracy, and the fermion masses in the two inequivalent valleys turn out differently." So the tabled transferable mechanism — twins split by **which sector couples to them** — is confirmed, and refined: split by *which symmetry the coupling sector breaks*, with topological character and experimental hardness both downstream of that.

**A checkable convergence, free.** The general result: **any** two-fold degeneracy, accidental or not, is protected by a hidden antiunitary symmetry whose square is −1 — "accidental" only means the protecting symmetry has not been found yet ([Front. Phys. 13:130301](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11467-017-0712-8)). Prediction for us: if our twins are genuine twins there must be an automorphism protecting the swap. `Core/Symmetry.lean` already computes exactly this (`aut_with_stack` = 4, "rigid up to two twin swaps"). That is an independent convergence testable inside our own Lean, and I'd flag it as the cheapest follow-up in this batch.

**Prior art — better than graphene: the eightfold way.** Hadrons are degenerate multiplets under exact SU(3), and the observed spectrum is generated by a *graded hierarchy* of breaking terms: the medium-strong interaction breaks SU(3) leaving isospin and hypercharge good, electromagnetism then splits isospin multiplets, the weak interaction last ([Gell-Mann, *The Eightfold Way*](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/7degs-kzv67); [Departures from the Eightfold Way, Phys. Rev. 134:B671](https://journals.aps.org/pr/abstract/10.1103/PhysRev.134.B671)). Classification just *is* "list the degeneracies, then list which sector lifts each and how hard" — and Gell-Mann–Okubo is a strength-graded lifting formula. **Closeness: same-family**, and it is the general classification tool the brief asked whether anyone had built. It exists, it is sixty years old, and it is the canonical success case for this exact method.

**Verdict: SUPPORT, and upgrade the avatar.** Graphene is a good concrete instance; the eightfold way is the better prior art and the better template, because it supplies a *hierarchy* of lifting strengths rather than a single split. The table's "two different lifting mechanisms of different hardness" is right but under-specified — say "different broken symmetry (P vs T), hence different topology, hence different hardness."

## 6. Stack → Past Hypothesis → law → state → error bar

**Mechanism.** The Mentaculus is explicitly a three-part package: (a) microscopic dynamical laws, (b) the Past Hypothesis — the low-entropy macrostate of the universe shortly after the Big Bang, (c) a uniform Lebesgue measure over microstates realizing that macrostate; together these assign probabilities to every proposition about the world's history ([Loewer, *The Mentaculus Vision*](https://sites.rutgers.edu/barry-loewer/wp-content/uploads/sites/195/2019/06/Loewer-The-Mentaculus-Vision.pdf); [SEP, Philosophy of Statistical Mechanics](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/statphys-statmech/)). The forcing argument: the dynamics alone is time-symmetric and cannot deliver either the thermodynamic arrow or the asymmetry of records; (b) and (c) cost little simplicity and buy enormous informativeness, which is the Best-System case for counting them as law. **The records argument is Albert's own and is verbatim our "accessible only through records"**: the initial macrostate is "the mother of all ready conditions," a record counts as a record only relative to a ready state, and conditioning on the PH stops the regress and makes present records reliable evidence of the past.

**Prior art. Closeness: same-claim** on the lower three rungs. This is not a structural resemblance — it is the same argument shape (frozen given, knowable only through records; applied rule; state-claim), with the same stated reason for the order. The Mentaculus *is* prior art for our stack and should be credited as such.

**Complication: the necessity is contested inside that literature.** Chen argues the Past Hypothesis is itself a candidate **fundamental law**, not a boundary condition ([arXiv:2008.00611](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.00611)) — which would collapse our Premises and Model rungs on the physics side. Others argue for arrows of time **without** any Past Hypothesis ([arXiv:1809.04646](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.04646)) — which would delete the bottom rung. And critics charge circularity: Albert and Loewer "cannot make sense of why this probabilistic structure exists without falling back on the very records they wish to explain."

**Second prior art for the order-forced-by-composition leg**: Curiel, *Kinematics, Dynamics, and the Structure of Physical Theory* ([arXiv:1603.02999](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.02999)) — kinematical constraints are fixed once and for all irrespective of interactions and are a *necessary precondition* for the equations of motion to be well-posed "or even just cogent"; satisfying them "renders meaning to those terms representing a system's physical quantities in the first place, even before one can ask" about dynamics. That is a constitutive grounding order, argued as necessary, and it is a cleaner warrant for our order claim than the Mentaculus is. **Closeness: neighbour.** Nothing found deriving the full four-rung tower (given/law/state/uncertainty) as necessary.

**Verdict: SUPPORT on structure, COMPLICATE on necessity.** And one thing worth recording beyond the verdict: the live dispute on the physics side is *whether the Past Hypothesis is a frozen given or a rule* — which is the same boundary dispute as our si2019 Premises-vs-Identity collision, on an unrelated substrate. That is the second independent appearance of that boundary this week, and it suggests the boundary is a real feature rather than a labelling defect in our corpus.

## Overall

**Strongest, in order.** **Stack** is the strongest of the six and the only one with near-same-claim prior art: the Mentaculus is a defended three-part package with Albert's records argument built in, and Curiel independently supplies a constitutive grounding-order argument; the honest cost is that its *necessity* is contested by Chen and by the no-PH programmes. **Identity** is second and the most useful, because the mechanism search did real work: eliminability + non-creativity is a clean independent derivation of the zero-depths signature, and Tal's coordination problem resolves the si2019 collision in favour of the file's own candidate split (declared constant = Identity, coordination ripple = Premises) — that is an outside adjudication of an internal dispute, which is worth more than a match. **Twins** third: the mechanism is confirmed and sharpened (P-breaking vs T-breaking, not merely "harder/softer"), the eightfold way is the general classification tool the brief asked after and it validates the method, and the hidden-antiunitary-symmetry theorem hands us a prediction checkable against `Core/Symmetry.lean` today. **Manner** fourth, and it is the pin that changed most: the matcher's fusion caveat is simply false — DES, large gauge transformations and edge modes are exactly objects that are gauge-like and content-bearing — and striking the caveat leaves a sharper, more falsifiable pin (content appears at subsystem cuts, and is holistic/frame-dependent).

**Should be dropped or demoted.** **Rules**: keep the ontology pin (Adlam, Chen–Goldstein, Meacham are three independent defenders of laws-as-constraints), but **strike the Dirac weak/strong sharp detail**. It is a pun on two unrelated senses of "strength" — bindingness versus surface-vs-everywhere — and the deontic literature names the structural disanalogy explicitly (norms are violable, physical constraints are not). This is the honest break in the batch. **Facts** needs a one-word repair rather than a demotion: the pointer basis is interaction-dependent, so "the einselected sector" must become "an einselected sector, selected by the monitoring channel," after which Healey's "decoherence licenses assertion of non-quantum magnitude claims" stands as near-same-claim prior art on the mechanism leg.
