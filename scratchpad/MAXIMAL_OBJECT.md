## The object

K11 on the eleven kinds: 55 pairwise channels z_ij = r_ij e^{i phi_ij}. Magnitudes are
locally visible (what mixes, how strongly); phases are NOT locally visible — they are
discovered only by going around loops. 55 edge phases minus 10 removable node conventions
= **45 independent loop phases**; 55 + 45 = 100, exactly the generalized mixing-parameter
count of U(11) after rephasing (n(n-1)/2 angles = 55, (n-1)(n-2)/2 physical phases = 45).
Matrix form: an 11x11 (maximally, unitary) mixing matrix U acting on semantic state
vectors — "the kind in which a consequential act begins may not be the only kind in which
its consequences appear."

## The aggressive sub-hypothesis (most vulnerable, staked as such)

CEG's five operations orient K11 as a regular tournament: 11 x 5 = 55, five
primitive-labelled outward channels and five inward per kind, every pair covered once.
NOTHING currently warrants this; the blinded 55-case exercise must PRODUCE the
tournament. SEPARABLE KILLS, the steward's own: (a) two primitive cases land on the same
hallway; (b) any case lands outside the eleven-dimensional plane; (c) the orientation
exists but is not regular. Any one kills the tournament reading without touching the
interferometer frame; the frame itself dies if loop residuals are consistently zero
(all-phases-removable = the mixing is potential-derived = no holonomy = Record carries no
route memory), which is therefore THE kill for the maximal object.
