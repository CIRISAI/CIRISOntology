# The gauge test for Circumstances — FROZEN 2026-08-20, before any arm is run

Four independent flags say Circumstances is unlike the other ten: uniquely
`assertsContent = false`; uniquely `outOfScope`; confusion crossing block lines to Facts
where its twin Structure leaks to its own block surface; and 85–94% zero polarity, measured
2026-08-20. The reading under test: **Circumstances is the plane's LABELLING FREEDOM —
content-free, gauge-like — and the content-bearing plane is TEN.**

Gauge has an operational meaning and this test uses it: **removing a gauge direction from
the description does not change the physical content.** So removing Circumstances from the
label set should not change how the other ten are read.

## The three arms — the control is the point

Same corpus (`plane_corpus/corpus_full.jsonl`, 248 authored items), same panel convention as
every BASE run (3 model families, modal by plurality, ties excluded), one variable: the
label set offered.

| arm | label set | purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **A** | the full 11 + Record | **the noise floor** — re-run with the same options, so label movement here is pure test–retest resampling |
| **B** | 10 + Record, **Circumstances removed** | the treatment |
| **C** | 10 + Record, **Structure removed** | **the matched control** — Structure is Circumstances's structural twin (same block, same Z2 orbit, n = 20 vs 20). Removing a kind that IS content-bearing costs something; this measures what |

Arm A is what makes B interpretable; arm C is what makes B *meaningful*. Neither is optional.

## Primary statistic

For each arm, over the items whose ORIGINAL 11-way modal was **neither** Circumstances nor
Structure (the untouched population, identical across arms):
**PERTURBATION = the fraction whose modal label differs from the original 11-way modal.**

Let `pA`, `pB`, `pC` be those fractions. Staked, before data:

- **GAUGE CONFIRMED** — `pB − pA` is not significantly above zero (two-proportion test,
  p ≥ 0.05) **AND** `pB − pA < (pC − pA)/2`. Removing Circumstances costs materially less
  than removing its content-bearing twin, and is consistent with resampling noise.
- **NOT GAUGE** — `pB − pA` significantly above zero (p < 0.05) **AND**
  `pB − pA ≥ (pC − pA)/2`. Its removal perturbs the others like a real kind's does. **This
  kills the gauge reading and the ten-plane shape with it.**
- **AMBIGUOUS** — the two conditions split (e.g. B is significantly above A but still far
  below C). Reported as such, no verdict, and the note says which way it leant.
- **UNDERPOWERED / VOID** — if `pC − pA` is itself not significantly above zero, the control
  failed: removing a genuine kind cost nothing measurable, so the instrument cannot resolve
  the question and NO verdict may be read in either direction. This is the outcome that
  protects against declaring gauge from a dead instrument.

## Secondary — where the orphans go

For the removed kind's own items (20 Circumstances in B, 20 Structure in C), report the
destination distribution and its entropy.
- Staked: **Structure's orphans concentrate** (its block surface, Manner, is the predicted
  sink — the block-absorption model). **Circumstances's orphans scatter or pile into Facts**
  without block-coherence, per its measured cross-block leak.
- A gauge direction has no natural home in the content plane; a content kind does. Entropy of
  the destination distribution is the summary number, reported for both.
