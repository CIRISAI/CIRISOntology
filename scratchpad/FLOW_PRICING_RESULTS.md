## VERDICT

**The flow reformulation fails, worse than the stock model it was meant to rescue, and it
fails on four independent legs. Four of the six pre-registered outcomes fire. Outcome (a),
survival, does not.**

The reformulation was supposed to buy something by pricing *maintenance against decay*
rather than *storage*. It buys exactly one factor — `lambda/(3H)` — and that factor is
already measured elsewhere in the same model. The failure is conserved, not removed.

| pre-registered outcome | fires? |

| **(f)** budget / waste-heat kill | **FIRES** — and it is parameter-free |

The single most damaging result is **(f)**, because it needs no bit count and no temperature
at all: the flow picture demands, as dissipated waste heat, `3 rho_DE = 2.05 rho_crit` per

included**, and if the heat lands in the CMB it produces a Compton distortion `y ≈ 9.4e3`

**Recommendation: do not adopt the flow reformulation. Record it as priced and dead.** The
`precedent-is-bits` wager keeps its existing status and its existing fired kill (K4); this
study adds a second, independent fired kill (call it **K6**, §7) and one **correction to the

## 1. The collapse test, run first, as pre-registered

Dividing the flow formula by the stock formula at fixed `(N, T)`:

```
    rho_DE(flow) / rho_DE(stock)  =  lambda / (3H)
```

**The entire content of the reformulation is that one factor.** So the required maintenance
rate is fixed algebraically by the stock model's shortfall:

```
    lambda_req / H0  =  3 * rho_DE / (N kB T ln2)  =  3 x (stock shortfall factor)
```

If `lambda = H` the flow answer is the stock answer over three, and the reformulation
inherits the killed model's failure *worsened by 0.5 dex*. The flow picture helps only if
`lambda >> H`, and it must supply the whole shortfall itself.

### 2.1 The one row that works is a thermodynamic identity, not a calculation

The holographic/de Sitter row reaches the target — and requires **λ/H₀ = 2.0541**, which is
`3 Omega_L` **exactly**. That is pre-registered outcome **(d)**: `lambda ≈ H`, the
reformulation is empty and collapses to the stock model.

It is worse than a coincidence: the pairing is an algebraic identity of de Sitter
thermodynamics carrying no maintenance content whatsoever. Analytically,

```
   T_dS * S_horizon / V  =  [hbar H/(2 pi kB)] * [kB A/(4 l_P^2)] / V
                         =  hbar H (3/R) / (8 pi l_P^2)          [A/V = 3/R, R = c/H]
                         =  3 H^2 c^2 / (8 pi G)  =  rho_crit c^2      EXACTLY
```

and numerically the script returns `stock/u_crit = 1.0000000000`. The Gibbons–Hawking
relation *defines* this product to be the critical density. Multiplying the horizon's bit
count by the horizon's own temperature is a restatement of the Friedmann equation, not a
Landauer bill. **Nothing about error correction was used to obtain it, so it cannot be
evidence for error correction.**

The pre-registered rate check (§5) confirms this independently and was written down in
advance precisely so this row could not be claimed as a success.

### 2.2 The maintained-fraction point pushes the wrong way

The flow picture must count only *actively maintained* bits, and `N_maint <= N_total`
always. Every λ in the table is therefore a **lower bound**; a maintained fraction `phi`
multiplies it by `1/phi`. The reformulation's own physical discipline makes its arithmetic
strictly worse. The same is true of the obvious rescue — "each decayed bit needs `m`
correction operations" — which is algebraically `N -> mN`, i.e. a demand for exactly the
bits the stock model already failed to find. **The failure is closed under that class of
rescue**, as pre-registered.

### 2.3 CORRECTION TO THE PREDECESSOR STUDY

`DE_LEDGER_MODEL.md`'s K4 table compared Egan & Lineweaver's entropy **totals** — counted
within the observable universe — against an energy computed in the **Hubble volume**. Those
are different volumes.

Cross-check as pre-registered: E&L's `S_CMB = 2.03e89 k` divided by the first-principles CMB
entropy density `s = (4/3) a_rad T³/kB = 1.4793e9 k/m³` implies `V = 1.372e80 m³`, a sphere
of radius **10.37 Gpc** — `+1.10 dex` larger than the Hubble volume `1.083e79 m³`.

**The K4 table therefore credited the model with about 1.1 dex of bits it does not have; the
true stock shortfalls are ~1 dex larger than recorded.** This study is immune because it
works entirely in densities. This correction makes the already-fired K4 worse, not better,
and it is reported whichever way it fell.

Two independent checks that the bit densities used here are sound:
- CMB entropy per photon comes out at **3.60 k**, the textbook value.
- The IGM gas count from **first-principles Sackur–Tetrode** (159 k/baryon at 10⁶ K) agrees
  with the E&L-derived density to **1.29×**. No table was needed for the load-bearing rows.

## 3. L1 — the λ inconsistency (outcome **(e)**)

This is the leg that makes the failure *internal* rather than merely quantitative.

`Core/Maintenance.lean`'s `rent_holds` says an entry is held steady when the payment equals
the decay. In steady state **the maintenance rate is the decay rate**: the λ in the flow
formula and the λ in the ledger `dn/dt = Y psi - lambda n` are the same symbol in the same
equation. It has now been measured twice:

| leg | λ/H₀ | source |
|---|---|---|
| shape (DESI DR2 BAO + CMB-lite) | 1.671, 68 % [1.33, 2.02] | `DE_LEDGER_MODEL.md` §4 |
| magnitude (this study) | 2.8×10⁴ – 2.1×10¹² | §2 |

**Ratio: 1.7×10⁴ to 1.2×10¹² — 4.2 to 12.1 dex. The pre-registered bar was a factor of 10.
KILL.**

The model cannot be made self-consistent. Magnitude and shape cannot both be right, and no
choice of `(N, T)` on the defensible menu closes a gap of four orders of magnitude, let
alone twelve.

## 4. L2 and L3 — the budget and the waste heat (outcome **(f)**)

**These legs use no bit count and no temperature. They cannot be evaded by any choice in
§2.** This makes them the strongest result in the study.

### 4.1 The energy is not there

Integrated over one Hubble time, the flow model's demand is `P/H = 3 rho_DE`, a fixed
multiple of the critical density independent of N, T and λ:

```
    P/H  =  1.5753e-09 J/m^3  =  2.054 x u_crit
```

| available free energy | J/m³ | shortfall |
|---|---:|---:|
| **all matter, dark matter included** (`Omega_m u_crit`) | 2.416e−10 | **6.5×** |
| all baryons (`Omega_b u_crit`) | 3.781e−11 | 41.7× |
| all stellar mass (`Omega_* u_crit`) | 2.071e−12 | 761× |

**Annihilating every particle of matter in the universe, dark matter included, inside one
Hubble time does not pay the bill.** It falls short by a factor of 6.5.

Against the actual power source the model names — star formation — using the already-verified
MD14 eq. 15 normalisation `psi(0) = 0.01496 Msun/yr/Mpc³`:

- hard upper bound (every gram formed burned instantly at full H→He efficiency, 0.7 %):
  `2.019e-32 W/m³` — short of `P` by **5.23 dex**;
- even *100 % rest-mass conversion* at the star-formation rate: `2.885e-30 W/m³` — short by
  **3.08 dex**.

### 4.2 The heat is not where it would have to be

Landauer heat is real heat and must appear in the reservoir that absorbs it.

| reservoir | test | result |
|---|---|---|
| **CMB** | Compton `y ≈ dU/4U` vs COBE/FIRAS `\|y\| < 1.5e-5` | `y = 9.4e3` — **violated by 8.8 dex** |
| warm IGM (10⁶ K) | deposit vs actual thermal energy density | **3.0e8×** too much |
| hot IGM / WHIM (10⁷ K) | same | **3.0e7×** too much |
| de Sitter horizon | entropy dumped vs horizon entropy | ratio 2.054 — order unity, **but by the same identity as §2.1**, not by a maintenance calculation |

The CMB is a blackbody to about one part in 10⁵. The flow model requires it to have absorbed
~3.8×10⁴ times its own energy content. There is no version of this that survives FIRAS.

The only reservoir that balances is the horizon — and it balances because
`3 rho_DE / T_dS = 3 Omega_L x S_horizon`, which is the Gibbons–Hawking identity again.

prediction; it is confirmation that this row is the Friedmann equation in costume. That this
was written into the prereg *before* the number was computed is what makes it a test rather
than a rationalisation.

## 6. The "named clock" requirement, and why the surviving rates fail it

The prereg fixed a deliberately strict standard: a required λ in the `>10³ H₀` regime
survives only if `1/lambda` coincides with **an independent, named physical clock of the
same system whose bits are counted** — not merely with the existence of some astrophysical
timescale nearby.

The required timescales (12 yr, 5 kyr, 53 kyr, 0.46 Myr, 0.51 Myr) do land in the range of
real astrophysical processes. That is not evidence: the space of astrophysical timescales is
dense, and the model predicted none of these. **No candidate clock is derivable from the
rent clause.** `Core/Maintenance.lean` fixes the *form* of the decay, never its rate.

The repo's own empirical work on decay rates cuts the same way and is worth recording. On
the one real substrate where whole-only pattern's decay has actually been measured
(`HABIT_DYNAMICS_RESULTS.md`), unmaintained order-3 pattern does not decay exponentially at
all — it falls off a **cliff**, reaching its floor after 2 kernel iterations, with both
pre-registered decay families rejected at reduced χ² of 859 and 1227. That result is
qualitatively consistent with `lambda >> H` (decay is fast) and flatly inconsistent with the
`lambda ≈ 1.67 H₀` the shape fit needs — but it is a different substrate and cannot be
transported quantitatively. It is reported here as a consistency note in the same direction,
not as evidence.

## 8. The bridge was never forced — recorded so it is not laundered

`P = 3 H rho_Lambda` is an **interpretation, not a derivation** (prereg B1). Covariant
conservation `d(rho)/dt = -3H(1+w)rho` makes `rho` constant at `w = -1` with no source at
all: the negative pressure does the work, and GR has no global energy conservation law that
would forbid it. **General relativity requires no maintenance power whatsoever.** The flow
picture's whole bridge is the assertion that the constancy is *maintained* rather than
automatic — the exact analogue of the stock picture's imported non-dilution assumption A5.

Recorded also (prereg B2): the natural *rate-matched* reading — maintenance power equals the
record's decay power, `P = lambda rho_rec` — returns `rho_rec = N kB T ln2`, which is
**algebraically identical to the stock model**. Of the three available identifications, two
are the killed model and the third is what this study kills.
