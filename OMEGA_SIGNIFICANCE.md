1. **Emergence becomes objective**: a macrostate is real when the dynamics descends
   through it — `vT = T̄v`, approximately under μ, robustly under interventions,
   within a measured timescale. The privileged-coarse-graining problem gets a
   selection criterion.
2. **Objects become maintained dynamical quotients**: fiber partition + population +
   dynamics + transport + maintenance geometry. To exist effectively is to sustain a
   dynamically autonomous equivalence relation.
3. **Reductionism survives; reductionist ontology does not**: supervenience intact,
   exclusive micro-reality refuted — higher levels have law-governed autonomy and
   energy budgets of their own.
4. **Information becomes constitutive without becoming a substance**: constitutive
   the way symmetry is — a constraint on possible organization, not a material.
5. **Life and agency on a physical continuum**: adaptation is learning a repair map;
   control manufactures closure; semantic information is what the repair requires;
   metabolism pays the rent; death is loss of the maintained closure regime. Not a
   theory of consciousness; a testable account of autonomy.
6. **Contextuality and geometry might share an origin** (the curvature theorem's
   stake): context change as parallel transport, path dependence as holonomy —
   quantum contextuality, gauge holonomy, and dynamical non-closure as graded
   invariants of one context-dependent architecture.
