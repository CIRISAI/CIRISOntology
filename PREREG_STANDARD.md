# The prereg standard — a freeze the audit can refuse

*The fix for the pattern Ω-KILL completed: every miss of the campaign season was
knowable at freeze time from the repo's own record. A prereg is now a CHECKED
ARTIFACT: `Audit/prereg_audit.py` refuses a freeze missing any block below, the
same way the axiom audit refuses an admitted proof.*

Every STAKED ARM must carry, in its freeze document:

1. **THEOREM LINE.** The Lean law it instantiates (`witness:` a resolvable name),
   with EVERY hypothesis of that theorem mapped to either a gauge check or a
   substrate fact with provenance. A stake that rides no theorem says
   `witness: none` and carries its own derivation. *(The season's rule: misses
   happened exactly where the informal stake diverged from the formal
   hypotheses — μ dropped, identity-stability implicit, Markov unverifiable.)*
2. **REGISTRY LINE.** `defects:` — the `DEFECTS_REGISTRY.md` ids touching the
   instrument chain, each with the mitigation applied, or the word `none` (the
   audit greps the chain's keywords against the registry and refuses silent
   contact).
3. **TWO-SIDED GAUGE.** A planted-truth run where the arm PASSES and a planted
   run where it FIRES, both logged before unblinding. **An arm that cannot fail
   is refused** — this is what stopped A5/M1 before quota, and it becomes
   mandatory.
4. **NUMERIC EVERYTHING.** Bands numeric at freeze (no qualitative pass-bands
   quantified later in the instrument); floors by procedure named; family-wise
   correction declared with the arm count; error estimators matched to the
   dependence structure (autocorrelated ⇒ block/cluster).
5. **POSABILITY WITNESS.** For each staked quantity: the demonstration that it
   CAN vary under the data's generative structure (the boundary-spread rule,
   generalized) — including outcome-variance counts for prediction targets.
6. **DATA-CLOSABLE EXITS.** Verdicts are functions of readings alone; every
   outcome bucket names its mechanism discriminator; branches pre-committed.
